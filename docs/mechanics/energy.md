# Energy

> **Summary:** Stamina for tools/foraging; restore by eating/coffee; sleep refills; starts low.

## Rules (wiki)
- Spent by chopping, mining, watering, digging, foraging, some spells. 0 energy → collapse.
- Restore: food from the inventory, **coffee** (Cafe Moretti, cheapest per coin), Revitalizing potions, Mountain Onsen (paid, 1×/day).
- Max energy grows via story rewards (Increase Energy spell at the end of Spring).
- Strategy: spend all energy daily; carry food when mining/exploring.
## Verified in-game
- An orange ring on the clock widget may be the energy meter ❓.
