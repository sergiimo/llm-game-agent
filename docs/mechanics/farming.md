# Farming

> **Summary:** Garden beds (4 seeds), water daily from the well, harvest; compost makes soil; bees need blooming flowers.

## Spaces
| Space | Size | How |
|---|---|---|
| Garden (fenced, by the house) | 8×8 | start: 2 beds |
| Vegetable Patch | 11×12 | *Patch Things Up* (Parker): 1500c, 50 Soil, 50 Wood, 5 Iron Ingot |
| Greenhouse | – | *Greenhouse Affection*: 50 Wood, 20 Iron Ingot, 18 Glass (any season) |
| Hay Field | – | *Hay What's Up?*: 2000c, 40 Soil |
## Structures
Garden Bed 10 Wood + 1 Soil + 25c (4 seeds or 1 tree) · Compost Bin 6 Wood + 10c (2 Weeds → Soil in 24 h, + worms) · Bee Hive 10 Wood + 50c (honey/wax near blooming flowers) · Hummingbird Feeder (decor).
## Crops (seed → sell, days) — full table `reference/wiki/Farming.md`
Spring: Green Bean 3→10 4d · Potato 5→15 5d · Carrot 10→20 5d · Cauliflower 15→20 6d · **Wheat 8→48 8d** · Tulip · Snowdrop.
Summer: Corn · Tomato · **Strawberry 20→48 6d** · Sugarcane · Hops · Marigold · Sunflower · Cotton (Thomas).
Fall: Cabbage · Grapes · Fennel · Pumpkin · Soybean · Tiger Lily · Turtlehead. Winter: Onion · Spinach · Sweet Potato · Fennel · Black Tea · Crocus · Primrose.
Trees (125c at Kai's, any season, no watering): Apple, Lemon, Lime, Orange, Pomegranate, Mulberry, Olive.
## Verified in-game
- Interact with an empty bed (Space at the sprout bubble) → planting panel → seed card → **Выбрать**. 1 seed packet fills the bed (4 spots).
- Fill the can: Space at the well (watering-can bubble). Water: Space at the planted bed (potato + drop bubble).
- Gramma supplies the first potato seeds; the can is given during *From Bud To Spud*.
