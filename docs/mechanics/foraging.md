# Foraging

> **Summary:** Respawning forage on Farm/Woods/Beach/Mountain; hoard it (unlimited inventory).

## Rules (wiki)
- Farm: mushroom, toadstool, thistle, weed (lower grass), poppy (summer/fall). Woods: moon flower (night), mandrake, peanut, mushrooms. Beach: coconut, seaweed, shells, pearls, bananas. Mountain: herbs, nuts, pinecones.
- Respawn every few days; keep 2+ of everything; don't sell what only respawns.
- Per-area tables: `reference/wiki/Farm.md`, `Woods.md`, `Beach.md`, `Mountain.md`.
