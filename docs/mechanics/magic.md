# Magic

> **Summary:** Separate magic meter for wand spells; crafting of potions/incantations in the Basement.

## Rules (wiki)
- Restore magic: alcoholic drinks (cost a little energy, safe at 0 energy), Restore Magic potions.
- Basement (trapdoor under the rug, unlocked in Spring coven quests): Cauldron (potions), Desk (incantations, ink), Distillery (essences), Mortar & Pestle (powders, dyes, flour), Crystal Ball (favourite gifts), Scrying Bowl (find things).
- Weather/time spells: Summon Rain, Sunny Day, Summon Wind/Storm/Night, Speed/Freeze/Reverse Time.
- Recipes: [../world/items/potions-and-incantations.md](../world/items/potions-and-incantations.md).
- Season changes are coven rituals (see [seasons-and-weather.md](seasons-and-weather.md)).
