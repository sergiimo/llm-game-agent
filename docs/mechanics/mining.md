# Mining

> **Summary:** 20-level mine next to the farm (after Parker repairs it); keys from ore nodes; pickaxe upgrades matter most.

## Rules (wiki)
- Unlock: *Don't Take Mines For Granite* (100c, 10 Wood, 5 Stone). Levels: iron (1+), copper (5+), silver (10+), gold (15+); gems from 9; level 14 needs the Deep Mine key (coven).
- Progress resets when you leave, so bring food/coffee. Chests (Lockpick incantation) on 4, 9, 14, 16–20.
- Upgrade the pickaxe early (Copper: 3 Copper Ingot + 5 Wood + 250c …). Smelting at Natalia's Blacksmith.
- Table: `reference/wiki/Mine.md`.
