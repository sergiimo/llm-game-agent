# Screen: cutscene

> **Summary:** Scripted camera sequence, voiced, often without subtitles.

## Recognise
Mode `cutscene`: black letterbox bars at the top (0–~125 px) and bottom (~950–1080 px), no HUD.

## Elements
- Camera pans; subtitles may appear (then the mode may read `dialogue`).

## Actions
- Space advances to the next shot (`./wf advance`).

## Exit
Automatic; returns to gameplay.
