# Screen: planting-panel

> **Summary:** Seed picker opened by interacting with an empty garden bed.

## Recognise
Camera zooms on the bed; left panel titled **Инвентарь** with tab **Семена**.

## Elements
- Seed cards with counts (e.g. Семена картошки ×1). Clicking a card shows details (grow days, season) and **Выбрать**.
- With no seeds: "Семена можно купить в универмаге Лины." (buy seeds at Lina's store).

## Actions
- Click card (≈(110,540) for the first) → `./wf click-text Выбрать`.

## Exit
Esc.
