# Screen: shop

> **Summary:** Space at the shopkeeper's bubble → menu with **speech + coin** icons → coin = shop window
> (buy/sell tabs) → click an item card → detail popup with **Buy (N)** focused → Space buys → Esc ×2 to leave.
> ✅ Verified at Lina's Dahl General Store (Wed d3).

## Recognise
Overlay panel titled with the shop name, e.g. **"Dahl family general store (EN wording ❓)"** (Dahl family general store); item cards with
price tags; coins top-right; an "out of season" section **"Out of season..."**; two tabs at the left edge:
**basket = buy**, **sack = sell**.

## Elements
- Item card → detail popup: name, description, how many are in your bag, price, pink button **"Buy (1)"** (Buy (1),
  focused), a **▶** arrow next to it (probably raises the quantity ❓).
- Stock depends on shop level (stars ❓ not seen yet); prices in `reference/wiki/<Shop>.md` (Rice 8c at Lina's).

## Actions
1. Stand at the counter until the shopkeeper's bubble shows → Space → interaction menu with 2 icons: speech (talk),
   **coin (trade)**. Select the coin (it may already be highlighted; `key:right` then Space worked) → Space.
2. Click the item card (e.g. rice at ≈ (220,420)) → the detail popup opens → **Space = buy 1** (no extra confirmation).
   Money drops immediately; a toast with the item name appears.
3. Selling: the sack tab (❓ not yet used).

## Exit
Esc → the shopkeeper's menu → Esc → gameplay.
