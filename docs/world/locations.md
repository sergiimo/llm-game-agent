## Beach
The Beach consists of two upper and two lower areas. The upper left and right areas are separated by a wooden bridge, with each featuring a slope down to the lower area on its same side. There is no bridge connecting the lower left and right areas directly, instead requiring that you travel up and across to get to one from the other.
**Connections:** Farm (upper left; exits to the Farm's southwest); Town Center (upper right; exits next to Kim's Local Butcher and Deli & lower right; exits next to The Bar)
**Forage:** Banana (Lower left and right areas, Year-round); Blueberry (Three bushes surrounding the lighthouse (upper right area), Summer, Spring, and Fall); Coconut (Lower left and right areas, Year-round); Conch Shell (Lower left and right areas, Year-round); Pearl (Lower left and right areas, Year-round); Seaweed (Lower left and right areas, Year-round); Seashell (Lower left and right areas, Year-round); Witch Weed (Below Thomas's farm, Spring and Summer); Heather (Below Thomas’s farm, Fall and Winter)
**Businesses:** The Hayseed Homestead; Thomas's Farm
**Residents:** Thomas Lightfoot, Kim Izumi, Damon Mthembu-Haas, Kai Hoapili, Cameron Conner, Amira Syed, Marty Emerson

## Woods
The Woods is a wooded area to the far east of Fairhaven where many resources can be foraged, including respawning trees for collecting wood. There are opportunities for fishing, and its upper left area serves as the meeting place for the Fairhaven Coven!
**Connections:** Town Center (lower left; exits next to Nailed It!); The Gloaming (upper left, once unlocked)
**Forage:** Forget-Me-Not (Below the lower path, Fall and Winter); Mandrake (Above and below the lower path, Year-round); Moon Flower (Three regrowing plants above the area entrance and below the Coven meetingplace, Spring, Summer, Fall); Mushroom (Along the paths, Year-round); Peanut (Above and below the lower path, Year-round); Thistle (Along the paths, Year-round); Toadstool (Along the paths, Year-round); Walnut (Above and below the lower path, Fall and Winter)
**Residents:** Shelby Demeter

## Mountain
The Mountain area is initially inaccessible until the connecting bridge is upgraded during the Summer season. It hosts a variety of forgeable goods, including respawning trees that can be chopped for wood. It also has the unique feature of periodically-spawning dig spots that can reveal a small amount of coins or a new Cooking recipe!
**Connections:** Farm (bottom left; exits to northwest)
**Forage:** Almond (Either side of the river towards the bottom of the screen, Summer and Fall); Blackberry (Several bushes along the edges of the lower area and along the path up to top of mountain, Summer, Fall, and Winter); Coriander (Either side of the river towards the bottom of the screen, Fall); Mint (Either side of the river towards the bottom of the screen, Summer and Fall); Nigella (Either side of the river towards the bottom of the screen, Summer and Fall); Parsley (Either side of the river towards the bottom of the screen, Winter); Pecan (Either side of the river towards the bottom of the screen, Fall and Winter); Pinecone (Either side of the river towards the bottom of the screen, Fall and Winter); Pistachio (Either side of the river towards the bottom of the screen, Fall and Winter)
