# A Change of Recipe

A Change of Recipe is a quest given by Angus Seton in Year 3. It requires the player to deliver a succession of baking items to Angus at the Bakery for his cookbook project.

## Introduction
### Dialog
Angus: 

Tara: Hang on, I'm not sure I should be keeping secrets between you two.

Angus: Don't worry, this is a good secret. Just one I don't want Francis to get too excited over yet.

Tara: Well... shoot, I guess.

Angus: The Bay-King Publishing Co contacted me last week. They want to publish a book of my recipes!

Tara: Angus, that's amazing.

Angus: Obviously, I said yes, but I'm going to need help.

Angus: I want to knock their socks off. If I'm going to do that, I'll need the best ingredients to help perfect my recipes.

Angus: Could you help me source some things? Your farm produces some of the best of food I've ever tasted.

Tara: Of course I'll help! I'm so excited to see what recipes you come up with!
