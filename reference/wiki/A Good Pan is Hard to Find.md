# A Good Pan is Hard to Find

A Good Pan is Hard to Find is a quest given by Natalia Kuznetsova in the epilogue. It requires the player to deliver 10x Iron Ingot to her so she can make a set of cast iron pans for Sophia to apologize for accusing her of witchcraft in the Fall. The reward for completion is 190 coins.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Natalia: Heya, Tara. Got a minute?
* Tara: Now that Fairhaven is safe, I’ve got plenty of minutes. What’s going on?
* Natalia: I’ve been thinkin’, and… I still feel real bad about accusing Sophia of being a witch. Not that bein’ a witch is anything terrible!
* Tara: I know, Natalia. It’s fine.
* Natalia: Course, the wraith was part of it. But the wraith picked me. What can I do to make Sophia forgive me?
* Tara: You can’t make her forgive you. But you could create something heartfelt. That’s a great first step.
* Natalia: Ooh! Last Summer, I did hear her say that she missed her best cast iron pan. It cracked. I could make her a new one? Or better yet, a whole set!
* Tara: That’s the spirit!
* Natalia: I’m low on supplies. The next time you’re in the mine, do you think you could pick me up a few things?
* Tara: Sure thing.
* Natalia: Thanks a million, Tara. You always give the best advice.

Natalia has asked for some iron ingots so she can make an apology gift for Sophia. 

* Natalia: I can’t wait to get started on that cast iron pan!
* Tara: Your wait is officially over.
* Natalia: Ooh, this is gorgeous stuff. Thanks, Tara! It won’t take long to shape this into a set of pans. But then… I gotta give it to Sophia…
* Tara: Hey, it’s okay. Just be sincere. Sophia appreciates sincerity.
* Natalia: You’re right. Wish me luck!
* [Reward: 190 coins]

Speaking to Natalia the next day:

* Tara: So… how'd it go with Sophia? 
* Natalia: Oh, Tara, she loved the pans! You were right! Knowin' that Sophia forgives me - it's a big weight off my shoulders. 
* Tara: And now you don't have to avoid the diner. No more missing Sophia's pies of the week! 

Speaking to Sophia the next day:

* Sophia: Natalia did the sweetest thing this morning. She brought me a set of cast iron pans she'd made! And she apologized for what she said last Fall. 
* Tara: How shocking! I had no idea that would happen! 
* Sophia: She hadn't come into the diner for months. And to be honest, I don't know if I'd have let her. 
* Tara: Natalia doesn't always know what to say. 
* Sophia: This time, she said the exact right words. Gosh, I feel so much better!
