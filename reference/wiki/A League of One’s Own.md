A League of One’s Own is a quest given by Cameron Conner in the epilogue. It requires the player to deliver 10x Paper and 2x Black Ink to him so he can compose a new set of sermons to help undo the damage his previous sermons helped cause. The reward for completion is 170 coins.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: Am I interrupting something?
* Cameron: Oh. Hi, Tara. I’m trying to work on new sermon ideas. But nothing seems right. I never thought I would say this, but I’m considering leaving the League of the Conscious Mind. I’ve followed Messenger Corne’s teachings for so long, but they led me astray. I was to blame for suggesting that some of our neighbors might be being Malcontents. And I let Otto take the trials a bit too far.
