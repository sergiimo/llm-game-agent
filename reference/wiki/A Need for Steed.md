# A Need for Steed

[Quest]
  title: Need for Steed
  type: To-Do Quest
  start: Ravenwood Hollow
  item_needs: Mushroom Leather Saddle
  rewards: Pet Treat (Recipe); 2x Pet Treats

## Description
## Dialogue
### Quest Intro
Peri: Oh hello, Witchling. I was just asking Aryel if he has found any fairy steeds lately.

Tara: A fairy steed?

Peri: I read abut them in one of Westley's books. And I thought: yes. Why should witches be the only ones with mounts?

Tara: But don't you have...

Aryel: Don't.

Tara: Wings?

---

Tara: What was that about?

Aryel: Her wing's don't work. I dont' know if they ever did, but she hasn't flown for many centuries.

Aryel: I've tried asking but she doesn't want to talk about it.

Tara: Oh. Well now I feel terrible.

Aryel: Ordinarily, I do not approve of animals being put into service. But I trust Peri to treat it with respect and care.

Aryel: I will find her this fairy steed she desires. But she will need a saddle when it arrives.

Aryel: Can you fashion one for me?

---

Tara: This... can't be right. Maybe Aryel forgot to convert something when he made the pattern...

Tara: Like the Fae version of the metric system?

### Quest Completion
Tara:I made the saddle according to your pattern, Aryel, but I have to say it...

Aryel: Oh! What excellent timing! The fairy steed just arrived this morning! Let me go get her.

Peri: What's this? Aryel has my mount?

Aryel: Yes! Presenting: Ginger, the fairy steed!

Peri: This is... and odd prank. Even for you, Aryel.

Aryel: What? You're not delighted?

Peri: That is... a dog.

Tara: So that's why the saddle is so tiny!

Aryel: I asked Westley and he confirmed that the fairy steed mentioned in his book is indeed a corgi.

Aryel: You see... they were called fairy steeds because the pattern on their fur looks like a little saddle! Isn't that marvelous?

Peri: Did it occur to you that it is far too small for me to ride?

Aryel: Well, yes, but I thought perhaps you planned to use a shrinking potion or...

Peri: Aryel! I . Am Not. A Pixie!

Tara: Awww... You're the cutest fairy steed ever!

Aryel: Well, Peri doesn't want her, maybe you would like to adopt her? She needs a good home. I'll care for her in the meantime.

Aryel: Dogs are very loyal and useful companions. If you appreciate them.
