# A New Sheriff in Town

[Quest]

A New Sheriff in Town is a quest given by Gloria Miranda in the epilogue. It requires the player to deliver 3x Cloth to Kai so he can make some new paintings for the Town Hall. The reward for completion is 285 coins.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: Hi there… Mayor Miranda!
* Gloria: It’s so strange to be called that! Antonio was Mayor Miranda, not me!
* Tara: Does he know that you’re back?
* Gloria: We spoke on the phone – he still can’t believe it, to say the least. And we don’t know when he’ll be able to get his visa sorted and come back home. I would like to update the art in here… some inclusive pieces that are more reflective of our diverse community. And I never knew it, but Kai is a very talented painter! Could you source some cloth for Kai to make his canvases? He was able to get pigments for his paints once the ferry started up again, but he still needs something to paint on!
* Tara: Sure, I would be glad to help you brighten this place up!
* Gloria: Thanks, Tara.

Deliver cloth to Kai so he can get to work on his artistic vision. 

* Tara: I’ve got the cloth for your canvases.
* Kai: Thanks, Tara! I’m going to make some choice paintings for Mayor Miranda! So like, the mayor’s in the coven too, huh? That’s sick! We’re probably like the only island around who has a witch for a mayor.

Wait for Kai to create the artwork.

* Gloria: Hey Tara, what do you think? This place finally has some personality!
* Tara: Wow. Is that…
* Gloria: It’s our history. The Dahl sisters… Thomas’s family…
* Tara: And… Gramma?
* Gloria: Yeah. I guess she and Kai had a greater friendship than I realized. He was always cutting past her place on the way to the beach. Now she’ll always be remembered. It’s been a big upheaval, suddenly returning to my life on the island, like it was before, but also nothing like it was before. The kids are being champs, but I’m still trying to get my bearings. Thanks for all your help with this. I feel more at home now.
* [Reward: 285 coins]
