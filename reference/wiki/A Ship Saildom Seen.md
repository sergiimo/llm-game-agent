# A Ship Saildom Seen

Steeped in Mystery is a series of quests given by the Farseer that serves as an introduction to  Ravenwood Hollow. The quest starts automatically after the Summer Festival. It requires you to locate the dilapidated boat at the lake by your farm, repair the boat using 15x Wood, 5x Iron Ingot and 2x Silver Ingot, and craft the Fealty to the Fair Folk Spell using 1x Black Ink, 1x Paper, and 3x Amethyst. 

After the questline is completed, you can use your boat to travel instantaneously between the lake by your farm and Ravenwood Hollow.
