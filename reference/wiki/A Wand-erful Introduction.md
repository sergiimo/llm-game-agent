# A Wand-erful Introduction

A Wand-erful Introduction is a quest given by the High Priestess in the Winter. It requires the player to create 1x Create Wand incantation to help initiate Francis into the coven. The reward for completion is 5x Scrying Potion. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: Francis, you did so well at the trial!
* Initiate Francis: Yes, well I’m still not sure what I’m supposed to do with this cursed amulet I made…
* Adept Amira: Give it here. I’ll take care of it.
* Initiate Francis: Just asking… why didn’t everyone in town hall freak out when that thing came flying out of Angus?
* High Priestess Lina: Because only witches can see it.
* Initiate Francis: Lo sabia! I was afraid you would say that. You really think I’m… a witch?
* High Priestess Lina: Absolutely. You have great potential, Francis. But first, you’ll need a wand.
* Initiate Francis: Wait, seriously?
* High Priestess Lina: And then we will initiate you into the coven. Speak with the Novice, she was the last to join us.
* Initiate Francis: I feel so strange about all of this. I hated lying to Angus about where I was going tonight. But he would hate me if he knew... This initiation - will it hurt? Am I going to have to sacrifice animals like my brother did when he became a Santeria priest?
* Tara: No, you just need to read a special incantation in front of the ancient tree in the woods... My gramma gave it to me when it was my turn and I'll write one for you.

Make a create wand incantation to help initiate Francis. 

Give the create wand incantation to Francis at the coven circle.

* Tara: Here’s the incantation, Francis. Remember to only read it in front of the ancient tree, and when no one from town is looking.
* Initiate Francis: Oh believe me, I don’t want anyone to see me having a conversation with a tree, witch trials or not.
* High Priestess Lina: You will also need to bring an initiation incantation. Speak to the Farseer to learn more.

Wait until tomorrow night to attend Francis’ Initiation.

* Initiate Francis: I have my wand and the incantation, Adept.
* Adept Amira: Then you are ready to be initiated. You will need a sponsor. Usually, it is someone in the initiate’s family but…
* Initiate Francis: Tara. Will you sponsor me?
* Tara: Of course, Francis. I would be honored.
* Initiate Francis: Thanks.
* High Priestess Lina: Initiate Francis, take your place in the wheel. Novice, take your place beside him. Initiate, you seek new knowledge, but with it comes a sacred duty. Who brings forth this initiate to join our coven?
* Novice Tara: Um, I do!
* High Priestess Lina: As the great wheel turns, so must we take our part, here where our ancestors stood before us, and where others will follow when we are gone.
* Novice Tara: Enter our circle, for this is the hour, a Fairhaven witch comes into his power!
* Initiate Francis: Whoa! Uh oh. Is one of those things going to escape again?
* High Priestess Lina: No, but we must maintain our offerings to keep the Wellspring balanced. Welcome, Initiate.
* [Reward: 5 Scrying potion]
