# About Time

[Quest]
  title: About Time
  type: To-Do Quests
  start: Shelby
  level: Epilogue
  previous: ?
  next: ?

About Time is a quest given by Shelby in the epilogue. It requires the player to repair his old pocket watch. After the watch is repaired, Shelby explains that it can be used to visit the past, and Tara uses it to see Gramma one last time and tell her about what she's accomplished. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro

* Shelby: Well, thought you might be able to help me with somethin’. Got this old pocket watch, handed down from my pa. Ticker stopped workin’, but ya got a metal working table, dontcha?
* Tara: I do, but wouldn’t you rather give it to Francis? He’s the jewelry expert!

Fix up an old, broken watch for the Farseer. 

* Tara: Oh, hey Shelby, I was just about to… You already knew, didn’t you.

* Shelby: You keep it. It’s special, like I said. Ya know the time-rewind spell I taught ya? Well, it works like that, only instead of just fixin’ a cake you can rewind the whole room you’re in. I put a charm on it, mind. The people in the past won’t be able to see you, and you won’t be able to change what you see. Interfering in the past is too dangerous, even for the most skilled witch.
* Tara: So it’s more like a visit to the past?
* Shelby: That’s right. It’s an old heirloom, though. Probably only has one charge in it left, enough to get you there and back. Might be safest to try it in yer house.
