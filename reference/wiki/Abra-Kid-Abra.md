# Abra-Kid-Abra

[Quest]

Abra-Kid-Abra is a quest given by the High Priestess in the epilogue. It requires the player to deliver 5x Chocolate Cupcake to her so the coven can throw a party for the future witches of Fairhaven. The reward for completion is 5x Levitation Potion.
