# Adder Stone

[Crafting Ingredient]
  itemName: Adder Stone
  description: A magical, lustrous stone found in the Gloaming. Use it to turn the seasons from Winter to Spring.
  source: The Gloaming (Level 2)
  other: ==Information==
The Adder Stone can be mined from a rock to the East in the second level of the Gloaming. After Year 1, you must cast a Reverse Time Spell at the spot to bring back the rock and mine it again.
