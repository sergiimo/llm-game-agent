# Alpaca

## Unlock
Before Tara is able to raise alpaca she must help Marty Emerson provide enough feed to his livestock transportation shipments via the Menagerie Mixup quest. Tara must also have the Upgraded Greenhouse.

## Raising Alpaca
Alpaca can be purchased from Marty at The Hayseed Homestead. They will providing Alpaca Wool after a few days. Though baby alpaca are identical, adult alpaca may be varied!

Alpaca require one serving of Hay per day and can be pet to improve Tara's relationship with them. Alpaca who have not yet been fed will have an exclamation mark over their head (similar to the appearance of villagers with quests available). After # days of not being fed, an alpaca will fall ill and need to be cured using a Cure Animal Sickness Potion (unlocked through a questline with Aryel). Once a Hay Field is built, alpaca will feed themselves each day.
