Amira Syed (38) is the town doctor. She's confident, stylish, and outgoing... maybe the most magnetic person to ever walk this island.; ; Amira's taken quite an interest in how you're finding Fairhaven. While she loves treating patients, she also needs peace and quiet, which she finds in the woods. Amira also came here from the city. 

She has been known to use work to avoid getting close to people, but she still wears a necklace that her ex gave her.

## About
Amira is known, from the very beginning, for her flirty personality! She lived in Tehran for a year and has a taste for Persian cuisine.

Though Kim is a romanceable character, they don’t really show romantic interest in Tara and will make a couple subtle jealous comments in regards to Amira’s interest in Tara. While Tara is dating Amira, Kim makes reference to seeing “Amira and Giva going by up the mountain wearing snow shoes together but don’t worry it’s not like she’ll ditch you for the cute new girl that moves to town”. Amira doesn’t show any signs of disloyalty when in a relationship with Tara. Before Tara arrived, Amira used to flirt with Kim but it doesn’t seem like Amira was as into Kim as Kim was into Amira. As soon as she meets Tara she’s smitten. If Tara is not married to Kim or Amira by the end of Winter Year 1, they will become a couple and will not longer be romancable.
