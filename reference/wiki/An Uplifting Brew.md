# An Uplifting Brew

[Quest]
  title: An Uplifting Brew
  type: To-Do Quests
  start: the Journeyman
  end: the Journeyman
  prerequisites: 1x Levitation Potion
  previous: ?
  next: ?

An Uplifting Brew is a quest given by the Journeyman to unlock his identity. It requires you to brew 1x Levitation Potion and deliver it to him during the day. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Journeyman: Hey, new witch! Is it my turn to unmask? This thing is getting pretty itchy.
* Tara: Hey… you know, if you want to just take it off I won’t tell anyone…
* Journeyman: Nice try! But you gotta work for it. I need you to brew me a bottle of something so good, it’ll make you float right off the ground. Literally. It’s a levitation potion!
* Tara: So witches do fly!
* Journeyman: Yeah, but we need the right juice to do it. Don’t go drinking it just yet. It’s for your broom!
* Tara: You can’t be serious. Witches don’t actually fly on brooms.
* Journeyman: Of course we do! But not like, regular brooms. That would be ridiculous!
* Tara: Yes, that would be ridiculous.
* Journeyman: Here’s the recipe. You’ll need to use your distillery as well as your cauldron to get it just right.
* [Receive levitation potion recipe]

Quest completion:

* Tara: Here you go… Damon.
* Damon: That looks perfect! You really do take after Hazel! If you make any more, I’ll buy it to add to drinks. A splash of levitation potion makes a lekker good cocktail!
* Tara: But… isn’t that dangerous?
* Damon: A few sips of the stuff won’t make gravity let up. It just makes you feel light on your feet.
