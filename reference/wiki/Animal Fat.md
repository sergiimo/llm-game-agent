# Animal Fat

[Crafting Ingredient]
  itemName: Animal Fat
  source: Kim's Local Butcher and Deli
  description: Animal byproduct. Cook with it or make tallow candles using the candlemaker.
  other: ==Other information==
Animal fat can be obtained by selling a cow or a sheep to Kim's Local Butcher and Deli. The reward for selling a cow includes 2 Animal Fat, while the reward for a sheep includes 3.

; 
This ingredient can be used in a handful of recipes that accept either Animal Fat or Olive Oil as their fat.
