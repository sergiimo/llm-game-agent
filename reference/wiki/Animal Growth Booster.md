# Animal Growth Booster

[Potions Incantations]
  itemName: Animal Growth Booster
  recipe: x1 Flower Paper; x1 Black Ink; x1 Mistletoe; x1 Rainbow Egg
  magicCost: 5 bars
  workstation: Desk
  useWord: Effect
  use: Turns a baby animal into an adult animal, or speeds up the gestation period of a pregnant animal.
  description: "Speeds up the growth stages for animals."
  other: This incantation was not originally a part of the game, and was added as a part of the Fabulous Farming update that was released on April 27th, 2023. This update included new animals, the opportunity for animals to get pregnant/produce babies, a larger barn and coop, and magical animals.
