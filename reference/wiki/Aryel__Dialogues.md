# Aryel/Dialogues

CharacterNav
This page lists the dialogue lines for Aryel.

| Summer |
| Tara: Where are you from, Aryel? |
Aryel: It is a place you humans call Brazil. But when I came into being, the humans called it Pindoretama.; Tara: That's a long way from here.; Aryel: One day, I may tell you the story of how I came to Ravenwood Hollow.
| Tara: Woah.; Aryel: You may stare. I have wondrous ankles. This is my watchpost. I keep a lookout for wild animals who may be wounded or otherwise in danger. Come visit me if you ever want to learn how to commune with all of nature's creatures! |
| Tara: I'll be honest. I've heard of werewolves before. Fairies and goblins, too. But not curupiras. Are curupiras a kind of goblin or something?; Aryel: That's silly! No! We are curupiras, nothing more, nothing less. Though we survive on the same magic as our goblin friends. |
| Tara: Maybe this is a rude question to ask, but… why do your feet point backwards?; Aryel: They allow for the best kinds of trickery! When I lived in Pindoretama, I led more hunters away from the animals of the forest than any other curupira. Nothing confuses a human more than a set of tracks going the wrong direction! |
| Tara: So… what is this little guy? Some sort of… butterfly… caterpillar?; Aryel: Ha! No, it's a spinner. They're responsible for all the webs in the… where they're from.; Tara: Does he have a name?; Aryel: I'm sure she does in her tongue, but I couldn't presume to give her another. |
| Aryel: Humans have terrible senses of humor. I asked a flock of sparrows who had recently eaten to fly over the leader in your town. But he didn't even laugh when the birds let loose!; Tara: Well, I think that's funny. |
| Tara: Do you ever visit our town? You know, at night, maybe?; Aryel: Yes, but only when a great need arises and I must save a creature. Otherwise, the crabs and chipmunks are my eyes and ears. |
| Aryel: Can you believe this? There is a shopkeeper in your town whose sole job is to cut up and sell the flesh of animals. Who would take that job willingly? I don't understand. |
| Aryel: How many years do you have, Wyldling?; Tara: Me? I'm 28. How old are you?; Aryel: Five!; Tara: You're five?; Aryel: Five centuries! |
| Aryel: I like a good chuckle more than anything. But don't be deceived: if anyone threatens the woods, I will become so very fearsome-! |
| Aryel: I love tending to dragonlings. They hiss in my face, so I hiss right back! Sssss! We have a grand time. |
| Aryel: Westley once asked me why I am so very rascally. So I asked him why he's so hairy. You should have seen his face! Hee!; Tara: Aryel! That was rude.; Aryel: What?! I can no more change my personality than I can turn my feet around. |

| Fall |
| Aryel: I hear you are choosing a new leader in your town? Strange. Peri has never given us much of a choice. Although I can't imagine who would want to run against her! Not even me, and I can outrun anyone! |
| Aryel: My mole in the mine has been telling me great things about you.; Tara: You have someone spying on me? Who, Zephyr?; Aryel: No, he's an actual mole! He didn't like the last mining operation, you see. They were always setting off explosives and disrupting his habitat! But, well, they're gone now. And my mole tells me you're much nicer! |
| Aryel: That ranch looks nicer than it did before I… it collapsed. Spontaneously. It was poorly constructed. |
| Aryel: You might want to have Zephyr teach you how to teleport. Just in case the supports in the mine give out again. Oh wait, I forgot you're human. Nevermind! |
| Aryel: Peri gets annoyed when I leave Ravenwood Hollow to check on my little woodland friends. But they're all so busy preparing for Winter! I must go help them however I can. |
| Aryel: Humans do not know how to hibernate. That is your problem. Always busy, busy, and in each other's business! |
| Aryel: I asked Zephyr to help make a whirligig to help clear the last snares and traps that hunters left in the mountain pass. She said she was busy, but I just took some chocolate and whipped up a batch of brigadeiros, and she suddenly had all the time in the world! |
| Aryel: You may think that Peri is hard to win over, and well… That's only because she is. I mean, I freed her from a warlock, and she still gives me grief all the time!; Tara: Wait, you did what?; Aryel: Oh, it was many centuries ago, when she first came here. It's not something we speak about. A warlock had her trapped in servitude. I screamed and set her free. Any fae would have done the same for another. |
| Aryel: Peri is always accusing me of creating mischief around here. But if you ask me, Westley is the one to keep an eye on. I don't trust anyone with one foot in the human and one foot in the fae worlds. Both of my feet are planted firmly on this side of the veil, as you can see! |
| Aryel: Peri says that the coven let a wraith escape from the Gloaming. That is serious. I have been here many centuries, and have not seen that happen for some time.; Tara: Um, what happened last time?; Aryel: It is better that I not say. |
| Aryel: I have known Zephyr's ancestors for many generations. Goblins are a good sort. They know not to disturb the bats and other little creatures in the mine caves. |
| Aryel: I knew your great great Aunt Vivian. She used to come by Peri's apothecary all the time, they were always brewing up new potions and tinctures. But then, she stopped coming as often. Curious. |
| Aryel: It would be wise to focus all your energies on capturing the wraith. Humans do terrible things to animals. But when they turn on each other, it can be just as bad. |
| Aryel: I can't talk now, I must keep my eye out for wildlife in need of my assistance! |
| Aryel: Well, you've averted one crisis. But be wary. I can always sniff out a hunter… and that mayor of yours is on the hunt. |
| Aryel: Sometimes, my Wyldling, I hear birds singing so cheerily that I want to throw myself on the ground and weep. It's so pretty! It never gets old! |

| Winter |
| Aryel: Westley told me about that woman who cooks delicious food. Sophia. Why would your Otto say she's a witch? The scent of magic on a human is simple enough to sniff out.; Tara: Humans don't have a nose like yours, Aryel. This would all be easier if we could just smell magic. |
| Aryel: Westley has been doing some excellent howling lately. But it will never be better than mine! |
| Aryel: The spiders have told me that someone who shouldn't be out walks through the woods some nights. Now, who could that be? |
| Tara: Does Ravenwood Hollow have any Winter celebrations?; Aryel: Look around you. Does this place look different than it did in Fall or Summer?; Tara: Uh… no?; Aryel: We have no seasons here. Which means I never have to worry about finding a shirt! |
| Aryel: Lately I have had the urge to do very wicked pranks. But I have to remind myself. It's the Wellspring becoming unbalanced. |
| Aryel: Westley says that most of the humans are worried about repairing the ferry. But why do you need the ferry? It churns up the waters near the shore, and the people who ride it throw trash in the water… Best to leave it broken. |
| Aryel: Then again, perhaps you humans should repair the ferry. Zephyr won't stop talking about her plans to fix it! She's going to explode my brain! |
| Aryel: Westley has been pacing back and forth like an animal, even when he's in his human form. I think he is - what is the expression? - "stir crazy." |
| Tara: Aryel, are you able to stop yourself when you want to do some, you know… inadvisable pranking?; Aryel: I did not make it five centuries without learning to control myself. When it is Winter in the mortal world, I cross over and put my ear against the ground. And I grow calm listening to the many slow heartbeats of the creatures who hibernate. |
| Aryel: Please turn the seasons soon, Wyldling. This Winter has been harsher than a tiger's teeth. I don't know how much all the creatures can handle it. |
| Aryel: So a mortal man has accused another of being a witch. But he does not know that his own husband is a witch. Every day I am more thankful that I am fae, not human. |
| Aryel: [Snowy owl noises] Oh! Tara! You caught me practicing my snowy owl vocabulary.; Tara: What were you saying just now?; Aryel: Hello, friend! Want to dive-bomb the rancher every time he comes outside? |
| Aryel: Last night, I crept into the woods and helped a mother lynx teach her kits how to hunt. Even the kits are fast little creatures. But they don't realize just how mighty they are. They must be shown. |
| Aryel: So you've added a new witch to your numbers! You're not the smallest hatchling in the nest now. Your pin feathers have come in!; Tara: Thanks. I think. |
| Aryel: Humans have hearts of stone.; Tara: Not all of us.; Aryel: Maybe not. But - too many of you. |

| Epilogue |
| Aryel: The caterpillars have told me that Spring is back. I wasn't sure if you witches would be able to manage the turning of the seasons this time!; Tara: Thanks for the vote of confidence…; Aryel: Oh, don't cut your eyes at me. You're some of the most powerful witches I've ever known. |
| Aryel: The woodpeckers have returned to mate. There are more kinds of woodpeckers than I can count on my fingers and toes! Even a little island like Fairhaven contains worlds and worlds of wildlife. |
| Aryel: A pack of tanukis came through Ravenwood Hollow the other day. They have the cleverest little eyes and hands! Why are humans the dominant species, mmm? Why can't we live alongside badger dogs instead? |
| Aryel: I see Peri smiling more these days. Helping your town has made her feel like she has no debt to repay, I think. And… I am glad. We older fae don't have "best friends" the way you speak of it, but if I did have one… she would be it. |
| Aryel: Zephyr says that now Spring has arrived in the human world, it is time for "romance to bloom." I don't understand! Why would you ever want to smush your lips against-; Tara: Ugh, Aryel, don't put it like that. |
| Aryel: This language has so many words for the concept of playing tricks. Pranks! Shenanigans! Hijinks!; Tara: Well, with spirits like you around…; Aryel: Waggery! Naughtiness! Tomfoolery! Wait, is Tom the one fooling, or is he the one being fooled? Hmmmm. |
