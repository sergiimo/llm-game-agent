# Barn

Tocright
The Barn is a space where Tara can raise cows for milk and sheep for wool. It is unlocked after restoring the chicken coop and completing the upgrade quest titled A Barn in Stable Condition. The upgrade costs 120 wood, 60 Iron Ingots, and 1800 coins and takes 2 days to complete.

Cows and sheep both require one serving of hay per day until Tara builds the Hay Field, which allows the animals to graze. Animals who have not yet been fed will have an exclamation mark over their head (similar to the appearance of villagers with quests available). After 1 day of not being fed, an animal will fall ill and need to be cured using a Cure Animal Sickness Potion (unlocked through a questline with Aryel.)

Tara can also pet them to improve their relationship with them. Having a good relationship with your animals does not influence the amount or quality of products produced; rather, it increases the price for which you can sell them to Kim or Marty. (Note: Kim will not buy baby animals)

## Cows
Calves can be purchased from Marty for 1500 coins and mature to adulthood in one week. Each adult cow produces at least one milk per day.

## Sheep
Sheep can be purchased from Marty for 1200 coins and mature to adulthood in 8 days. Each adult sheep produces at least one wool every 3 days.
