# Basement

## Basement
The basement is a space in Tara's farmhouse where she can build a variety of crafting stations to create magical items. This workspace is critical to Tara's progression through the ranks of The Fairhaven Coven as many of its quests require items crafted here.

### Cauldron
A witch's trusty vessel for brewing potions. Build more cauldrons to keep a steady supply of potions at your fingertips! (Note: The basement comes equipped with one cauldron and more cannot be built until the ability is unlocked later in the game)

### Desk
Your incantation station. Use it to make ink as well.

### Distillery
A device that extracts essences and distillations. Required for many potions and some drinks.

### Mortar and Pestle
A device for crushing raw ingredients. Use it to make dyes and powders.

### Scrying Bowl
An enchanted bowl.  Fill it with Scrying Potion to find the location of people or objects.

### Crystal Ball
A magical device that reveals innermost desires.  Requires Crystal Ball Solution, brewed in a Cauldron.
