# Bee Hive

[Workstation]
  itemName: Bee Hive
  description: Bees will live here and produce honey and wax if surrounded by plenty of flowers. Build it at the garden Construction Table.
  size: 1x1
  buildCost: 10 Wood; 50 coins
  location: Garden; Vegetable Patch
  unlockRequirements: Meant To Bee

Bee Hives are structures placed in the Garden that produce Honey and Beeswax over time. Production time depends on how many fully-grown flowers are planted per bee hive. 

Daily production requires a ratio of 12 Flowers:1 Hive.

| Name | Sell Price | Description |
| Honey | 70 Coins | An amber-colored sweetener produced by beehives. Cook with it or sell it to Violet. |
| Beeswax | Cannot be sold | A wax produced by honey bees. Use it in the candlemaker to make beeswax candles. |
