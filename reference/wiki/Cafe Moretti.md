# Cafe Moretti

| <b>Cafe Moretti</b> |
| <b> Mon | Closed |
| <b>Tue-Fri | 9am-5pm |
| <b>Sat | Closed |
| <b> Sun | 9am-5pm |
"Step in for some pie and other classic comfort foods"; 
Cafe Moretti is run by Sophia Moretti and sells foods that can restore a substantial amount of energy. Several villagers are known to frequent the cafe at different times of day, and selling cooked dishes to Sophia can be a great source of profit!
