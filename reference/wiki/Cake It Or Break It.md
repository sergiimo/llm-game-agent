# Cake It Or Break It

[Quest]
  title: Cake It Or Break It
  type: To-Do Quests
  start: Angus Seton
  end: Angus Seton
  prerequisites: Reverse Time Spell
  level: Fall
  rewards: * 2x Blueberry Tart
* Blueberry Tart recipe
  next: ?

Cake It Or Break It is a quest given by Angus Seton during the Fall before his wedding. He asks to temporarily store his wedding cake in your fridge. You agree, but the fridge breaks the next morning and causes the cake to spoil, requiring you to find a magical solution to the problem. The Farseer teaches you the Reverse Time Spell, which you use to restore the cake right before Angus returns to pick it up. The reward for completion is 2x Blueberry Tart and the Blueberry Tart recipe.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: Hey Angus, what’s going down?
* Angus: My wedding cake, is what! My fridge in the bakery is full of tiny individual tarts for a fundraiser Mrs. Soft is hosting for Mayor Soft… And if I put the cake in our fridge at home, Francis will see it! I know Hazel baked a lot, you must have a good-sized fridge. Can I borrow some space, pretty please?
* Tara: Of course!
* Angus: Thank you thank you thank you! I’ll just pop it in. Byeeeee!

Part 1: Angus has asked to store a wedding cake in your fridge until his is clear.

* Tara: What’s that smell? Spoiled milk? Uh oh. Of course the fuse blew. The wiring is probably from when Gramma still used ice boxes! Ugh, it’s too late to make a new cake!
* Violet: Hellooo? Angus said he was storing his cake in your fridge and I’ve got the edible flowers! Ew. Tell me that melted disaster isn’t…
* Tara: It is. Can we magic it back together somehow?
* Violet: How? That buttercream has to be spoiled! Unless…. see the Farseer. He can help you.

Part 2: The wedding cake has spoiled! Talk to the Farseer about a possible solution.

* Tara: Farseer, I ruined Angus’s wedding cake! And I don’t have time or ingredients to make another!
* Farseer Shelby: Folks always obsessin’ over time. It doesn’t just go one way, you know. Try this incantation. But mind that you aim your wand right at the object you want to set back in time. Don’t want it hittin’ someone by mistake and turning ‘em into a baby. That’s a whole lotta trouble. Heh.

Part 3: Craft a reverse time incantation.

* Tara: Okay, I guess it’s now or never. Tick, tock, turn back the clock, and let the past be unlocked!
* Angus: Yoohoo! I finally got rid of that tart! I mean those tarts! I would never call Mrs. Soft… Anyway, can I pick up my cake?
* Tara: Sure, go ahead!
* Angus: Thanks, you’re the best, Tara! For being so kind, I’ve brough you a couple spare tarts I had from the catering gig. Aaaaaand… the recipe to make your own!
* [Reward: 2x Blueberry Tart, Blueberry Tart recipe]
