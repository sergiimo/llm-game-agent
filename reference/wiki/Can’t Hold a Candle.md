# Can’t Hold a Candle

[Quest]
  title: Can’t Hold a Candle
  type: To-Do Quests
  start: Sophia Moretti
  end: Sophia Moretti
  prerequisites: 3x Beeswax Candle
  level: Winter
  rewards: * 3x Latke
* Latke recipe

Can’t Hold a Candle is a quest given by Sophia Moretti in the Winter. It requires the player to deliver 3x Beeswax Candle to her so she and Juliet can celebrate Hanukkah. The reward for completion is 3x Latke and the Latke recipe.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: Hey Sophia.
* Sophia: Hi Tara. Kim mentioned you’re going to help out with the Winter Feast festivities this year.
* Tara: Yeah. I don’t blame you if you don’t feel up to organizing things.
* Sophia: I don’t think we’ll even go this year. Juliet doesn’t want to, and I’m not going to force her to pretend to be merry. This time of year is already hard enough, celebrating Hanukkah without Giacomo. I want to make a small but cozy celebration for Juliet and I this year. Do you have a candlemaker in your shed? I would love some handmade candles for our menorah. I don’t quite have enough.
* Tara: Sure, I would love to help.
* Sophia: Wow, thanks. And Tara? Thanks for, you know, still talking to me. It feels like people have been avoiding me lately. 

Quest completion:

* Sophia: Any progress with the candles?
* Tara: Yes, here they are!
* Sophia: Oh, they’re perfect! Thanks so much, Tara! And just in time for the first night of Hanukkah! Have some latkes! And here’s the recipe, if you want to make more! All you need are some potatoes and a good frying pan!
* Tara: Oh, but potatoes are Spring-season crops.
* Sophia: Hmmm, well there must be some way for you to grow out of season plants! A greenhouse, maybe? Island life does have its limitations. To be honest, I’ve been thinking about leaving. Marty offered to introduce me to a real estate agent who could help me sell the diner.
* Tara: Oh no, you can’t leave, Sophia!
* Sophia: You’re very kind, Tara. It means a lot.
* [Reward: 3x Latke, Latke recipe]
