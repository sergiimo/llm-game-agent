# Charmed, I'm Sure

[Quest]
  title: Charmed, I'm Sure
  type: To-Do Quests
  start: the Acolyte
  prerequisites: 1x Charming Potion

Charmed, I'm Sure is a quest given by the Acolyte to unlock her identity. It requires you to brew 1x Charming Potion and deliver it to her during the day. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Acolyte: Ooh, it’s my turn! Yay! I can’t believe they made me wait until second to last. I hate secrets.
* Tara: But the coven itself is one big secret…
* Acolyte: Yeah, but it’s one we keep together, so that’s more fun. There’s still friction with the normies, though. And since Otto was elected, it’s gotten worse. So! Enter the charm potion.
* Tara: Are you serious?! I asked Gramma if we could use a charm potion to smooth things over between the coven and the town last Spring, and she said no!
* Acolyte: Well yeah, dingus, you don’t want to charm an entire town. Not only would that take forever, but it’s a surefire way to make people go, “Huh, maybe one of my neighbors IS a witch!” One person at a time, though? Less of a problem.
* Tara: Got it. One charm potion, coming up.
* [Receive Charming Potion recipe]

Quest completion:

* Tara: Here you go… Violet.
* Violet: Yes! You figured it out! What gave me away? Now you’re really one of us. I know it’s a bit intimidating being the newest member, but I can already tell you’re going to do great!
