# Chicken Fillet

[Crafting Ingredient]
  itemName: Chicken Fillet
  description: You can buy chicken fillets from Kim to use in recipes, or sell Kim grown chickens you have raised.
  source: Chickens; Kim's Local Butcher and Deli
  buyPrice: 160 coins
  sellPrice: 80 coins
  sellTo: Kim's Local Butcher and Deli
  other: ==Notes==
The day after selling your full-grown chicken to Kim, they'll show up at your house and hand over three chicken fillets and some feathers!
