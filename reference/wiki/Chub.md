# Chub

[Fish]
  itemName: Chub
  description: A common ray-finned fish found near the mountains. They're too small to use in recipes, but can be sold to Bruno or used as bait.
  location: Mountain
  shadow: Small fish
  sellPrice: 3 coins
  buyPrice: 4 coins
  bait: Chub; Worm
  other: ==Other information==
Bruno first teaches the player about using chubs as bait early on in the Spring, also commenting that they can get more of the bait by the mountains. However, the player will not be able to catch any chubs until much later in the Summer, after they've had Parker fix the bridge to the Mountain.
