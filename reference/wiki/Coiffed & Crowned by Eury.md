# Coiffed & Crowned by Eury

| <b>Coiffed & Crowned by Eury</b> |
| <b>Mon-Tue | 9am-5pm |
| <b>Wed-Fri | Closed |
| <b>Sat-Sun | 9am-5pm |
"Here for your hair, and always a free chair!"; 
Coiffed and Crowned by Eury is a hair salon run by Eury Sinclair, located between the Mayor's residence and the Consciousness Center. By selling crafted hair products to Eury, the player unlocks more hairstyles to buy for Tara.

Hairstyles require their corresponding hair color and sometimes pomade to purchase. These can be crafted at your Mortar and Pestle. Hairstyles only need to be purchased once, then you can select them at any time via the salon or your inventory.

For an image comparison of all hair/eyebrow combinations, see Hair Styles.
