# Coop

The quest to gain the Coop is All Cooped Up.

To upgrade the Coop, Tara must attempt to buy a Duck from Marty Emerson on The Hayseed Homestead. Marty will say the Coop has to be expanded first and will unlock the Expanded Coop blueprint with Parker at Nailed It!. 

In order for Ducks to be available from Marty, Tara has to complete the Menagerie Mixup quest first. That quest triggers after completing the Upgraded Greenhouse and the Propogation Shed, and potentially also the Hay Field.
