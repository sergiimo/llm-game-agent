# Copper Ingot

[Crafting Ingredient]
  itemName: Copper Ingot
  source: Blacksmith
  recipe: 2x Copper Ore; 10 coins
  sellPrice: 25 coins
  sellTo: Blacksmith
  description: Copper Ingots can be sold to Natalia or used to upgrade tools and craft useful items for your farm.
  other: ==Notes==
Copper ore can only be found in the mines and only floor 5 and above. Mine the big Iron blocks to receive either Iron OR Copper.
