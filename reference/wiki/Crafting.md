# Crafting

Crafting is anything that Tara creates on her farm, using foraged, grown, or purchased items. There are 3 crafting locations on the farm: the tool shed, the basement, and the garden. You must first craft machinery to then craft items.

## Tool Shed
The tool shed is a space next to Tara's farmhouse where she can build a variety of crafting stations to refine resources. Originally (size), it can be upgraded by Parker Johnson to have a usable layout of 7x12 (total size 9x12). The upgrade costs 50x wood and (amount) coins and requires Tara to clear the surrounding space, which may call for a wood axe and/or pickaxe. The upgrade will be complete on the second morning after purchasing.

### Paper Press
A device that presses wood into paper.

### Loom
Used to make cloth from cotton and wool or silk from silk thread.

### Candlemaker
A device for making candles out of beeswax, soy milk, or animal fat. 

### Juice Press
A device that extracts juices and oils from raw fruits and vegetables.

### Dyeing Bath
A large bucket for dyeing fabric. Make dyes with the mortar and pestle.

### Metalworking Table
A workstation for creating metal jewelry.

### Fermentation Barrel
A big wooden barrel used to make beer, wine, cheese, and other interesting recipes!

### Glass Kiln
A furnace powered by coal. It turns high-quality sand into glass.

### Silkworm Box
A silkworm house. Fill it with silkworms and feed them mulberry leaves to get silk thread.

### Butter Churn
A device that makes butter from milk or milk alternatives.

## Basement
The basement is where Tara will craft magic items. Originally outfitted with a cauldron, you will have to add other machinery and furniture in order to craft all magic items. The basement has 9x10 useable spaces.

### Cauldron
The cauldron is included with the introduction of the basement. Used to craft potions.

### Distillery
The distillery is used to craft ingredients for potions, drinks, and liquor.

### Desk
The desk is for crafting incantations and ink.

### Crystal Ball
The crystal ball can see the "desires" (read: favorite foods) of any character, using a crystal ball potion.

### Scrying Bowl
The scrying bowl can find anything you have misplaced, using the scrying bowl potion.

### Mortar and Pestle
The mortar and pestle is used to create dyes for fabric, crushed powders, and flour.

## Garden
The garden is where Tara grows fruit trees, vegetables, and flowers. Hazel's home starts with an 8x8 garden beside it, but Tara can purchase an 11x12 vegetable garden from Parker.

### Garden Bed
The garden bed is used for planting 4 seeds or one tree.

### Compost Bin
The compost bin creates soil using two weeds from the farm.

### Beehive
The beehive creates honey to be sold or crafted with.
