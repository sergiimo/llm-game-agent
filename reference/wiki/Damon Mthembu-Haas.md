  CharacterDescription: Damon Mthebu-Haas (24) is the owner of the only bar in Fairhaven. 

He cares a lot about the bar's upkeep and often works into the early morning, rising in the afternoon to drink coffee at Sophia's diner.

Damon may be young to own a bar, but it's a family institution - his dad ran it before he and Damon's mom moved back to South Africa. His mother, who was once the Fairhaven pharmacist, wanted to return to her roots as a healer. Now that Damon's parents have moved, Lina is like his surrogate family.

Damon is friendly and enjoys socializing with all inhabitants of Fairhaven. He is especially close with Lina, whom he regards as an older sister.
