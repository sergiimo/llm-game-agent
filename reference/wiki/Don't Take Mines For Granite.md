# Don't Take Mines For Granite

[Quest]
  start: Natalia
  item_needs: 100 Coin; 10x Wood; 5 Stone
  title: Don't Take Mines For Granite
  next: Oh, the Iron-y
  rewards: Metalworking Table Blueprints; Glass Kiln Blueprint
  type: Building Quest
  location: Farm

Don't Take Mines For Granite is a quest given by Natalia Kuznetsova. It requires you to gather 100 coins, 10x Wood, and 5x Stone and deliver them to Parker Johnson so he can repair the mine entrance. The rewards for completion (along with unlocking the mine) are the recipes for the Metalworking Table and Glass Kiln.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Natalia: Good morning! I’ve had an inspiration!
* Tara: Uh oh. When people here get inspired that somehow always means more work for me.
* Natalia: Haha! You got me! Course, I know you’re busy, what with the farm and takin’ care of your Gram. But I’ve been looking at old blueprints of the mine, and I think Parker could fix it up for you. Nothing to it but some new wood supports and some stone to build back up the rubble wall entrance. Anyhow, if you do get it fixed up and dig out some good iron ore, I’d be happy to smelt it for you!
* Tara: You’re right, that does sound useful. I’ll get to it.

Part 1: Collect wood and stone so that Parker can repair the mine on your farm.

* Parker: Should only take a day or so!

Part 2: Wait for Parker to finish repairing the mine. It should only take a day!

* Natalia: Yoo-hoo! Parker stopped by on his way out to let me know the mine was finished. Let’s go check it out!
* [Reward: Metalworking table and glass kiln recipes]
