# Echo Blossom

Echo Blossoms are magical flowers found in the Gloaming. Violet's mother, Gloria, used them to leave messages behind before she disappeared.

There are ten Echo Blossoms in total. 

## Dialogue
Blossom 1 (first pillar)

* Herbalist Violet: Ooh. Hold up. I’ve never seen a plant like this before.
* Adept Amira: I’ve only heard of them, but I think this is an Echo Blossom.
* Tara: Woah. All the petals are covered in crystal.
* Adept Amira: Echo Blossoms look normal, but if you grasp the stem, the flower takes a short impression. And if you crack the layer of crystals…
* Herbalist Violet: Oops! It’s so slippery! No. No way.
* Gloria: I hope this works. Echo Blossoms are powerful magic, and my mama said not many of them are left… … but if I don’t make it out of the Gloaming tonight, I want someone to know I was here. And these will be my little breadcrumb trail.
* Adept Amira: Gloria…
* Tara: Is it true, Violet? That was your mom?
* Herbalist Violet: I – I can’t believe I just – yeah. That was her.
* Tara: So we know she came this way the night she disappeared!
* Adept Amira: It seems so. Violet, are you all right? What a shock to your system.
* Herbalist Violet: Oh my god! I saw her! I really saw her!!! Yes!
* Adept Amira: We should look for more of these.
* Herbalist Violet: Are you kidding? I’m not letting a single one get away! I need to know everything!
* Adept Amira: Well, before we get into the search we need to get back and speak with the coven about it.
* Herbalist Violet: Ugh, fine but then I want to come right back and begin looking!

Blossom 2 (first pillar)

* Herbalist Violet: I knew it! I knew we’d find more Echo Blossoms!
* Tara: Do you want to listen to it?
* Herbalist Violet: Pffft, what kind of question is that?
* Gloria: Ever since I was a little girl, the wraiths have been getting stronger. Once, when I was ten, a wraith escaped. Took over poor Mr. Gibson, and he let all his cows loose… It was funny then. Not so funny now.
* Herbalist Violet: I heard about Mr. Gibson! I had no idea he set his cows loose because of a wraith… What else could my mom have told me if she’d been there?
* Tara: I’m sorry, Violet.
* Herbalist Violet: Don’t be sorry. Coming here is the closest I’ve gotten to her for years!

Blossom 3 (fae circle, first pillar)

* Herbalist Violet: Yes! Another one!
* Gloria: Over the years, the Gloaming itself has changed. It used to be fully alive, you know? Everything bloomed. But now there are so many places where nothing grows. I worry that when Violet and Sebastian are grown, there’ll be nothing left.
* Herbalist Violet: She was always stressed. I thought it was because she had a lot to manage, being the mayor’s wife and our mom and all. But this must have stressed her out too.
* Tara: Because it affected you. And your brother.
* Herbalist Violet: And she was right to be worried.

Blossom 4 (second pillar)

* Herbalist Violet: Tara, look! Another Echo Blossom!

Lina and Giacomo just want to keep the town from finding out about us, Arno and Kaya are too busy planning their move back home, and Hazel seems distracted lately. But I can’t forget what Shelby said: “A daughter of the coven will be in mortal danger.” I will protect Violet. Even if I have to do it alone.

* Herbalist Violet: I’m going to find her, Tara. No matter what, I need to know what happened.

* Herbalist Violet: Is that another Echo Blossom?

Blossom 6 (second pillar)

* Herbalist Violet: Yeah… that hasn’t happened.

* Gloria: By now, Antonio’s probably noticed I’m gone. I haven’t come up with a good excuse yet. He senses my magic, even if he doesn’t understand it. He never asks too many questions. But I’ve been stepping out late at night a lot more lately.

My dad had to drop out of the race after someone started blackmailing him about us being witches. We still don’t know who sent them.
