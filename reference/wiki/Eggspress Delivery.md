# Eggspress Delivery

Eggspress Delivery is a quest given by Angus Seton during the Fall before his wedding. It requires you to deliver 6x Egg and 1x Milk to him. The reward for completion is 5x Marmalade Pudding.

Although Angus says he wants fresh ingredients, it makes no difference whether the eggs and milk you give him come from farm animals or the Dahl General Store.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Angus: Tara! Just the person I was hoping to see. Do you have milk and eggs to spare?
* Tara: Aren’t those essential ingredients for bakers?
* Angus: Well, yes… but I wanted to make marmalade pudding for the rehearsal dinner, and it’s best with fresh ingredients.
* Tara: Marmalade pudding. Sounds British.
* Angus: Excuse you! It’s a classic Scottish dessert! My gran taught me her own recipe. Help me out and I’ll make an extra pudding just for you. 

Quest completion:

* Angus: I’ll use store-bought ingredients if I have to, but-
* Tara: No need. Here.
* Angus: Fresh eggs and fresh milk! You’re worth a true baker’s dozen. Come by in a day and I’ll show you what I’ve whipped up.
* [Visit Angus tomorrow to try one of the puddings!
* Angus: I’m so excited to see what you think! Actually… I might’ve over-catered a bit… so I’ve got a few for you! Thanks for all your help.
* [Reward: 5x Marmalade Pudding]
