# Epilogue

The Epilogue takes place during the Spring of Tara's second year in Fairhaven, after the quest Locket Up has been completed. 

## Cutscenes
### Otto and Vanessa's Departure
A few days into Spring, the three Mirandas will show up in front of the farmhouse and tell Tara that an emergency meeting has been called.

* Tara: Morning, Violet – oh, it’s all the Mirandas!
* Gloria: I wanted to see the Wylde farm again. Violet said you’d restored it to its former glory.
* Sebastian: I just wanted to go for a walk.
* Violet: He’s still getting used to Mom being here. So he’s like her barnacle.
* Sebastian: I heard that!
* Gloria: Otto’s called an emergency meeting. Will you two come with us?
* Tara: It would be our pleasure.

* [Town Hall]

* Mayor Otto: Good morning. I am truly glad to see you all looking well.
* Violet: Otto Soft, everybody, now with 100% less slime.
* Mayor Otto: There’s no point in theatrics. I am resigning as the mayor of Fairhaven, effective immediately.
* --[Choice: I’m shocked]
** Tara: What? I thought Otto would never give up power!
** Violet: Me, either. Oh no, do I have to respect him a little now?
* --[Choice: He had to do this]
** Tara: No shocker there. He kinda had to step down.
** Violet: Right? Meeting Stranger Danger Vanessa in the woods for all those years was corruption. I think.
* Marty: Who will replace you?
* Mayor Otto: I would like to nominate Gloria Miranda to be interim mayor until you all can hold another election. Does anyone disagree with that? [pause] Good. Gloria, do you accept?
* Gloria: I do.
* Mayor Otto: Then that’s settled. And it means that Vanessa and I can leave Fairhaven. The state attorney general wants to have a conversation about some of my… um… past fundraising efforts…
* Vanessa: I have something to say. I’m sorry for the way I acted. I may have been possessed by a wraith, but before that, I kept secrets and manipulated people. I was only concerned about myself. I’m sorry to the coven, and Juliet, especially. I’ve thought about it, and I don’t have a place in Fairhaven. Not right now.
* Sophia: Or ever.
* Vanessa: So I’ve spoken to Giva about taking over at the school.
* Giva: I’m quite excited!
* Vanessa: We’ll be taking the next ferry to Milkwater…

* [Docks, the ferry departs]

* Violet: And then a sea monster came out of nowhere and ate the ferry…
* Gloria: Violet!
* Violet: Joking, joking.
* Tara: Hey, I just realized. I came to Fairhaven a year ago today.
* Violet: And do you wish you were on that ferry, heading back to the city?
* Tara: No. Not for anything.

* [Credits roll]

### Parker and Lina
At the Mountain Pass overlook, Tara overhears Parker and Lina talking. 

* Parker: I don’t know, Lina… to be real with you, if it weren’t for the kids, I wouldn’t even be talking to you right now.
* Lina: I want to start over. Hi there, handsome stranger. I’m Lina. I’m a witch.
* Tara: Lina. “Handsome stranger?” Come on…
* Parker: Hi, Lina. I’m Parker. I’m not a witch. But if you had just told me the truth from the beginning…
* Lina: I know. Do you think we could take these seedlings and plant them together anyway?
* Parker: When I first met you, I felt like you understood me. Like we were meant to put roots down together. But I didn’t know you at all.
* Lina: Wow, what a coincidence! So, why don’t we get to know each other while we get these seedlings in the ground?
* Parker: Hey. You gotta water ‘em right away so they don’t get transplant shock. Learned that one the hard way. Here. Like this.
* Tara: And this is my cue to leave! 

### Westley's Atonement
At the coven circle at night, Westley makes an important offering to the Wellspring. 

* Farseer Shelby: Figured ya’d be back. Guilty conscience weighin’ on ya, boy?
* Westley: Yes. Very much.
* High Priestess Lina: I’ve been thinking about your betrayal, too. Because of what you did, those of us in the coven had to use a tremendous amount of magical force. It was exhausting.
* Westley: I realize that. So I brought something to contribute to the Wellspring, if you’ll take it. This is the werewolf suppression potion Vanessa gave me. It’s made with rare ingredients.
* Farseer Shelby: Ahhhh, so it’s powerful stuff.
* Westley: Powerful enough to keep the Wellspring balanced for a good while.
* High Priestess Lina: That would give us a much-needed break.
* Tara: But Westley… … doesn’t that mean you’ll be a werewolf in the daytime, too?
* Westley: Yes, at least until I start taking the potion again. Vanessa told me how to brew more, and Peri’s already begun a batch. But it takes a long time to steep. In the meantime, giving you my supply is the right thing to do.
* --[Choice: That’s brave]
** Tara: It’s brave of you to give up something you’ve relied on.
** Westley: Is it brave? Doesn’t feel that way to me. You witches are the brave ones.
* --[Choice: That’s something]
** Tara: After what you told Vanessa, this is the least you could do.
** High Priestess Lina: He didn’t fully know what he was doing-
** Westley: No, no. You’re right, Tara.
* High Priestess Lina: All right, Westley. We accept. Go ahead.
* [Westley offers the potion to the Wellspring]
* Tara: I feel amazing.
* Journeyman Damon: Hoooooooly mother of -!
* Westley: It’s working! Guess I better get used to looking like this for a while.
* Farseer Shelby: Don’t fret. It’s your more fetchin’ form, boy.
* Journeyman Damon: Hey, hey, hey. Both your forms are smokin’. This is a body-positive coven!
* Westley: I wish I agreed, but thanks for the support, I guess.
* Tara: You’re supporting us, so we’re supporting you. That’s how this works. 

### Marty's Ranch
Marty talks to Tara about his future.

* Tara: Hiya Marty – Martin? Um, which one is it?
* Marty: It’s Marty. My father’s real estate managers just called. Maplethorpe Tower has been sold to pay his creditors. And I feel… fine, actually. It’s strange. All my life I’ve carried the weight of the Maplethorpe name. But now, for the first time, my future is completely mine to define. For better or worse.
* Tara: So you’ll stay?
* Marty: Yeah. What would become of Mootilda or Attila the Hen if I left? Besides, I can’t give Thomas the satisfaction of watching me move out.
* --[Choice: That sounds petty]
** Tara: So you’re staying to spite Thomas? That sounds awfully petty.
** Marty: Ha, well, I guess I’m still a Maplethorpe in the end.
* --[Choice: That sounds responsible]
** Tara: Look at you, taking responsibility for your livestock!
** Marty: I know, following through on something we start is not exactly a Maplethorpe trait. It feels weird… but good!
* Marty: When I came here, I thought I was going to be dealing with a bunch of country rubes. But then this strange thing happened. I began to actually like the people here. I just hope they give me another chance.
