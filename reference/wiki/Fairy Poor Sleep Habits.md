# Fairy Poor Sleep Habits

[Quest]
  title: Fairy Poor Sleep Habits
  imagecaption: Peri, at the lighthouse, staring into the distance
  type: To-Do Quest
  start: Farm, after dark
  previous: Up to the Tusk
  item_needs: Sleeping Potion
  end: Peri
  rewards: 100 Gold; 3x Turkish Delight

## Description
Speak to Aryel and see if baby Zoey can help Peri fall asleep.

Speak to Amira about finding a potion to help Peri Sleep.

Use your cauldron to craft a sleeping potion.

## Dialog
### Quest Intro
Tara: Who's there? Peri?

Tara: Peri? Are you OK?

Tara: Oh, are you asleep? Wake up, Peri!

Peri: Tara! Oh. It happened again.

Tara: Have you been... sleepwalking a lot?

Peri: Yes. And I always end up here. I don' tknow why. I can never remember the dreams, but I feel so drained when I awaken.

Peri: I've been wondering if Aryel's new creature might be able to help me. I'm so tired.

Tara: No wonder! You're running around all night! Don't worry, Peri, we'll find a way to help you get some rest.

---

Tara: Aryel, I saw Peri sleepwalking the other night. Do you think Zoey could help with her nightmares?

Aryel: Ah yes. She could draw them out, but only if Peri is sound alseep.

Aryel: She is too re4stless at night now. Perhaps the doctor witch has something amongst her potions that might help?

Tara: Okay, I'll go ask Amira.

Aryel: We must only give Peri the potion on the night that Zoey visits her.

---

Tara: Amira, do you have anything that could help someone sleep through the night?

Amira: Oh? Have you been having trouble falling asleep?

Tara: It's not for me. It's for... Peri. She's been sleepwalking lately.

Amira: Hmm, that is unusual. Usually she sleeps so well she snores loud enough to wake the dead.

Amira: Ahem. Make her this sleeping drought. She'll sleep soundly until dawn.

### Quest Completion
Tara: Here's the sleeping potion.

Aryel: Ah yes, that will do nicely!

Peri: Thank you, witchling.

### Rewards
Tara: Hi, Peri. How are you feeling?

Peri: Oh, much better! it's a bit strange to think that elephant-faced creature somehow -- ate -- my nightmares. But I slept like a... like a...

Tara: A baby?

Peri: No! Such a strange expression. Everyone knows mortal infants sleep terribly.

Peri: But I did sleep much better, yes. Thanks to you. Please accept this.

Tara: You're very welcome, Peri.
