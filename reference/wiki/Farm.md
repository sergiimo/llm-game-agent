# Farm

; 
The Farm serves as Tara's home base throughout the game and supports crafting in the kitchen, Basement, and Tool Shed, farming in the Garden and additional farm plot, and animal rearing in the Barn and chicken coop. There are resources to forage along the left and lower parts of the area, as well as respawning trees to chop for wood and rocks to break for stone. Early on raw wood may occasionally spawn on the ground, serving as the only source of this resource before obtaining a suitable wood axe!

## Connections
* Town Center (upper right; exits next to Blacksmith)
* Beach (bottom left; exits north of Thomas's farm)
* Mountain Overpass (top left, once unlocked)
* Mine (following the path along the left side of screen, once unlocked)
* Ravenwood Hollow (mid-left, once unlocked)

## Residents
* Gramma
* Tara Wylde
