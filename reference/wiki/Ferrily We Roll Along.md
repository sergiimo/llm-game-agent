# Ferrily We Roll Along

Ferrily We Roll Along is a quest given by Zephyr Grimspark in the epilogue. It requires the player to deliver 1x Ruby to her so she can create a new engine for the ferry. The reward for completion is 3x Powered Sapphire.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Zephyr: Hiya, Tara! How are you doin’? You’ve had it real rough.
* Tara: Yesterday, I wondered if it was possible to die from being too tired.
* Zephyr: Ahhh, well, I hate to ask this, then, but the ferry is finally out of the ice. If ya bring me a ruby, I’ll use astral spectroscopy to tune it’s vibrational frequencies-
* Tara: My brain can’t handle those words right now.
* Zephyr: Ooh, sorry. It’s like this… Embed my customized ruby in the ferry hull, and it’ll send out tendrils of magic to repel the water. No human engineer needed!
* Tara: I’ll work on it.

Zephyr has asked for a ruby to create a new engine for the ferry.
