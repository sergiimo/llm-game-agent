# Fertility Potion

[Item]
  itemName: Fertility Potion
  source: Pig's Wings and Dragonlings
  description: Feed to an adult barn animal and it will become pregnant! Purchase from Aryel.
  buyPrice: 230 coins
  other: ==Information==
This potion is only available to purchase, not for crafting. 

Mature barn animals can become pregnant by being fed the Fertility Potion. A pregnant animal moves into the birthing stall inside the barn and stays there until the baby animal is born. It takes several days for the birth prompt to appear: pigs give birth in 4 days, sheep and cows in 7, and alpacas in 8.

If a pregnant animal is fed its favorite food on every day of its pregnancy, it will give birth to a magical animal.
