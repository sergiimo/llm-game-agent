# Fish Fingers

[Food]
  itemName: Fish Fingers
  sellTo: Sophia
  description: Baked chunks of fish coated in breadcrumbs. Can be sold to Sophia.
  recipe: 1x Any Fish; 1x Flour
  sellPrice: 100 coins
  category: Oven
  personsFavorite: Emmi
  recipeSource: Available at start of game
  other: == Notes ==
It is possible to earn nearly infinite money by purchasing flour from Lina for 15 coins, Tuna from Bruno for 20 coins, and cooking them into fish fingers which sell for 100 coins (net profit of 65 coins per item). For maximum efficiency, it is best to aim for a time when all three businesses are open (Tuesday & Wednesday 11am-5pm) and repeatedly spend all of your money on ingredients for exponential growth of profits!
