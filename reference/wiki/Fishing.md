# Fishing

Fishing is unlocked when Bruno Soft gives the quest Lure One In, giving Tara a fishing rod.

There are 6 categories of fish, reflecting 7 areas with at least one fishing spot:
* River fish are caught in the Farm area
* Lake fish are caught in the Woods
* Ocean fish are caught in the Beach area and to the south of the Town Center
* Mountain, Mine, and Gloaming fish are found in the areas of the same names

Different fish can be caught using the three types of bait: chub, crickets, and worms.

Some cooking recipes can be caught when a bottle appears in the water after casting the line. The recipe is unlocked when the bottle is reeled in like a fish, which uses up the bait.
