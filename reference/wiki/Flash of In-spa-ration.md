# Flash of In-spa-ration

Flash of In-spa-ration is a quest given by Kim Izumi. It requires you to deliver 50x Wood and 50x Stone to them so they can build an onsen in the Mountain Pass. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Kim: Parker told me you’re the one to thank for the bridge to the Mountain Pass being open again! Thanks so much!
* Tara: Aww, it was nothing.
* Kim: The hot springs are so restorative! You know, I used to go to the onsen all the time with my grandma when we would go visit her in Japan. Well… until I turned 11 or so. Then I just felt… out of place. It would be so nice to have an onsen here with private stalls so everyone can take in the waters while feeling safe and comfortable. We just need some wood… and stones…
* Tara: You need my help to build the onsen, Kim?
* Kim: Only if it’s no trouble! 

Quest completion:

* Kim: Do you have the materials for the onsen yet?
* Tara: Yep! Here you go!
* Kim: Great! I’ll get to work on it right away. Once it’s done, I’m going to put a tip jar outside so everyone can contribute to its maintenance. And, I think it’s best if we all stick to one visit per day so that everyone can have a chance to enjoy the onsen.
* Tara: Those are great ideas. I can’t wait to try this onsen out!
