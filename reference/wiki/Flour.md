# Flour

[Crafting Ingredient]
  itemName: Flour
  description: A useful baking ingredient. You can craft it from wheat in your mortar and pestle or buy it from Lina.
  buyFrom: Dahl General Store
  buyPrice: 15 coins
  source: Mortar and Pestle; Dahl General Store
  recipe: 1x Wheat

## Notes
When bought from Lina can be used in conjunction with Tuna bought from Bruno to craft infinite Fish Fingers, selling for a profit of 70 coins per dish!
