# Flyers For The Marty Party

Flyers For The Marty Party is a quest given by Marty Emerson during the Fall before the election. It requires you to deliver 20x Paper to him so he can make flyers. The reward for completion is 200 coins.

This quest is optional and must be completed before the election to get the reward. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Marty: Hey Tara! Some of my volunteers were going to pass out some flyers. They want to introduce my ideas for a new, improved Fairhaven to the community!
* Tara: Let me guess, you need paper?
* Marty: You got it! It would be great to have you join The Marty Party!
* Tara: Sure, no problem.
* Marty: That’s great news! As quick as you can, we gotta get moving! 

Quest completion:

* Marty: Do you have my paper yet? We have to get the word out about “Fairhaven of the Future!”
* Tara: Yes, here it is!
* Marty: Thanks! Now which side do you think I should us for the photo, my right or my left? I’m joking, I don’t have a bad side!
* [Reward: 200 coins]
