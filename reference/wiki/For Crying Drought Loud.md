# For Crying Drought Loud

For Crying Drought Loud is a series of quests initiated by Thomas Lightfoot related to the Summer drought. It requires you to talk to certain townspeople about the water shortage. 

There are several points in the quest where you must talk to specific people:

* After Thomas first notifies you of the drought, you need to talk to eight people: Lina, Giva, Damon, Bruno, Cameron, Violet, Natalia, and Otto.

* After you cast the Summon Rain Spell, you need to talk to four people: Thomas, Marty*, Violet, and Otto.  (*Note: If you do not have Marty in Fairhaven yet, you do not need to talk to him.)

* After the town wells dry up, you need to talk to two of the following people: Thomas, Marty, Lina, Giva, Damon, Cameron, Natalia, or Otto.
If you need to water your crops after the wells dry up, you can refill your watering can using bottled water that can be purchased from the Dahl General Store for 60 coins (up to three bottles) or received from Cameron for free on Wednesdays and Sundays (one bottle). Each bottle fully refills your watering can. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: Good mornin’, Thomas!
* Thomas: Hmmph. Not such a good morning at my farm. My well water’s comin’ up full of mud and silt.
* Tara: Oh. Is it… broken?
* Thomas: No, that means it’s about to go dry. The drought has gone on too long.
* Tara: Well, you’re welcome to use my well if you need to.
* Thomas: Appreciated, but that’s not gonna solve the problem long term. Yours will go dry soon too, I suspect. And while you’re plannin’ for this dry spell – might want to check in with everyone in town. This has been a scorcher of a summer.
* Tara: I will. Thanks for giving me a heads up, Thomas. 

Part 1: Thomas says a drought has hit Fairhaven! Ask around town to see if everyone’s ok.	

* Lina

** Lina: Oh, busy busy. We’ve ordered jugs of water from Milkwater. Everyone seems to need more than the town pipes can supply. So if you need some water, they should be coming off the ferry soon. Oh! And if you have any eggs to sell, that would be great! Marty’s hardly got any lately. I can’t wait until this dry spell is over!
* Emmi
** Tara: Hi, Emmi. How’s business?
** Emmi: Booming! People are so thirsty we can charge whatever we want! Heehee! I’ll have enough for the Slingshot 3000 in no time!

** Tara: Hi, Damon! Gosh, you must be the one person whose business is doing well in the drought!
** Damon: Well, the problem with being the local watering hole is you have to have water to make beer! I’ve been bringing jugs of water over on the ferry, but the price keeps going up! My taps may be running dry soon. I’m not sure I have the heart to tell Bruno.
* Bruno
** Tara: Well, I guess the fish are the only ones who don’t have to worry about running out of water, eh Bruno?
** Bruno: They might not, but I do! I’ve barely got enough to keep my ice machine goin’ and my fish fresh! I’ll buy what ya’ve got to sell now, young miss, but I might have to close up soon if it gets much hotter. And to make things worse, Damon’s keepin’ me to a two-drink limit. I’m parched!
* Cameron

** Cameron: Sure is! Come by the Consciousness Center anytime you need a break from the heat – we just installed new air conditioning! Not that air conditioning is a reason to go to a seminar… But it’s not NOT a reason, am I right?
* Violet
** Tara: Hi there Violet. How’s your garden doing?

And timing Sebastian in the shower. I swear, his hair routine is even more complicated than mine! But I think I might be done for the season after I harvest my last batch. The bees need water more than flowers do!

My water pressure’s been too low to keep my slack tub cold enough to set my pieces when I’m done workin’ ‘em in my forge. I won’t be able to upgrade any of your tools ‘til the water’s on full blast again. ‘Course, I can still melt yer ore into ingots as you need.
** Tara: Okay. Thanks for letting me know.
** Natalia: No worries! Hopefully this hot spell will pass soon!

** Marty: I’ll tell you what doesn’t go over well: no water for anyone, your voters included. What, exactly, is your plan, Mayor Soft?

** Marty: Oh, er. I was just telling Otto about my personal development plans! I came to Fairhaven to allow my soul to bloom. Just like Thoreau. As the great man said, “I went to the woods because I wished to live deliciously.”

Part 2: Speak to the High Priestess about the drought.

* Tara: Thomas told me his well is running dry.
* High Priestess Lina: Yes, Summers here in Fairhaven are always hotter and drier as they wear on. The growing trees and crops will drain the groundwater until the harvest time.
* Tara: Is there anything we can do?
* High Priestess Lina: A Summon Rain incantation might bring some relief. Here’s what you need. [Gives Summon Rain Spell recipe.] Bring it back here to the circle when you have it ready. 

Part 3: Craft a summon rain incantation.	

* High Priestess Lina: Do you have all you need for the Rain incantation?
* Tara: Here’s the Rain Spell.
* High Priestess Lina: Wonderful. You can recite it when you’re ready. All you need to do is take it from your pack and read it.
* Tara: Clouds, break open! Release your waters to the parched Earth! Rain, pour down!
