# Forget Me Not

Forget Me Not is a quest given by the High Priestess after the quest Weather She’s A Witch begins. It requires the player to find two of Parker's personal items for the incantation to banish the wraith from Parker. Unlike the last two times, the player does not need to craft the incantation. Instead, the player gives the items to Lina so she can craft and cast it herself. 

The two required items are 1x Parker's Wooden Figurine, which can be obtained by talking to Parker in his workshop, and 1x Bus Ticket Stub, which can be found by using a Lockpick Incantation to search Parker's workshop when he is not there. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: Lina… I mean High Priestess…
* High Priestess Lina. I know. I’ll craft and cast this incantation myself, Novice. Just bring me the items after letting the Farseer look at them.
* Farseer Shelby: Don’t mean to interject, High Priestess, but you know Parker better than anyone. Maybe you should choose the objects you want me to read.
* High Priestess Lina: No, it’s better that I keep him occupied before he casts suspicion on one of us. We can’t afford to lose a single member if we’re to turn the season.

Find some of Parker’s personal items for the banishment incantation.

* [After using Lockpick spell on Parker’s workshop]
* Tara: Okay, Parker, what mementos do you keep around here? Hmmm, an old bus ticket stub? Why would you have held onto that?

* Tara: Farseer, all I could find was this faded old ticket stub. It’s dated ten years ago.
* Farseer Shelby: Parker’s sitting at a bus stop in the city. Notices a pretty blonde lady next to him. Oh no! She’s fainted! He catches her just as she falls. Her eyes flutter open. She says…
* High Priestess Lina: I said… I’m so embarrassed, I guess I forgot to eat today. That’s what I told him. It wasn’t true. I had been training with the high priestess of a coven in the city. One of my spells just went a bit sideways and I passed out. I never really thought of it this way, but the first thing I ever said to Parker was a lie. And I’ve been lying to him ever since. … He really kept that ticket stub all this time?

* [Talking to Parker in his workshop]
* Tara: Hiya, Parker. How are you, um… feeling?
* Parker: Never better! Angus and Natalia might have gotten it wrong but I think we’ve really found the Malcontent this time! So what can I do for ya today? Need something built on your farm?
* Tara: No, I just came in to admire your workshop! You’re always working on something cool in here.
* Parker: Aww, thanks! Not too many folks really appreciate handmade woodwork anymore. Here, I want you to have this. [Gives 1x Parker’s Wooden Figurine] I’ve been practicing this new carving technique. Maybe when I get good enough, I can sell these in the shop, too!

* Tara: Parker made this, Farseer. Do you think it might tell you something?
* Farseer Shelby: Parker spends a lot of time carving these. It’s what he does when he wants to think something through. He’s trying to sort out memories that aren’t memories. Or maybe they’re dreams. He doesn’t know. He thought he saw Lina in a cloak. But that doesn’t make sense to him. Must have been a dream…
* High Priestess Lina: It wasn’t a dream. He saw me. It was months ago. I cast a memory charm on him. I had no choice, you see. I had no choice.
* Tara: Oh, Lina.
* High Priestess Lina: Don’t judge me, Tara. I did what I had to do to protect us all.

* [After both items have been shown to the Farseer]
* High Priestess Lina: I’ll take those items, Novice. This is my burden to bear. I’ll have the incantation ready before Giva’s trial. But one way or another, this won’t be the end of it. I can see that now.
