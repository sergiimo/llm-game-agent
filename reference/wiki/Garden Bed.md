# Garden Bed

[Workstation]
  itemName: Garden Bed
  description: A patch of ready-to-plant land for growing crops, trees and flowers.
  size: 2x2
  buildCost: 10 Wood; 1 Soil; 25 coins
  location: Garden; Vegetable Patch

A Garden Bed is a structure in the Garden that allows crops, flowers, and trees to be grown. Seeds that are planted in a garden bed and watered each day will produce crops. Each garden bed will produce 4 crops unless a production-boosting potion is used.

For a full list of products grown in garden beds, see: Garden.
