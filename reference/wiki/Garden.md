# Garden

## Garden
Using the fenced-in space next to the farmhouse, Tara can grow a number of crops and can craft areas to produce compost and honey.

## Vegetable Patch
A second garden area located south of the farmhouse, Tara can grow a number of crops and can craft areas to produce compost and honey. Parker can add this to your farm via Nailed It!.

### Structures
| Name | Cost | Use | Description |
| Garden Bed | 10 Wood; 1 Soil; 25 Coins | Grow Crops | A patch of ready-to-plant land for growing crops, trees and flowers. |
| Compost Bin | 6 Wood; 10 Coins | Produce Soil and Worms from Weeds | Turns weeds into soil for new garden beds. |
| Bee Hive | 10 Wood; 50 coins | Produce Honey and Beeswax | Bees will live here and produce honey and wax if surrounded by plenty of flowers. Build it at the garden Construction Table. |
| Hummingbird Feeder | 10 Wood; 100 coins | Decorative | Placed in garden areas, this decorative hummingbird feeder is a great place to watch the hummingbirds gather. |
