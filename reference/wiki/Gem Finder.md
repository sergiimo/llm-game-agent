# Gem Finder

[Workstation]
  itemName: Gem Finder
  description: A device in the mines that shows you which stone on that level has a gem in it.
  buildCost: Coal  x5 to activate
  location: Mine
  unlockRequirements: Enchanted Silver Wire x5 or x10 or x15
There are 3: One each on levels 15, 18, and 20. 

Each one needs a different number of Enchanted Silver Wire to unlock:

* The one on level 15 needs 5
* The one on level 18 needs 10
* The one on level 20 needs 20
