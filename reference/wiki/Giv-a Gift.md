# Giv-a Gift

A Fab-Yule-Us Feast is a quest given by a letter from Otto Soft at the beginning of Winter. It requires the player to give a gift to Giva Joshi as part of a holiday gift exchange. The reward for completion is 1x Strawberry Milkshake.

The first gift you give Giva after receiving this quest will be counted as the gift for the gift exchange.

Some of the dialogue changes if you give Giva one of her favorite foods as a gift. 

## Dialogue
Letter from Otto:

* Greetings, neighbor!
* Every year, we have our traditional secret holiday gift exchange!
* The names have been drawn, and your recipient this year is: Giva Joshi!
* Bring her a gift anytime before the Winter Feast. And be on the lookout! You have a secret gifter too!
* Merrily Yours,
* Mayor Otto Soft

* Tara: How am I supposed to know what kind of present Giva would like?

Quest completion (favorite food):

* Giva: Oh! You’re my secret gift-giver? How charming! Oh, and good on you for not being afraid of a bit of heat! Luckily I made some strawberry milkshakes to cool down after!
* Tara: You make strawberry milkshakes in the winter?
* Giva: It’s my favorite time for frozen treats! You can drink it as slowly as you please and it won’t melt!
* Tara: I never thought of that.
* Giva: They’re no mango lassis, but they’re not bad! And can you believe Kai was able to find me some strawberries in Winter? He’s a wizard, I tell you.
* Tara: What? Who’s a witch?
* Giva: Oh, I didn’t mean literally, what with all the… unrest in town, I wasn’t accusing him of anything! Well, I must get back to work. I’m cooking up a special new project, it’s terribly exciting!
* Tara: Hmmm… a new project? I wonder what she’s up to… 
* [Reward: 1x Strawberry Milkshake]
