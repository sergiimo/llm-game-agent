# Give a Ranch Hand

[Quest]
  title: Give a Ranch Hand
  type: To-Do Quests
  start: Otto Soft
  end: Parker Johnson
  previous: ?
  next: All Cooped Up

Give a Ranch Hand is a quest given by Otto Soft. It requires you to deliver 1000 coins, 80x Wood, 50x Stone, and 10x Iron Ingot to Parker Johnson so he can rebuild the ranch. The reward for completion is 500 coins, delivered to your mailbox after the items are given to Parker.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Otto: Hello! How are things on your farm? I’ve been thinking, perhaps you would like to try your hand at raising livestock?
* Tara: Like, cute sheep and stuff? That would be so much fun!
* Otto: Great! All we have to do is rebuild the broken-down ranch by Thomas’s farm.
* Tara: Wait, what?
* Otto: The previous rancher sourced all of the livestock for farms on the island. If you find the materials to rebuild the ranch, I can ask Parker to fix it up… … and we can flip it to a new rancher who has inquired about moving here! Would make us a tidy little profit, and you could then keep chickens, cows, and suchlike!
* Tara: Okay, I’ll help!
* Otto: Wonderful! Just bring the materials to Parker when they’re ready.
* [Receive Ranch blueprint]

Letter received after paying Parker:

* Tara,
* I just sold the ranch! Here’s your share of the sale!
* The new rancher will arrive tomorrow. Go welcome him, won’t you?
* Cordially,
* Mayor Otto Soft

* [Reward: 500 coins]
Quest completion:

* Marty: Whoa! This ranch is incredible! I’m Martin Ma – Emerson. Pleased to meet you.
* Tara: Nice to meet you, um, Mr. Memerson.
* Marty: Please. Just call me Marty. I want you to know that the ranch you helped rebuild is in good hands.
* Tara: I didn’t really…
* Marty: I’ve graduated with honors from the top national course in animal husbandry! Is that… can we still call it that? I mean, you’re a farmer too, and you’re a girl! Maybe it should be animal spousedry.
* Tara: I don’t think…
* Marty: This is already more complicated than I thought!
* Tara: Is it, though?
* Marty: Maybe we should start by keeping things simple. For now, you can buy eggs from me. [Gives 6x egg.] When you’re ready to raise your own chickens, I can sell you some chicks, too. You’ll want to build a coop to keep them from scampering off. Learned that one the hard way! That nice carpenter fella, Parker can help you out.
* [Receive Chicken Coop blueprint]
