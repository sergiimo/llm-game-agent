# Glam-more

Glam-more is a quest given by the High Priestess during the Fall after completing the quest Living on the Hedge. It requires you to venture into the Gloaming, plant 2x Anemone Seeds, and harvest 8x Anemone Flower to boost the coven's glamour spell. The reward for completion is 3x Restore Magic Potion.

Beginning this quest unlocks the ability to buy Anemone Seeds, Moly Seeds, and Magic Bean Seeds from Peri's Apothecary.

Crops in the Gloaming must be watered every day. It does not rain in the Gloaming even if it is raining in Fairhaven.
