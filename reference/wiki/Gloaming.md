# Gloaming

The Gloaming is a location that can be reached by magic.
Spoilers
|  |
The Gloaming is a magical realm that can be reached after repairing the standing stones in the woods.

It does not have any inhabitants but is full of magical plants and spiderwebs. To move around the place, magic must be used against the writhing vines and magic barriers and a spirit flame potion is needed to get past the spiderwebs. In the Gloaming, you can find a wellspring, a watering well and several flowerbeds that can be used to grow magical plants. 

The Gloaming is also where Echo Blossoms can be found, which contain messages from Violet's mother and reveal how she vanished.
