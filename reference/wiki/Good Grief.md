# Good Grief

Good Grief is a quest given by Vanessa Soft after you unlock the identity of the Acolyte in the quest Charmed, I'm Sure. It requires you to cook 3x Berry Smoothie and attend a grief group at the Consciousness Center after 7pm on any day. The reward for completion is 1x Apple Pie and 3x Caramel Popcorn.

## Dialogue
Letter from Vanessa:

* Dear Tara,
* I wanted to invite you to the grief support group that I run. Violet, Sebastian, and Juliet come sometimes, too. We’d all love to see you at the next meeting. It’s very casual.
* Just bring something to eat or drink, and let me know when you’re ready.
* Sincerely,
* Vanessa Soft

* Tara: That’s sweet. What should I bring? Maybe berry smoothies? 

Attend the grief group at the Consciousness Center any night after 7pm.

* Vanessa: Tara! Come in, come in. That smoothie looks delicious! Have some of the pie that Juliet brought. And popcorn from Sebastian. Violet! Welcome!
* Violet: Thanks. Hey, bud.
* Vanessa: As I was saying. Sometimes it can be helpful for us to confront the strength of our emotions. It makes us realize what actions we want to take. And what those actions are…
