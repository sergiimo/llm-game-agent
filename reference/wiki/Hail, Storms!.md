# Hail, Storms!

Hail, Storms! is a quest given by the High Priestess in the Winter. It requires the player to gather ingredients (1x Essence of Hellebore, 1x Amethyst, 1x Pearl Dust) to upgrade to an Amethyst Wand. The reward for completion is an Increase Magic spell, which permanently increases the size of the magic bar.

The player is required to craft and use 1x Summon Storm Spell on the beach by the lighthouse to get a Pearl, regardless of whether they already have any pearls in their inventory. Pearl Dust can then be made using the Mortar and Pestle.

Essence of Hellebore can be obtained by speaking to Peri.
