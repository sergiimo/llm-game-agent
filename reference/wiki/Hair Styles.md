# Hair Styles

Hair Styles can be purchased from Coiffed & Crowned by Eury using coins, hair color, and pomade. The hair salon is unlocked in Summer Y2. Once a hairstyle is purchased, you can select it at any time via the hair salon or your inventory.

Hair color and pomade can be crafted at the Mortar and Pestle in your sanctum.;
