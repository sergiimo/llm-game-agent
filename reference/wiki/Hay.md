# Hay

[Item]
  itemName: Hay
  source: The Hayseed Homestead
  buyPrice: 5 coins
  description: Delicious, nutritious food for cows and sheep. Buy it from Marty.
  other: ==Notes==
Cows, sheep, pigs, and alpaca each eat 1 hay per day and will fall ill if feedings are missed for multiple days. Until building the Hay Field on your own farm, hay must be purchased periodically from Marty. The hay field does not produce hay as a item, but does take care of feeding all of your barn animals without it!
