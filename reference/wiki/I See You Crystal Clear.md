# I See You Crystal Clear

I See You Crystal Clear is a quest given by the Farseer that teaches you how to use the Crystal Ball to discover the favorite foods of villagers. It requires you to build a Crystal Ball, brew 1x Crystal Ball Potion, and deliver him his favorite food. 

If you already discovered Fish Stew as one of his favorite foods and the Crystal Ball reveals a different meal, the quest still requires you to deliver him Fish Stew. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: So, you’re the Farseer. That means you can see the future?
* Farseer Shelby: When the need arises. But there’s plenty to see in the here and now if ya just pay attention.
* Tara: You sound just like my gramma. I miss her so much. I want to do what she said and make things better between the coven and the town, but I don’t really know where to start.
* Farseer Shelby: Ah, that’s simple enough. Ya just gotta find a way into their hearts.
* Tara: Simple as that, huh?
* Farseer Shelby: Here. Craft this crystal ball in yer sanctum. Once you’ve created it, you’ll be able to see the heart’s desire of any person on this island. You’ll need this crystal ball potion anytime ya want to use yer crystal ball. Come see me when it’s ready.
* [Receive Crystal Ball Potion recipe, Crystal Ball blueprint]

Part 1: Use the crystal ball to discover the Farseer’s favorite food.

* Tara: Hmm, I think I see something…

Part 2: Make a fish stew for the Farseer.

Part 3:  Take the fish stew to the Farseer.

* Tara: Here ya go. One fish stew.
* Farseer Shelby: Well that smells just fine. I thank ya. Here’s some more of that crystal ball potion. You’ll need it.
* [Reward: 3x Crystal Ball Potion]
