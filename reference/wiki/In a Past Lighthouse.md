# In a Past Lighthouse

[Quest]
  title: In a Past Lighthouse
  type: To-Do Quest
  start: Gloria; Mayor's Office
  item_needs: 1x Beacon Bulb; 750x Wood; 500x Stone; 250x Iron Ingot
  rewards: Spellbound Witch Outfit; Spellbound Witch Hat
  end: Peri; Lighthouse
  previous: A Need for Steed

## Description
Supply Parker with the lighthouse building supplies.

## Dialog
Gloria: I'm worried about Bruno

Gloria: Sebastian told me Bruno's ship has a hole from hitting a rock on his way back last night.

Gloria: It's clearly dangerous for him to navigate his way home in the dark.

Gloria: I've had Parker draw up a new plan to restore the lighthouse.

Gloria: Could you work with him on this?

Tara: Absolutely!

___

Parker: Tara, ready to get started on that lighthouse?

Tara: Helping Bruno and beautifying my blueberry picking spot all at once? Lets do it.

Parker: Heh, perfect. I'll need some supplies to start -- most important is the beacon bulb. You can make that in your glass kiln.

Parker: Let me know when you've got everything ready to go.

### Quest Completion
Tara: Here are the materials you asked for, Parker.

Parker: I knew you'd deliver. Don't forget to talk to Gloria and get paid for your hard work.

Tara: I won't.

### Reward
Gloria: Thank you for helping Parker with the lighthouse, Tara.

Gloria: It'll go a long way for helping look out for our community.

Tara: I'm glad I could help. I wish I has thought to do it earlier myself.

### Finale
Tara: Huh. I wonder if Parker noticed this old cupboard.

Peri: Funny running into you here, witchling. It seems you've found what my dreams sent me to find.

Tara: You should be the one to open this, then. If you want.

Peri: I do. It's time to face my past.

Peri: First, will you do me the honor of restoring the memories I've chosen to give up?

Tara: I'd be honored.

Tara: Memories that have faded from view, this spell shall render them back to you!

Peri: Oh... my.

Tara: You OK?

Peri: I will be.

Echo Peri: The warlock forced me to destroy the lighthouse beacon so we could hide our existence from an approaching ship.

Echo Peri: So many innocents were taken because of what I did. A friend helped free me from his chains, and we banished him from the island.

Echo Peri: And now that I am a free fae, I'm choosing to forget.

Echo Peri: I don't want to be burdened by this guilt and that man any longer.

Tara: I'm sorry you went through that, Peri. It wasn't your fault, though.

Peri: I know that now. Memories were such a burden to me, but I've learned that they can be gifts, too.

Peri: Thank you, Tara. You are a Wylde to remember.
