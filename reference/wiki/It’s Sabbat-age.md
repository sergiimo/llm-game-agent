# It’s Sabbat-age

It’s Sabbat-age is a quest given by the High Priestess in the Winter, after the Winter Feast. It requires the player to deliver 5x Copper Ingot and 5x Gold Ingot to Zephyr so she can build a scrambling device to break Giva's drone. The reward for completion is 5x Boost Harvest Potion.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Farseer Shelby: We any closer to sorting out who shattered our gate into pieces, High Priestess?
* High Priestess Lina. No. But whoever did it left the cauldron and altar unbothered. The glamour must still be holding.
* Tara: But who outside of Ravenwood Hollow would have the power to break the gate in the first place?
* Herbalist Violet: Um… I hate to bring this up, but it’s not just magical intruders we need to worry about. Giva has a new drone that she has been testing, in order to release something called dropsondes into the atmosphere.
* Tara: That must be the secret new project she was talking about!
* Herbalist Violet: The good news is she’s not very good at piloting it yet! I only know she has it because she crashed it by my house.
* High Priestess Lina: That’s bad news, actually. If she were to crash it by our circle she would come in after it. We can’t risk it. Novice, you must find a way to sabotage this new drone so it won’t work.
* --[Choice: It could help]
** Tara: But what if it could help us learn about the island's weather?
**High Priestess Lina: Our coven alone is tasked with changing the seasons. We've never failed in our duty. 
* --[Choice: Is there another way?]
** Tara: Can't we place a magical barrier to keep the drone out?
**High Priestess Lina: Magical barriers would not work against a mechanical device of human invention. We simply can't allow it to come near.
* --[Choice: Okay]
** Tara: Okay, I’ll do it.
** High Priestess Lina: It’s for the best.
* High Priestess Lina: You will need Zephyr’s assistance. I’m sure she can fashion something that will prevent the drone from working.

Speak to Zephyr to find out how to stop Giva’s drone from revealing everything.

* Tara: Hiya, Zephyr.
* Zephyr: I know why you’re here. The High Priestess told me you have a nosy mortal problem. I can make a scramblin’ device so that any data the drone picks up will be useless. Just need ya to get me some copper so I can make some wire to redirect the transmitter, and some gold for the connector pins.
* Tara: Lots of metal, got it.

Gather copper ingots and gold ingots so Zephyr can sabotage Giva’s drone. 

* Zephyr: Ya got that copper and gold yet?
* Tara: Yes. Now what?
* Zephyr: I’ll make your scrambler, that’s what. Don’t worry, I’ll pop into Giva’s shop when she’s not there and stick it on the drone myself.

Wait for Zephyr’s device to destroy the drone.

* Tara: Hi Giva. What's new?
* Giva: Well, my drone was new, but now whenever I send it up the data it sends back is just rubbish! I must have broken something when I crashed it over by Violet's house. I'm hopeless with new technology, I suppose. It's so disappointing. These dropsondes could have really helped unravel the mystery of this island's bizarre weather.

Speak to the High Priestess and let her know the job is done.

* Tara: I gave Zephyr what she needed for the scrambling device.
* High Priestess Lina: You did well.
* Tara: You realize you’re making me lie to someone I really care about, right?
* High Priestess Lina: I do that every night, Novice. I know it’s not easy, but it’s for everyone’s safety.
* [Reward: 5x Boost Harvest potion]
