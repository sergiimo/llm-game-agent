# I’m Muffin Without You

I’m Muffin Without You is a romance quest given by Giva Joshi after you gain six full hearts with her. It requires you to deliver 3x Muffin to her. After delivering the muffins, she invites you to meet her at the mountain lookout between 3 and 5pm the next day, where she provides raspberry orange herbal tea and shows off a radiosonde balloon she set up to rain down flower petals as a romantic gesture. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Giva: Dear one, I have a surprise for you! But I’ll need some muffins to pull this off. Can you bring me three?

Giva has requested muffins as part of a surprise! 

* Tara: You requested muffins, milady meteorologist?
* Giva: Oh, these are perfect! I’ll need a night to work on my surprise. Meet me by the overlook tomorrow after 3pm!

Meet Giva at the mountain lookout between 3pm and 5pm tomorrow.

* Giva: You came! Here’s some raspberry orange herbal tea that I’ve been saving and your lovely muffins. And as the piece de resistance – one of my radiosonde balloons. Count down from ten…
* Tara: Ten, nine, eight, seven, six, five, four, three, two, one… [Giva releases the balloon, it floats away while raining flower petals] … woah!
* Giva: I used so many balloons testing my theory that the air current up here would catch the petals. But it was all worth it.
* Tara: I can’t believe you did all this for me!
* Giva: I care about you so much, Tara. And that’s unusual for me. My only serious girlfriend, other than you, was back in boarding school. I couldn’t put on spectacles of affection like this for Alice.
* Tara: Why not?
* Giva: The school was quite conservative. That’s English boarding school culture for you. I couldn’t run the risk of administration telling my parents. But nobody in Fairhaven gives a fig about who I love, as long as I’m happy! I can express my feelings however I want! So I will! I love you, Tara Wylde!
* Tara: I love you too, Giva. Give me your cup? I’ll pour you some tea…
* Giva: And there it is, proof that you love me.
