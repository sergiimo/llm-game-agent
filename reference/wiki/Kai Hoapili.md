  CharacterDescription: Kai Hoapili (27) is a Hawaiian man who runs the pop-up novelties stall in the town square, Kai's Treasures. When he's not working, he's surfing, or just relaxing. He lives an easy-come, easy-go life, which has led to a maxed-out tab at Damon's bar. As a child, Kai had debilitating asthma and couldn't participate in many sports.

He was determined to become a surfer anyway, and eventually went pro. Kai ended up in Fairhaven after a surfing visit left him injured. While he recovered under Amira's care, he decided to stay and run the pop-up stall.

But Fairhaven is remote, and sometimes Kai feels cut off from the rest of the surfing world.

He's considering going back to surfing full-time, but he also wants to stay in Fairhaven and spend time with you. (Only if in a relationship with Kai.)
