"Exotic goods from across the bay."; 
Kai's Treasures is a stall run by Kai Hoapili, located in the center of the town square next to Dahl General Store. It stocks a variety of treasures ranging from fruit trees to spices; villager dialogue frequently mentions that Kai gets his treasures from off-island, presumably by surfboard!
