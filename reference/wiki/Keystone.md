# Keystone

Keystones are circular rocks with symbols on them that are used at the standing stones in order to travel into Gloaming. There are three keystones in total, each one leading to a different part of the Gloaming. 

The first keystone is acquired from the display case in Town Hall during the quest At the Break-in Point.

The second keystone is found behind a magical barrier in the first section of the Gloaming.

The third keystone is obtained along with the Adder Stone.
