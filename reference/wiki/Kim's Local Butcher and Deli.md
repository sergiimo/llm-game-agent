"Humane butcher services available upon request"; 
Kim's Local Butcher and Deli is a small shop run by Kim Izumi. They buy and sell meat products, and will buy adult animals in exchange for coins and resources. Kim pays more than Marty when buying chickens, and a bit less than Marty when buying cows or sheep.
