# Know Witch Way the Wind Blows

Know Witch Way the Wind Blows is a quest that begins during the questline of Wraithing in Anticipation, after the Town Hall meeting where Sophia Moretti is accused of witchcraft by a wraith-possessed Natalia Kuznetsova.  It serves as a 7-day countdown timer until the date of the trial.  

You need the materials for Natalia's Wraith Banishment.
