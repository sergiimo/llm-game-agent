# League of the Conscious Mind

  map: between Dr. Syed's General Practice and the Mayor's Mansion
  location: Town Center
  imagecaption: Meetings are from 10-12 on Sundays and we welcome all visitors!
The consciousness center is a large grey masonry building with white trim and large picture windows all around.

When you enter the building, the first thing you see is a large picture window, the dais with an alter-like table, and two rows of chairs for believers to sit in during service.

To the left side you will see Cameron's office which includes a globe.

Small group meetings, such as the grief group, are held on the right side of the center where there is a circle of chairs for participants and a piano.
