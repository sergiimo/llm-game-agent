# Letter Know You Love Her

Letter Know You Love Her is a quest given by Damon Mthembu-Haas. It requires you to deliver5x Paper to him. The reward for completion is 3x Mojito. This quest is also Damon's 4-heart event. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Damon: Ag! This isn’t right! Hey Tara! I’m writing a letter to my ma, but I keep messing up and now I’m out of paper. Have any?
* Tara: I think I can get you some. 

Quest completion:

* Tara: Here’s your paper, Damon. So, what are you writing your mom about?
* Damon: Thanks! Well, I don’t know. I might be telling her about a new friend who’s become kind of special to me.
