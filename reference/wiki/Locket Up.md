# Locket Up

Locket Up is a quest that begins immediately after completing the quest Witch Way Did She Go and the final quest of the main storyline. It requires the player to search the town for Vanessa and banish the wraith possessing her. 

To obtain the items necessary for the spell, the player must speak to Otto where he is sitting on a bench by the eastern staircase to get Otto's Wedding Ring, and to Sebastian by the western staircase to get the Key to the Fairhaven School, where Vanessa's Gloves can be found on the teacher's desk. 

Other necessary items can be obtained by talking to other villagers, since the player cannot leave the area while Shelby's Freeze Time incantation is active:

* 1x Egg (Marty)
* 2x Parchment (Cameron)
* 1x Fennel Powder (Amira)
* 1x Paper (Juliet)
* 1x Squid (Bruno)

The reward for completion is an Increase Magic Spell.
