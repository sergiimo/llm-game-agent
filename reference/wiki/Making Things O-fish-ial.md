# Making Things O-fish-ial

Making Things O-fish-ial is a romance quest given by Westley Vuk after you gain six full hearts with him. It requires you to deliver 1x Salmon. After delivering the salmon, he asks you to meet him at the beach the next morning, where he tells a story, professes his love, and gives you the book of Russian fairy tales he spoke about in his four-heart event.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Westley: Sweetheart, if you have a moment, could you catch me some salmon? I want to make something special.
* Tara: Well, you never know what’s going to bite, but I’ll try!

Westley has asked for some fresh salmon for a surprise. 

* Westley: Is that fresh salmon? Perfect! Tomorrow morning, would you take a walk with me down by the beach? I’ll have your surprise ready then.

Wait till tomorrow for Westley to reveal his surprise at the beach.

* Tara: All right, what’s your scheme, honey?
* Westley: I made you roast salmon the way my nana taught me! She used to tell me stories while she cooked. Do you want to hear one?
* Tara: Of course I do!
* Westley: Once upon a time, a man found a falcon’s feather and gave it to his youngest daughter, Alenushka. That night, an enormous falcon landed on Alenushka’s window. But when it saw her, the falcon turned into a man – Finist. Finist and Alenushka fell in love, but Alenushka’s jealous sisters put knives on her window. When Finist landed, he cried out and flew all the way back to the magical land where he lived. Alenushka went on a long journey to Finist’s land, where she finally found him and told him she hadn’t been the one to hurt him.
* Tara: And then what? Did they live in Alenushka’s country or Finist’s?
* Westley: None of the stories say. But I still like it. Nobody judges the fair maiden for loving someone who turns into an animal. And I want us to be able to make a life together, even if it’s hard, because I love you. This is that collection of Russian fairy tales I told you about. I want you to have it. [Gives Book of Russian Fairy Tales]
* Tara: Oh, Westley, thank you. I love you, too. And for the record, I prefer werewolves to werefalcons.
