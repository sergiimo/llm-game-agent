  CharacterDescription: Marty is the new ranch owner in town and has also moved to Fairhaven from the city. His self-confidence and excitement at starting a ranch seem to outweigh his actual ranching skills. 

Most of the townspeople are suspicious of his intentions, and his interactions with Tara suggest he’s hiding something. Although Mayor Soft handpicked Marty to run the ranch, they rarely see eye to eye and Marty ends up running for Mayor in the Fairhaven election. 

Marty raises and sells livestock which the player can purchase for their farm after building the chicken coop or barn.
  HistoryDescription: Not much is known about Marty’s life before moving to Fairhaven. He mentions he was a businessman but hasn't said what kind of business he did - something about consulting?  

Upon his first introduction to Tara, he’s about to use a last name starting with 'Ma-', before using Emerson, suggesting that he may have gotten into trouble in the city and is starting over in Fairhaven.
  VarPersonal: Visits Angus's bakery and visits Sophia's diner when Ranch is closed.

He is on his Ranch when the Ranch is open.

Sometimes can be found in the forest.
