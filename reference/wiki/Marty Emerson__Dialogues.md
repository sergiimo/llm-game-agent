# Marty Emerson/Dialogues

CharacterNav
This page lists the dialogue lines for Marty Emerson.

| Spring |
| Marty: Whoa! This ranch is incredible! I’m Martin Ma – Emerson. Pleased to meet you. |

Tara: Nice to meet you, um, Mr. Memerson.

Marty: Please. Just call me Marty. I want you to know that the ranch you helped rebuild is in good hands.

Tara: I didn’t really…

Marty: I’ve graduated with honors from the top national course in animal husbandry! Is that… can we still call it that? I mean, you’re a farmer too, and you’re a girl! Maybe it should be animal spousedry.

Tara: I don’t think…

Marty: This is already more complicated than I thought!

Tara: Is it, though?

Marty: Maybe we should start by keeping things simple. For now, you can buy eggs from me. [Gives 6x egg.] When you’re ready to raise your own chickens, I can sell you some chicks, too. You’ll want to build a coop to keep them from scampering off. Learned that one the hard way! That nice carpenter fella, Parker can help you out. 
| Otto tells me you're also new to Fairhaven. Did the get-back-to-the-land bug bite you, too? |
Tara: Well… not like it bit you. I mostly came to take care of my grandmother. 

Marty: Hazel, right? I've heard she's a real spitfire.
| Say, the Wylde farm is on a gorgeous plot of land! How long's it been in the family? |
Tara: We've had it for… huh, I'm just realizing I don't know. Long story, but there was a family falling-out. I'm still learning a ton about this place. 

Marty: Well, that farmhouse is a stunner. Great bones. 

Tara: ... Thanks?
| Tara: So what'd you do before you switched up careers? |
Marty: I was, um, a consultant. Did a lot of consulting. And then I got hit with that consultant burnout! You know how it goes.
| Hey, make sure to follow my PicstaTwit account! I post animal photos every day. |
Tara: Gonna become a PicstaTwit influencer, huh? 

Marty: N-no! I'm just trying to share my love for my cute little buddies.
| Tara: Do you have any family back in the city, Marty? |
Marty: Not anymore. I used to work with my dad, but he moved. We're… not really in touch these days. 

Tara: I'm really sorry. That was how it was for Hazel and my dad, for a long time.
| Did you know that cows can eat up to twenty-four pounds of hay a day? Ah, nature. Miraculous. And expensive. |
| What the coursebooks don't tell you is that bags of feed are heavy. We should've had to do push-ups as part of our degree requirements. Then I'd have built some muscle mass, at least! |
| Parker came by the other day to see if I needed directions to his workshop. What a nice guy! I wish he was my neighbor instead of Thomas… |

| Summer |
| What a little pastoral delight to see everyone preparing for the Summer Festival! Fortunately, since I'm so new, Otto said I didn't have to contribute. |
Tara: Wait, what?!
| Real nice weather, huh? I should snap a pic, text it to Dad. You don't have to run off to The Bahamas just to get some sunshine… |
Tara: What? 

Marty: J-just talking to myself!
| I've already gotten a hundred more followers on my social accounts since I moved here! |
Tara: Wow, that's… great? 

Marty: People love photos of the authentic country life! But let me tell you, these chickens do NOT cooperate when I'm trying to compose a shot.
| Chickens can't blow away in the wind, right? |
Tara: I hope not. I would hate to get smacked in the face by an errant chicken.
| Giva is really something, isn't she? I wonder if she'd like the have drinks at Damon's some night? |
| Parker and Lina's kids have real entrepreneurial spirit! Of course, they're spirited in general. Sometimes… too much. |
| How serious are tick bites? I've kinda got this rash… But I get rashes all the time. Sorry, TMI? |
| Do you think I could take some of the chicks into the onsen for a photo op? |
Tara: Absolutely not.
| I asked Giva if she'd get drinks with me. And she said she didn't drink. So I asked if she wanted to get coffee instead. And she said she didn't drink coffee. |
Tara: Don't tell me you didn't get the hint! 

Marty: Well… then I asked if she wanted to get tea. And she said she loved tea, but not men. So that's that.
| My cattle corral's been a little dusty because of the Summer heat… … and because of the rain, it's turned into a swamp! I could paddle a canoe across this thing! |
| Oooh, I love Summer Festivals! Pics of bonfires always blow up on social! |
| I think my neighbor may be the one who keeps trying to "liberate" my cows. |
Tara: Who, Thomas? 

Marty: Yeah. I know they're not just escaping my fences on their own. They're the best fences, according to "Modern Farmer Magazine!" 

Tara: Have you tried talking to him? 

Marty: Huh... I guess I could give it a try.
| So, are you staying on now? If you're selling the farm, I might know an interested buyer. |
Tara: …. 

Marty: Too soon? 

Tara: Yep, I would say so. 
| Cattle get dehydrated so fast in this heat. Around midday, I've been turning on a bunch of fans in the barn, just to get the air circulating. I don't want to think about my electricity bill! |
| Summer is the worst. There are so many mosquitos, and they all live inside my house. |

| Fall |
| Tara: I didn't realize you had political ambitions. |
Marty: I didn't either, until I moved here! I guess I'm just an ideas guy, what can I say? 

Tara: Must be all that time consulting. 

Marty: What? Oh. Yes.
| Tara: If you win the election, will you move out of the ranch? |
Marty: Isn't it tradition for the mayor to live in the mayor's mansion? 

Tara: Yes, but the animals… 

Marty: … will come with me! We'll figure it out.
| People seem excited about the idea of shaking up the Town Hall status quo! Maybe I have a chance of winning this after all. |
| Otto was the one who invited you to move into the ranch, right? How'd he take your campaign announcement? Marty: Let's just say he very much would have preferred that I not run. |
| Since the days are getting shorter, I had to add a lamp to the chicken coop. Otherwise, the hens don't realize it's morning and they should get out of bed! |
| I keep trying to lure that black cat into my barn. I could use a good mouser. But it turns up its nose at fresh milk! What's it going to take? Filet mignon? |
Tara: Good luck. That cat's not really a mouser.
| Have you ever heard what I can only describe as 'blood-chilling screams' late at night? I hope I'm dreaming. Otherwise… maybe there really is something going on here. |
Tara: Uh… try not to worry about it too much. 

Marty: Blood! Chilling! Screams!
| Otto just can't stop talking about that Neighborhood Watch of his. How do I know he's not telling his people to watch me, hmm? |
| I don't know Francis and Angus that well yet, but they seem like a perfect couple. Too bad I'll never find a love like theirs! Joking, joking. Sort of. |
| That wedding sure was refreshing! The last wedding I went to was my dad's. He was marrying his fifth wife. They had a champagne luge. It was tacky. |
Tara: Yeah, that's… very… special.
| I can't believe it. If the two people who voted for Lina had voted for me instead, I would be mayor! I mean, no offense to Lina, but she doesn't seem like she wants to be in control of anything except her store. |
| I can't believe Natalia's tossing around accusations of witchcraft. And don't think I didn't notice that she's part of Otto's Friendly Neighborhood Watch! |
| What's so bad about going into the woods, anyway? Would Thoreau ever have written Walden if he hadn't been able to go into the woods? |
| What's a Malcontent, anyway? The term seems awfully broad. I'm not sure I like that. |
| I can't believe anyone bought into that Malcontent stuff in the first place. Anyone can see that League of the Conscious Mind is a grift. And I recognize a grift after all my years of… consulting. |
| Why didn't Natalia accuse Shelby of being a witch? He's the weird guy who actually lives out in the woods. |
Tara: I thought you wanted to be one with nature, too? 

Marty: Uh… by 'weird' I meant 'commendable.'

| Winter |
| So… does your town usually have witch trials? Is that like, a thing they do in the countryside? |
Tara: No, um, I don't think there's ever been one here before. At least not for a long time. 

Marty: I see. It's just something that started once I moved here. Great.
| I offered to help Sophia find a buyer for her place, if she decides she wants to move. I still have some real estate contacts back in the city. If you… ever wanted to sell your farm, I could help you with that too. |
Tara: I'm not moving anywhere, Marty. 

Marty: Oh. Okay. Sure.
| The Gentleman Farmer Quarterly says livestock need feed when it's cold to keep their body temperatures up. Ugh, I can't deal with another cow getting sick. |
| So, now we're trapped on the island. I didn't consider that was a thing that could happen when I first moved here, did you? |
| Did you get your secret gift yet? Mine was from Cameron. You'll never guess what he gave me. |
Tara: A copy of The Book of the Mind? 

Marty: Ding ding ding! Can't complain. The pages shred up nicely for my nest boxes. 

Tara: Marty!  

Marty: What? The hens need to keep warm this winter somehow.
| Have you tried my caramel milk? It's like a caramel latte, but there's no coffee. Sophia makes the only decent espresso on this island, so… |
| I just don't get it? What did I ever do to Angus? I mean, okay, maybe I forgot to get him and Francis a wedding present, but I hadn't really lived here that long, you know? |
| Now I see why my father always lives close to a private airstrip. Sometimes you gotta be able to make a quick exit. |
Tara: What? 

Marty: Oh, nothing…
| Saw your new greenhouse. [Whistles] That's nice! Might ask Parker to build me one. |
Tara: But… you don't plant crops, Marty. 

Marty: Yeah, but it would just look more authentic, you know? I want my ranch to be as on-brand as possible.
| I may have only made caramel milk for the feast… but really, Kim couldn't have made their roast if I hadn't moved to the island. So when you think about it, I'm the one who made the Winter Feast possible. |
Tara: Yes, that was very… generous of you. 

Marty: I do what I can.
| Tara: Um… congrats on your acquittal, Marty. |
Marty: Hmmph. Like I'm supposed to feel better? If Angus hadn't recanted, who knows what might have happened?
| Tara: So… you were trying to buy out Thomas's farm, and mine too, huh? Did you really think you would be able to get ALL of us to leave the island? |
Marty: Kinda! Especially once these witch trials started. I mean, I didn't plan for that to happen, but it seemed like this place might clear out quickly once they got going! 

Tara: That's really messed up. 

Marty: Look, the creditors were gonna take Maplethorpe Tower. You came back here to help save your grandmother's farm, right? Well, I was trying to save the last building that still bears the Maplethorpe name. I'm not proud of what I did... what I tried to do. I was desperate. And I'm sorry.
| I don't mind the cold. Frozen poop makes less of a mess when you step in it, at least. |
Tara: Way to see the bright side there, Marty.
| I see what's going on here. With the exception of Sophia, this seems to be a last hired, first fired situation, as Dad used to say. You better watch your back, Tara. You were the last one to move here before me, right? |
| Tara: Any luck with your wolf search, Marty? |
Marty: No, but the howling stopped! Who knows, maybe it was a trick of the wind in the first place!
| As Thoreau said, "Live in each season as it passes." But what if the season is, you know… really cold? |
| My dad always said it wasn't really Winter without a big wreath on the door. He didn't care about a lot of Danish traditions, but he liked that one. |
| Cameron really seemed upset at Giva's trial. I guess he didn't expect his Malcontent nonsense to ensnare one of his friends. Maybe now he'll stop. |
