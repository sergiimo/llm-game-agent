# Mine

DISPLAYTITLE:The Mine

The mine entrance next to Tara's farm can be safely reconstructed by Parker early in the game. Once accessible, Tara can mine through twenty levels, obtaining different ores and gems on each. Each level, except levels 14 and 20, requires mining ore nodes to find the key to the next level. For level 14, a "Deep Mine" key is needed to go any further, obtained from a Coven quest. All levels contain clay patches and stones as well. All gem nodes can produce a gem and/or coal or stone.

## Quests
### Don't Take Mines for Granite
Collect wood and stone so that Parker can repair the mine on your farm.

### Oh, the Iron-y
If you bring Natalia some iron ore from the mine, she’ll smelt it into an ingot.

### A Questionable Supply Chain
The mayor needs you to bring him some wood, stone, and iron ingots.

### This Rock is Mine
Look for artifacts in the bottom levels of the mine and take them to Giva.

### Get a Lode of This
Giva wants a sample from the lowest levels of the mine, but the coven members may have opinions on that...

### A Natural Alloy
Francis has requested silver ingots that he can turn into wedding bands.

### Pot, Meet Kettle
Bring Natalia copper so she can get to work on the soup kettle.

### Tying the Knot, Fairhaven Style
Get iron ingots and gold ingots for Natalia to craft your wedding ring.
