# Mountain

The Mountain area is initially inaccessible until the connecting bridge is upgraded during the Summer season. It hosts a variety of forgeable goods, including respawning trees that can be chopped for wood. It also has the unique feature of periodically-spawning dig spots that can reveal a small amount of coins or a new Cooking recipe!

## Broken Bridge
There's a note when you select the bridge before it's fixed.; 
Danger! This bridge has been declared unfit for use. The path to the mountain is off-limits. Parker

## Connections
* Farm (bottom left; exits to northwest)

## Residents
* Giva Joshi

## Businesses
There are no traditional businesses in the Mountain area, but Giva can be found during work hours in her Weather Tower (up at the top of the mountain to the east) and, after completing a quest for Kim, Tara can visit the Onsen in the lower left of the area once a day to restore a portion of her energy -- for a price!
