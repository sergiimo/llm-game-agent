# Musician in Treble

Musician in Treble is a quest given by Juliet Moretti during the Fall. It requires you to locate and return her lost sheet music by scrying for it. The reward for completion is 3x Black Ink.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: You look upset. Is something wrong?
* Juliet: I lost my sheet music! It’s a disaster… Francis asked me to play a song for his wedding, and I have to practice!
* Tara: When was the last time you saw it?
* Juliet: I practiced when I was babysitting the twins, after they’d gone to sleep. Maybe Emmi and Finn know something? If you see the sheet music, will you tell me?
* Tara: You better believe I will become the best sheet music detective.
* Juliet: Um. Okay.

Part 1: Juliet’s sheet music is missing! Found out if Emmi and Finn know anything. 

* Tara: What’s black, white, and lets you play cool tunes?
* Finn: Uhhhhhh…
* Tara: Sheet music! Juliet’s! Ring a bell?
* Finn: N-no?
* Tara: Finn. Be honest.
* Finn: Wahhh! I didn’t take it! Emmi did! I don’t even know how I know that. I didn’t see her do it. And she’ll be mad if she finds out I told!
* Tara: Don’t worry. You did the right thing.

* Tara: If I asked you about Juliet’s sheet music, you would say…
* Emmi: …
* Tara: What does that silence mean?
* Emmi: That I have nothing to say!
* Tara: Are you sure it didn’t mysteriously end up with your stuff?
* Emmi: That’s silly!
* Tara: Emmi…
* Emmi: Silly!!

* Tara: I might have to step up my game. Maybe the coven knows of a magic solution to my problem.

Part 2: Speak to the Journeyman to see if he knows how to find lost (or stolen) objects.

* Tara: Hey, Journeyman. You wouldn’t happen to know a spell to help me find something that’s missing would you?
* Journeyman Damon: I do happen to know what you’re looking for to… find what… you’re looking for. Heh. You need to learn some scrying!
* Tara: Which is… what?
* Journeyman Damon: It’s basically looking into a magic mirror and asking it to find stuff for you. It works on anything – people, possessions, whatever. Witches usually use pools of water because water has its own magical properties. A basin of water is best so you can more easily contain the spell. Build one of these scrying basins in your Sanctum and then pour this potion into it. Say the name of who or what you’re looking for and it’ll do the rest!
* [Receive Scrying Bowl blueprint, Scrying Potion recipe]

Part 3: Build a scrying bowl in your sanctum and use it to find Juliet’s music.

* Tara: Please, scrying bowl, I’m looking for Juliet’s music.
* Emmi: No! It’s mine! It’s mine!
* Tara: I’m pretty sure it’s Juliet’s.
* Emmi: She left it in our house. Finders keepers! Anyway, I’ll give it back once I figure out how it works…
* Tara: Huh?
* Emmi: Juliet makes such pretty sounds that come out of the guitar. I want to figure out how she does it.
* --[Choice: Learning an instrument is ok, but…]
** Tara: It’s wonderful that you want to learn how to play an instrument. But this belongs to Juliet.
** Emmi: I know, I know!
* --[Choice: Stealing is wrong.]
** -
* Emmi: Okay. You can give it back.
* Tara: Thank you, Emmi. Juliet will appreciate it.
* Emmi: Wait, how did you figure out it was me?
* Tara: Um… lucky guess.

Part 4: Return the sheet music to Juliet.

* Tara: I have something for you.
* Juliet: You did it! I thought this was gone forever! Where did you find it?
* --[Choice: I found it]
** -
* --[Choice: Emmi]
** Tara: Emmi had it.
** Juliet: That little-! I can’t believe her. This wasn’t funny.
** Tara: She was just trying to figure out how to make music. Don’t go hard on her.
** Juliet: I’ll try not to. But she is so annoying sometimes.
* Juliet: Thank you, Tara. You saved my butt.
* Tara: I’m looking forward to your performance!
* [Reward: 3 Black Ink]
