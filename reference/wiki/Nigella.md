# Nigella

[Forage]
  itemName: Nigella
  location: Mountain Overpass
  season: Summer, Fall
  buyFrom: Peri's Apothecary
  buyPrice: 10 coins
  description: A lacy purple flower that grows in the mountain overpass in Summer and Fall. Useful for magical crafting.

 Tanning Solution and Flower Paper can be made with any foraged flower. Nigella is not a necessary ingredient for either, just a possible ingredient.
