# No Time Like The Present

No Time Like The Present is a quest given by Gramma. It requires you to deliver some Corn Bread to Thomas and serves as a tutorial for giving gifts. The reward for completion is the Corn Bread recipe.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Gramma: Stay a moment, honeybee. How are you finding the town now that you’ve met everyone? I know we’re a bit different than the folks you might have known back in the city.
* Tara: That’s a good thing, actually. I needed to say goodbye to all that. For now, at least.
* Gramma: You haven’t said much about your… fiancé. Do you want to talk about what happened with… I’m sorry, I have such a bad memory for names these days…
* Tara: [Choice of Matt, Claire, or Sam]
* Tara: There really isn’t much to say. I woke up one day with a note next to my pillow saying that we were over. We haven’t spoken since.
* Gramma: What a coward.
* Tara: Gramma!
* Gramma: I’m sorry, but it’s true. My view is too many folks take the easy way out, these days. You deserved better than that, Tara.
* Tara: Um… thanks, Gramma.
* Gramma: On this island, when folks want to break up, they tell you to your face. It’s a bit of a convoluted tradition, actually… but well, we’re putting the cart before the horse, aren’t we? And maybe it’s time for you to get back on the horse! Anyone caught your eye, yet?
* Tara: I’m not sure I’m ready to date again, Gramma.
* Gramma: Come now. You can’t spend all your time doing farm chores. You’ve got to live a little!
* Tara: Really. I’m happy to just make new friends, for now.
* Gramma: Fine, fine, suit yourself. But if you really want to make new friends, a little generosity never hurts! Here, take this and give it to Thomas. You’ll see what I mean!

Giving to Thomas:

* Tara: Here’s some cornbread, Thomas! Gramma made it.
* Thomas: Oh. Thanks for bringing it by. That’s very kind.
* Tara: Sure! You’re welcome!

Quest completion:

* Gramma: Well, did you give Thomas the cornbread?
* Tara: Yes, and he… seemed to like it?
* Gramma: Ha. Thomas is a bit hard to win over, but I think you two will get on fine. And giving him his favorite food will certainly help!
* Tara: So… what you’re saying is I should bribe my way into my neighbors’ hearts.
* Gramma: Exactly! Well, talking to them once in a while helps too! You’ll get to know them better that way. And maybe soon, you’ll find someone you really want to get to know, if you get my meaning.
* Tara: Yep, I think I got it, Gramma.
* [Reward: Corn Bread recipe.]
