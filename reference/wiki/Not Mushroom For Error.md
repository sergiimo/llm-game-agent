# Not Mushroom For Error

Not Mushroom For Error is a quest given by the High Priestess during the Fall after completing the quest Know Witch Way the Wind Blows. It requires you to go into the Gloaming and find 1x Trompette de la Mort as an ingredient for the Winter Ritual Incantation. The reward for completion is 5x Levitation Potion.
