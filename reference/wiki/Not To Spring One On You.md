# Not To Spring One On You

Not To Spring One On You is a quest given by the High Priestess in the Winter. It requires the player to go through the third pillar to the Gloaming to gather 5x Dragon's Blood Vial so the coven can turn the season from Winter to Spring. The reward for completion is 5x Revitalizing Potion.

It takes 8 days for a tapped dragon's blood tree to produce a Dragon's Blood Vial. It is recommended that you use enough Spiles or Boost Harvest Potions to obtain 7 Dragon's Blood Vials, as there are two quests in the epilogue that require one vial each and there is no way to shorten the time it takes for the trees to produce them.
