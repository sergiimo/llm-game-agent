# Nuts About You

## Description
Brew a woodland medicinal potion, a shrinking potion, and a stretching potion, to help Aryel save a squirrel.
[Quest]
  title: Nuts About You
  type: To-Do Quests
  start: Aryel
  end: Aryel
  imagecaption: Squirrel in the mountains
  item_needs: Shrinking Potion; Stretching Potion; Woodland Medicinal Potion
  previous: Basilisk Quest
  rewards: 100x Gold; 1x Aryel's Hair

## Dialog
### Quest Intro
Aryel: ...

Aryel: I can reach far enough into the hidey-hole to give it food, but I'm not able to properly administer any medicine.

Aryel: But how will I get to the squirrel? I'm much taller than you--

Aryel: Mm, debatable. But you can create a shrinking potion, can you not?

Tara: Huh, good point!

Tara: So, shrink myself, head into the tree, and give our fluffy buddy some medicine?

Aryel: Exactly. Oh, make sure you adhere to the recipe -- it's potency will account for you having to shrink it down.

Aryel: Unless you believe you can heft something that will be the same size as you and twice as heavy...

### Quest Completion
Tara: All done with the potions, Aryel.

Aryel: Wonderful! Looks like you got the measurements just right.

Aryel: I'll take you to the pine where the squirrel is resting.

---

Aryel: You'll find the squirrel hiding inside.

Tara: ... Last time I did this, Violet and I both shrunk down. Since you're staying you-sized... No stepping on me, please.

Aryel: My feet will remain as they are. Backwards, and firmply rooted to the spot -- you have my word.

Tara: Here you go, squirrelly!

Squirrel: (squirrel noises)

Tara: Aww, you're welcome lil' guy!

Tara: The medicine worked like a charm, Aryel!

Tara: Oh!

Aryel: (inquisitive squirrel noises)

Squirrel: (happy squirrel noises)

Aryel: Indeed the medicine worked its magic, no small thanks to the Wyldling doing the delivering.

Aryel: The squirrel mentioned your gentle, kind manner, and wanted me to share that it is very grateful to you for the help -- as am I.

Aryel: You continue to prove yourself a true friend to the furry, the feathered, and all living things in-between, Tara.

Tara: Aww, I'm happy to be of service!

Aryel: And I am happy to hear you say so. You'll be the first I trust to help when a creature is in need of rescue.

Aryel: Here, take this for a job well done.
