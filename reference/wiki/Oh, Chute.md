# Oh, Chute

Oh, Chute is a quest given by Giva Joshi. It requires you to deliver 4x Silk to her, which sounds simpler than it is - you must first buy a Silkworm and a Mulberry Tree Seedling from Kai's Treasures, build a Silkworm Box, wait for the tree to grow and harvest a Mulberry Leaf to feed to the silkworms, then wait an additional three days for the silkworms to produce silk. The reward for completion is 5x Copper Ingot.

It is recommended to use a Boost Harvest Potion on the silkworms to produce 2 silk at once if you want to shorten the waiting period by three days.
