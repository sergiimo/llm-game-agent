# Ouch, That Hertz

Ouch, That Hertz is a quest given by Zephyr Grimspark during the Fall. It requires you to search the mines for her versorium and return it to her. The reward for completion is an Increase Energy spell.

The versorium can be found on level 16 of the mine. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Zephyr: GAH! You twice-blasted-
* Tara: Did you just electrocute yourself?
* Zephyr: Yes! Again! I was fiddlin’ with these wires, but it’s so dang hard to get an electrical charge reading without my versorium. So… bzzzzt. Over and over.
* Tara: Sorry, without your what now?
* Zephyr: My versorium! It’s an incantation-powered device that detects electric charges. I use it for everything. Charge up wires with magical energy and you’ve got dangerous currents on your hands! Except I haven’t seen the versorium since I last went into the mines…
* Tara: So you lost it.
* Zephyr: Yeah, probably. Hee! Classic Zephyr. Hey, if you see a little metal box covered in runes down there, couldja pick it up?
* Tara: Sure. As long as it doesn’t spark my fingers off. 

Quest completion:

* Zephyr: My versorium! Thank you so much.
* Tara: I can’t believe you kept experimenting this whole time without it!
* Zephyr: Oh, goblins build up resistance to electrical shocks over time. But the big currents still make me sit straight up, I tell you what! I appreciate you giving me a hand.
* [Reward: Increase Energy spell]
After some time, Zephyr will appear at the farm one morning:

* [Zephyr teleports in]
* Tara: Aiiieee!
* Zephyr: Tara! Remember the versorium you found for me? Well… you’ll never guess what I figured out when I went sniffin’ around that chair from the Summer Festival!
* Tara: It’s the crack of dawn, so I hope it’s good…
* Zephyr: So, uh… last Winter, I was runnin’ some experiments on that chair. Legend says it’s made of the same wood as the wand tree.
* Tara: Zephyr! Didn’t that seem relevant before?
* Zephyr: No! ‘Cause when I tried attachin’ some magical lodestone to see if I could detect any currents… nothin’. But when I tested it with the versorium, the magical current readings were through the roof! Here’s my hypothesis. Those lodestones primed the chair to receive magic. And when you sat in it – boom! The chair brought you right back to the place the lodestones came from.
* Tara: Ravenwood Hollow.
* Zephyr: Ravenwood Hollow. It was an accident, promise! Can you, uh, keep this a secret from Peri?
* --[Choice: Yes]
** Tara: I won’t tell. I can see Peri getting really mad.
** Zephyr: She sure might! Love her to death! But yeah! Thank you, Tara.
* --[Choice: Tell the truth]
** Tara: Peri’s been worried. It might put her mind at ease to tell her you solved this.
** Zephyr: Mmm. She does think Aryel did it and won’t fess up. Ah, geez, I gotta clear Aryel’s name.
* Tara: Next time you go attaching lodestones to something-
* Zephyr: Oh, I won’t make that mistake again. See ya back in Ravenwood Hollow, my friend!
* [Zephyr teleports away]
