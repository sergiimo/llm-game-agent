# Out of Whole Cloth

[Quest]
  title: Out of Whole Cloth
  type: To-Do Quests
  start: Thomas Lightfoot
  end: Thomas Lightfoot
  level: Summer
  rewards: * Recipes for Red Dye, Blue Dye, and Purple Dye
* Dyeing Bath blueprint
  previous: ?
  next: Can't A-bandage Me

Out of Whole Cloth is a quest given by Thomas Lightfoot at the beginning of Summer. It requires you to grow 4x Cotton, build a Loom, and weave the cotton into Cloth. The reward for completion are the Red Dye, Blue Dye, and Purple Dye recipes and the Dyeing Bath blueprint.

Thomas says he sells his cloth to the General Store, but Tara can only sell cloth to Kai's stall.
