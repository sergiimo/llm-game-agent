  CharacterDescription: Parker Johnson (45) moved to Fairhaven after marrying Lina to help her run the general store. Since then, they've had two children, Emmi and Finn. Like Lina, Parker is composed and competent. He now runs Nailed It!

But their marriage isn't perfect - he feels like Lina keeps him at arm's length, and he doesn't know why. He has recently joined the Consciousness Center. 

Parker grew up in the city, which he perceived as drab and gray. He grew to love plants, especially flowers, because of their vibrancy. 

Parker took up carpentry as a trade, but he would prefer to be a florist, like Violet.
