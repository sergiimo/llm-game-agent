## 1.5: Fabulous Farming
Released: April 28, 2023

Welcome to our Fabulous Farming Update!
* Visit Marty to get adorable new animals to your farm! There are ducks, pigs and even alpacas!
* Build a propagation shed and upgrade your farm buildings! A bigger and better toolshed, coop, barn and greenhouse await you!
* Guide your animals through pregnancy, and feed them their favourite foods to make them sparkle!
* Play now to see what else is new!

Need the details?
* A Giant Tool Shed can now be built from Parker's workshop any time after the first Tool Shed upgrade.
* A Spacious Greenhouse can be built from Parker's workshop after the first Greenhouse has been built.
* An Expanded Coop can be built once “The Menagerie Mix-Up” quest (which will start after Marty's epilogue in Year 2) is complete. This larger coop can house ducks as well as chickens, allows up to 10 animals in total to be kept, and includes an incubator that can be used to hatch eggs.
* A Huge Barn can be built once “The Menagerie Mix-Up” quest is complete. This larger barn can accommodate up to 10 animals, and includes a birthing stall for hosting pregnant barn animals. For adorable baby animals, purchase Fertility Potions from Aryel. Animals will need to be hand-fed for each day of their pregnancy, even if you've built the Hay Field.
* A Feed Mill can be built in your Tool Shed after “The Menagerie Mix-Up” quest is complete, allowing you to craft your own Poultry Feed.
* Baby ducks, pigs and alpacas can be purchased from Marty's ranch after “The Menagerie Mix-Up” quest is complete. Pigs will sometimes find a Truffle for you, and Alpacas are an excellent source of quality Wool.
* Animals can now be gifted fruit and vegetables, and each animal will have an individual favourite food that you can discover. There are some surprises to discover when animals get to eat their favorite foods!
* A Propagation Shed can be built from Parker's workshop shortly after Lina and Parker's epilogue in Year 2. (Lina will let you know when this is possible during one of her work days).
* Partake in a Harvest Festival and coven ritual in Fall of Year 2 or later, and a second Winter Feast with a magical fireworks display.
* A Candy Machine can be built in your Tool Shed if you speak to Zephyr during the Harvest Festival preparations.
* The Enhanced Scrying Potion can be used to immediately teleport yourself to another character's location.
* Animal Growth Booster can be fed to your farm animals to give them a quick growth spurt, or to hurry along a pregnancy.
* You may finally learn the Anti-Shapeshifting Spell, allowing you to return to human form during the day.
* Your best friends and most beloved animals may join in on your photo shoots.

We've also fixed a few bugs! Including:
* Lina's friendship milestones can now be attained if they weren't completed during Year 1.
* The “People Pleaser” achievement will be correctly awarded.
* Wellspring season requests can be fulfilled using a mixture of vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes.
* Westley no longer giggles in Tara's voice.

## 1.4: Violet's Boutique
Released: February 24, 2023

Welcome to the grand opening of Violet’s Boutique!
* Help Violet open her dream boutique
* Craft new outfits for Tara
* Capture your life in Fairhaven with our new Photo Mode!

Need the details?
* Any time after completing the “Out of Whole Cloth” quest (where you learn to make cloth in the first Summer), you can help Violet start her new clothing business. * Olive tree seedlings can be purchased from Kai and planted in a garden bed.
* Additional dye and cloth colors black, green, orange and yellow have been added.

## 1.0.5
Released: March 15, 2022

Peripherals
* Added support for Magic Keyboard and other wired or Bluetooth keyboards on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. Requires iOS 14 or later.
* Added support for Nimbus MFi controller.

Save Games and iCloud
* Save games are now automatically backed up every game-day, up to the 6 most recent days.
* Save games are also backed up when resolving an iCloud save game conflict, so that the alternative conflict resolution can be tried.
* To access and restore the backups, open the Load Game menu from the main menu, and click the history icon next to each save slot.
* Fixed multiple issues relating to save games being reset or losing progress after synchronising over iCloud.
* Added warning messages when save games can't be saved due to device storage exhausted.

General Gameplay
* Deleting an object in build mode now displays a confirmation dialog (text is currently English only; translations coming soon!)
* Cancelling an action on a crafting station that's in progress now returns all the original items to you.
* Cooking ingredients can be substituted once purchased without requiring them to be crafted or caught first. Yes, this makes fish sticks even easier, if that's your game.
* Fixed Kim not providing any more animal fats and meat cuts after a number of animals have been sold.
* Fixed whirligigs immediately producing materials for some players, instead of going off to work, and sometimes not producing the correct number of materials.
* Spiles can be used on maple trees after they've healed from damage.
* Some previously-undiscoverable recipes are now discoverable in the mountain.
* People Pleaser achievement can now be achieved.
* Pinecones and Nigella will be more prevalent in the mountain.
* Peanut Brittle is correctly classified as a dessert, not a main. Take care of your health.

Relationships and Romance
* Turning down a romantic advance, or breaking up with a partner, permanently changes your relationship with that character to be platonic. This prevents future romantic advances and lets you fill up all the hearts in your journal.
* Fixed multiple issues relating to dating and marrying someone new after breaking up with a previous partner.
* The ambiguous “let's take things slow” option has been removed from romantic advances.
* Fixed romantic partners waiting to speak to you indefinitely after the epilogue.
* Amira will continue her coven duties even after marrying you.

Spring
* Fixed some cases where witches were disrobing when they should be robed, and vice-versa.
* Players who had previously accidentally deleted their cauldron before obtaining the ability to craft a replacement will now be granted the Cauldron blueprint when they upgrade to this version.

Summer
* Fixed issues with “At the Break-in Point” not working for some players.

Fall
* Fixed Versorium cannot be picked up while Summon Small Things is active.

Winter
* Fixed Violet being non-interactive near Gloria for some players.
* Fixed Raven disrobing when loading a save game during the final showdown.
* Fixed some characters disappearing for some players during “Locket Up”.
* “Seton's Secrets” task will now be possible during “The Unjolly Rancher”, even if “We Make a Great Pair-ogi” was not completed. Yep, this was fixed in the last version too, but now it's really really fixed.
