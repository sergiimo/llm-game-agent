# Peace of my Mind

Peace of my Mind is a quest given by Angus Seton in the epilogue. It requires the player to craft 1x Atonement Incantation for him so he can make amends for the actions of his ancestor and lift the curse oh the Seton bloodline. The reward for completion is 3x Croissant.

The player must buy the incantation recipe from Westley for 100 coins before they can craft it. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: Hi Angus, how’s it going?
* Angus: Pretty good, considering I just found out that the love of my life is a witch. Not that there’s anything wrong with being a witch, Tara! Ugh, I’m sorry.
* Tara: It’s okay. I know it’s a lot to take in.
* Angus: It’s just, in my family, we haven’t had the best history with witches. And it’s our fault. Well, my ancestor’s fault. He accused his sister-in-law of witchcraft because he wanted her land. The wealth from that land is part of our inheritance. So long as I’m a Seton, I’ll be haunted by her curse. I just wish there was some way I could make amends.
* Tara: Maybe there is. By any chance, do you still have anything belonging to your ancestor?
* Angus: Yes, I have a brooch. Well, it’s a pin. My older brother didn’t want it, so it passed to me.
* Tara: I suspect Westley might have just the incantation we need in his bookstore. Bring the brooch with you to work, and I’ll meet you here once I have it ready.

Speak to Westley about potential solutions for Angus.

* Tara: Westley, do you have any incantations for… saying sorry?
* Westley: Actually I do! But who would you need to apologize to, Tara?
* Tara: It’s not for me, it’s for Angus.
* Westley: Ah. He wants to apologize to Marty.
* Tara: Well, not quite. I don’t think he really feels that bad about Marty, to be honest.
* Westley: Well, that’s because Marty is a slimy real estate heir who was trying to use the island for his own profit.
* Tara: True. Anyway, Angus wants to apologize to a ghost.
* Westley: Fair enough. Well, I do have an atonement incantation in my archives. And it’s on sale!

Craft the atonement incantation. 

Go to Angus with the atonement incantation.

* Angus: I’ve been carrying around the brooch. It’s kind of creeping me out. Can we go work your magic?
* Tara: Yes, let’s go to the woods now.
* Angus: The woods? Okay. Just promise you won’t stray far, okay?

* [The woods]

* Tara: Okay, I need you to hold up the brooch. Repeat after me: spirit of Euphame, accept this sacrifice as an offering in atonement for the sins of my forefather.
* Angus: Spirit of Euphame…..Effie…
* Tara: Angus, are you all right?
* Angus: Effie, I didna mean for them to put you in the fires. I thought you would be locked in the jail for a time, and once I had yer land you would be set free. But then the king got word, you see. He blamed you and the others for the storm that set upon his ship. He had to have his revenge. Effie, forgive me. Forgive me.
* Angus: What… happened?
* Tara: Angus, are you all right?
* Angus: Yes. I feel… great! She’s gone. She’s let me go. Thank you, Tara. Thank you so much.
* [Reward: 3x Croissant]
