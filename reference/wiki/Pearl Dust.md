# Pearl Dust

[Crafting Ingredient]
  itemName: Pearl Dust
  description: A dazzling dust with magical properties made by grinding pearls in the Mortar and Pestle. Obtained from Lina during the wand upgrade quest.
  source: Mortar and Pestle
  recipe: 2x Pearl
  unlockRequirements: Recipe is unlocked during wand upgrade quest with Lina
  quests: Zephyr will sell you powered gems, which requires one gem (each gem has a different time effect) and one Pearl Dust
