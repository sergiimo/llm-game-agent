# Peri's Apothecary

| <b>Peri's Apothecary</b> |
| <b>Mon-Tues | Closed |
| <b>Wed-Sun | 9am-5pm |
; 
"Magical ingredients for your potioning and incantations."
; 

Peri's Apothecary is a shop in Ravenwood Hollow run by Peri, who sells a rotating stock of forage items, crafting ingredients, potions, potion recipes, and seeds that can be grown in the Gloaming. Peri also buys certain potions and magical flowers. Selling enough items to Peri unlocks new items available for purchase.
