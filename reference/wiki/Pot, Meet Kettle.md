# Pot, Meet Kettle

Pot, Meet Kettle is a quest given by Natalia Kuznetsova in the Winter. It requires the player to deliver 20x Copper Ingot to her so she can make a soup kettle. The day after giving her the materials, Cameron will thank the player and ask them to make the first batch of soup. The reward for completion is 250 coins.

The player can get 1x Pumpkin Soup for free every Sunday from the soup kettle next to the Consciousness Center after completing this quest. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Natalia: Hey Tara. If it’s no trouble, can I speak to ya for a moment?
* Tara: Sure. What’s up?
* Natalia: I want to help Messenger Cameron prove that the League is a force for good in the community. I feel badly, you know, about Sophia. I still can’t explain what happened. It’s not just about weeding out the Malcontents. We can help our neighbors in other ways. Anyway, Cameron wants to start a new charity initiative – a soup kettle! We’ll be offering free soup to anyone in the community who wants it.
* Tara: That’s a wonderful idea.
* Natalia: I thought you’d like it! ‘Course, we’re gonna need a really big pot! I’ll take any copper you have to spare. Once I’ve got enough, I can hammer out the soup kettle.

Bring Natalia copper so she can get to work on the soup kettle. 

* Natalia: Been in the mines lately?
* Tara: Yep! Here’s your copper!
* Natalia: Wonderful! I’ll get right to work and give this to Messenger Cameron tomorrow. If you have a moment to stop by the Consciousness Center, I know he’ll want to thank you himself.

Wait for Natalia to finish making the soup kettle.

* Cameron: Tara, thank you so much for your contribution to our charity initiative! Natalia said she couldn’t have finished the kettle without you!
* Tara: Happy to help. Besides, who doesn’t love soup? Especially the free kind.
* Cameron: Well, hehe, about that. You see, I grew up on TV sets, we always had catering tables and… I never quite learned how to cook. But I’m trying to learn! It’s just my first few attempts haven’t been exactly… edible. If you wouldn’t mind supplying the first batch of soup, I’ll get some more practice in to keep it going!
* [Receive Pumpkin Soup recipe]

Make a batch of pumpkin soup so that Cameron can get the soup kettle started. 

* Cameron: Have you had the chance to make some soup?
* Tara: Yes, here you go!
* Cameron: Thanks! I’ll get this heated up in the kettle straight away. You’re welcome to stop by every Sunday for some soup, if you like!
* [Reward: 250 coins]
