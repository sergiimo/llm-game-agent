# Ravenwood Hollow

## Ravenwood Hollow
Ravenwood Hollow is a location near Fairhaven.
Spoilers
|  |
Ravenwood Hollow is a Fae town that can be reached by repairing the boat that will appear on the small lake by Hazel's farm. 

It is home to Zephyr Grimspark, Peri, Aryel and Westley Vuk.

Aryel runs Pig's Wings and Dragonlings (on the left of the landing place) a shop for animal care. Peri runs Peri's Apothecary (on the right), where you can buy magical ingredients for portioning and incantations. Zephyr Grimspark owns Magical Mechanicals (on the right), a shop where you can have your broom calibrated and buy magical machines to help you on your farm. Westley Vuk runs Arcane Texts, a magical bookshop.

There is also a notice board, similar to the one in town, where you can fulfill orders for the inhabitants of Ravenwood Hollow. Shelby can sometimes be seen visiting the town.
