# School

[Location]
  title: School
  map: between Violet's Violets and Nailed It!
  location: Town Center
  imagecaption: On room schoolhouse
The town's school is a teal building with white trim and a small bronze bell. The playground outside has a slide and two person swing set. Inside, the school is only one room, with a blackboard at the far end and six desks lined up in 3 rows.

The school does not hold classes in Summer, but the building remains open 9am to 3pm M-F.
