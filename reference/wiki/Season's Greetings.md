# Season's Greetings

Season's Greetings is a quest given by the High Priestess during the Fall after finishing the quest Know Witch Way the Wind Blows. It requires you to craft 1x Winter Ritual Incantation and 1x Spirit Flame. The reward for completion is 5x Revitalizing Potion.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Damon: I can’t stop thinking about Sophia. She had to suffer for something we messed up. 
*(Wraithing in Anticipation completed) 
**Damon: At least Tara and Amira got Natalia to quit spreading that skinder.
**High Priestess Lina: You did Fairhaven – and Sophia – a great service. Thank you both.  
*(Wraithing in Anticipation not completed) 
**Damon: And now Otto can say his neighborhood watch is important because it flushed out a witch! If only he knew... 
* High Priestess Lina: Journeyman, we did our best to keep the Wellspring balanced.
* Farseer Shelby: But all that trying amounted to a hill of beans. The wraith still got out.
* Adept Amira: It’s the danger we always feared would emerge from the Wellspring…
* High Priestess Lina: It’s true. We have to do a better job of keeping this island safe. But for now, let us turn our attention to the changing of the seasons. It won’t be easy this time. 

Quest completion:

* High Priestess Lina: Have you prepared the Winter Ritual incantation?
* Tara: I have it, and I’m ready to change the seasons.
* High Priestess Lina: Good. Then let us begin. Witches of the Fairhaven coven, take your places around the circle. Once again, we turn the wheel of the year.
* Herbalist Violet: From witches new and witches old, we greet the coming of the cold.
* Journeyman Damon: We call forth the spirits of quiet and reflection…
* Adept Amira: We call on the candle’s flame, surrounded by darkness…
* Farseer Shelby: We say goodbye to the bounty of the Fall harvests…
* Novice Tara: … and welcome Winter. It’s snowing! I love snow!
* Farseer Shelby: A good sign it is, too. Means the island is in harmony with itself.
* Journeyman Damon: I’d say I agree with you, Farseer, but…
* High Priestess Lina: Belladona’s knickers, it’s coming down hard now!
* Adept Amira: Quick, everyone get home. I don’t want to treat any of you for frostbite tomorrow.
