# Sebastián Miranda/Dialogues

This page lists the dialogue lines for Sebastián Miranda

| Spring |
| Oh, hey! You're new here! I'm Sebastián, who are you? |
Tara: I'm Tara, I just moved here to help out my Gramma Hazel. Do you know her?  

Sebastián: Oh yeah! Hazel's cool, like, for a grown up. I'll see ya around!
| Hazel is a dope old lady! You're lucky, having a gramma like that. |

Sebastián: My mom's parents died when I was little, and my dad's parents can't visit much 'cuz of visa problems. 

Tara: Oh… I'm sorry for bringing it up. 

Sebastián: It's cool. Hazel's the communal gramma.

Tara: Sebastián… I don't even remember what that is. 

Sebastián: Hah! That's what I told Mrs. Soft when she said I should pay more attention in algebra II!

| Fall |
| Back to the grind. |
Tara: What, school? 

Sebastián: No, I'm learning how to grind down ramps on my skateboard! I keep rolling off the edge instead, though.
| Violet will not shut up about the flowers for Angus and Francis's wedding. But like, aren't they having the wedding outside? Why do they need more flowers? |
| Otto shouldn't be mayor. He only lucked into it last time because my dad decided not to run for re-election. I still don't know why he did that. He was a great mayor! At least I thought so. |
| I've been thinking lately - Mom left us right after Dad decided not to run for mayor again. Do you think that might be why? Maybe she just didn't want to live here if she couldn't be first lady anymore. |
Tara: I'm sure that's not why, Sebastián. 

Sebastián: Yeah, that doesn't make sense to me, either.
| Tara: How's school, Sebastián? |
Sebastián: It's weird. I graduate this year, did you know that? I've wanted to get off this island for so long… But now I'm not really sure where I'll go.

| I'm so glad the wedding is over. My sister's been a total stressball over the flowers. Maybe now she'll mellow out. Who and I kidding? Violet doesn't really "do" mellow. |
| Angus and Francis seem really happy. But my Mom and Dad did too, before she left. I don't know why I said that. I just hope they have better luck, I guess. |
| I can't believe anyone would think Mrs. Moretti is a witch. She's the nicest person in Fairhaven! After my mom took off and my Dad got his job off-island, she brought us dinner casseroles like every night. I finally started going to her diner just to save her the trip. |
| Well, I'm not surprised everyone picked Mayor Soft again. |

Sebastián: Yeah, well. Nothing is ever really going to change around here.
