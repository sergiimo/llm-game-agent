# See the Farseer

See the Farseer is a quest given by  the Farseer to unlock his identity. It requires you to create 1x Freeze Time incantation and deliver it to him during the day. You get to keep the incantation.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: I’ve been reading this journal. It belonged to Gramma, and-
* High Priestess: Thank goodness! I know Hazel took the journal in for a re-binding. I’m glad that it’s safely back in your hands.
* Tara: There are entries that mention the fairies. Does… does Fairhaven have fairies?
* High Priestess: You still have much to learn about the magic on this island. But before I tell you more about that, you must prove that you understand us, as a coven. Speak to the Farseer witch, he will explain. 

Part 1: Speak to the Farseer to learn about the witch identity rites.

* Farseer: Your gram wasn’t a fan of our masks. But rules are rules. You’ve got to earn our trust before we can reveal who we really are. Unless you think you can guess my name?
* Tara: Rumplestiltskin!
* Farseer: Gah! Don’t be callin’ the fae folk unless ya mean it! Now you’re to complete a test. Each member of the coven will ask ya for a magical item. You’re to bring it to them…
* Tara: Sounds easy enough.
* Farseer: During the day, in town. If ya give it to the right person, we’ll say so.
* Tara: What? But… what if I give it to the wrong person? Won’t Cameron freak out if I give him some magic doodad?
* Farseer: Don’t worry. Civilians can’t read incantations. It just looks like a blank piece of paper to ‘em. I’m to set you the first task. It has to do with time.
* Tara: Oooh, time travel? How far back can I go?
* Farseer: You’re not ready for that yet, Novice. But you can try pausing time for a moment, to give ya long enough to finish what you’ve got to do. It will only work while you’re in the place you read the spell, once ya leave that space, time will resume its course. Here’s what you need to write the spell. Come to me during the day when you’ve finished it.
* [Get Freeze Time Incantation recipe]

Part 2: Make a freeze time incantation as part of your quest to unlock the Farseer’s identity.

* Tara: Here you go… Shelby.
* Shelby: That’s fine. You keep it. You’ll need it.
* Tara: So… I passed?
* Shelby: You were always gonna pass. Now go on. Don’t want folks getting too curious.
