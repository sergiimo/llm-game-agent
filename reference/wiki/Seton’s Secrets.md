# Seton’s Secrets

Seton’s Secrets is a series of quests that begins at the first coven meeting after the quest The Unjolly Rancher begins. It requires the player to enter Seton's Bakery using Francis's key to retrieve a photo, show the photo to the Farseer, and craft 1x Angus' Wraith Banishment Spell.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: Francis! Angus is-
* Francis: Not himself. He would never accuse Marty of being a witch. Does it… have something to do with whatever it was that came flying out of the woods?
* High Priestess Lina: Yes. I’m afraid Angus is under the influence of a dark spirit. We call them wraiths. We’ll need your help to free him.
* Francis: I’ll do whatever it takes to cure Angus. But that doesn’t mean I’m joining you in this… group that meets in the woods.
* Adept Amira: We need you to fashion an iron amulet to capture the jinni.
* Francis: Done. Anything else?
* High Priestess Lina: Yes. I know it might be hard to hear, but the wraith didn’t pick Angus at random. He has an existing prejudice against witches, and the wraith bound itself to that. Help the Novice find out why.
* Tara: I feel weird asking this, but… you must have a spare key to Angus’s shop, right? I need to find a personal item to use in the spell to free him from the wraith.
* Francis: Yes. Here. [Gives Angus’ Bakery Key] You should go at night, or on Angus’s day off. If he catches you, I never gave you the key, understand?

Find a personal item in Angus’ bakery.

* Tara: That was easy! If I were a personal item that might reveal a dark secret from Angus’s past, what would I look like?
* Tara: Aha! I better take this to the Farseer.

Bring the photo to the Farseer.

* Tara: What do you think, Farseer?
* Farseer Shelby: I think Angus uses too much butter in his baking.
* Tara: No, I mean, what do you see?
* Farseer Shelby: I see a little boy hiding in a closet. His parents have brought the priest for their eldest, who’s complainin’ of visions. A witch at the edge of the woods, beckonin’ him at night.
* Tara: The curse…
* Farseer Shelby: The priest says his rites. Whatever it is, rises out of the eldest son. And finds the middle one, hiding where his parents don’t see him.
* Tara: It passed to Angus… maybe he’s seen one of us, and thought we were his witch!
* Farseer Shelby: Could be. Made him an easy mark for the wraith, that’s for certain. Ya have what ya need now to write the incantation to free Angus. Mind you get it done in time.

Craft the banishment incantation.

Let the High Priestess know it’s ready.

* High Priestess Lina: Is all in readiness for Marty’s trial?
* Tara: Yes. I just hope Francis made the locket in time.

Now that the incantation is prepared, there’s nothing to do but wait until Marty’s witch trial to use it.
