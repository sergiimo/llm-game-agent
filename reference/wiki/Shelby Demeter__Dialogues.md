# Shelby Demeter/Dialogues

CharacterNav
This page lists the dialogue lines for Shelby Demeter.

| Spring |
| Tara: Hi, I'm Tara. I just moved to town. |
Shelby: You look just like I pictured.  

Tara: I'm sorry, what? 

Shelby: Being Hazel's granddaughter, I mean. 

Tara: Oh. And you are… 

Shelby: Been many things, but if it's a name you're looking for, folks here call me Shelby.
| I know what ya must be thinking. Why does Old Shelby live in the woods? |
Tara: Oh… well, it's really pretty… and quiet… 

Shelby: Don't patronize me, missy. Truth is there are riches here the untrained eye can't see. You bring me forageables, and I'll pay ya fairly for your troubles. 

Tara: I don't mind helping you out, you don't have to pay me. 

Shelby: Heh. I'm a hermit, not a pauper. Don't need charity from no one. My bones may be gettin' old, but my mind's as sharp as a... sharp as a... 

Tara: A tack? 

Shelby: Don't interrupt. Young folk these days. No respect for their elders.
| Tara: How long have you lived in Fairhaven, Shelby? |
Shelby: Long enough to know I prefer the peace and quiet of the woods to town. I don't mind when yer gram comes for a visit, though. She knows when to take her leave without needing a hint. 

Tara: Oh. Okay. I, um... guess I'll be on my way.
| Tara: Ah-choo! Sorry, I'm still not used to being around this much pollen. Triggers my allergies. |
Shelby: Don't go blamin' the trees. They didn't knock ya out of balance. 

Tara: Huh? 

Shelby: Don't worry. You'll be set right soon enough.
| I see the doctor 'round here a fair bit. Looking for medicinal herbs. Reminds me of my mother. |
Tara: Was your mom a doctor, Shelby? 

Shelby: Not formally as such, no. But she was a healer all the same.
| Tara: Hiya Shelby. Bruno gave me a rod. Thought I might try my chances at the lake. |
Shelby: You'll do better if you aim where they're going to be. No point goin' where they were.
| Some folks in town are afraid of the woods. I'd try to ease their minds about it, but it's no use. Hard to reason with irrational fear. |
| Tara: So why did you pick Fairhaven, Shelby? Had you ever been here before? |
Shelby: No, not in this life, at least. I was living in the city, like you. Stayed too long, to say the least.
| Findin' yer way here on the island? |
Tara: Yes, well, the longer I'm here the more it feels like coming home. Sometimes I'll turn a corner and be hit by some memory from when I was a kid, like making sandcastles at the beach with grampa… Memory's funny like that. 

Shelby: Yer lucky to have such ties to this place. It's special.
| Tara: Do you have any family nearby, Shelby? |
Shelby: Have cousins, they came across the ocean with me when we were small. Rest of our family… well they never made it out of Hungary. 

Tara: Oh. I'm sorry. 

Shelby: It was a long time ago.
| Tara: Have you ever been by the Consciousness Center, Shelby? |
Shelby: Why would I want to do that? Heard that Cameron's always going on about walking the path. Hmmph. Never seen him set foot in these woods, I'll tell you that.
| Hmmph. |
Tara: What's wrong, Shelby?  

Shelby: Was making some tea but the rain put my fire out. 

Tara: Oh, I could bring you some tea. 

Shelby: No need. It'll stop soon enough.
| You know who I do see in these woods sometimes? Otto. Never after dark, of course. |
Tara: Oh, does he sometimes go fishing with his dad? 

Shelby: Hahahahahahahahhaahhaahaa! You do have a fine sense of humor, I must say.  

Tara: Gee, thanks.
| Went by Lina's store to get my weekly provisions. She's yer friend, right? |
Tara: Lina? Yes, we've known each other a long time. 

Shelby: You should tell her not to worry herself so much over those kids. They're good kids. Got up to worse mischief in my day. 

Tara: You did? Like what? 

Shelby: Nothing anyone could pin on me. Hehe!
| Parker keeps offerin' to build me a house in town. I know he's just tryin' to be kind. But I like being out here with my little woodland friends. They're plenty good company. |
| It's almost time for Summer. |
Tara: I know, I know, I'm working on it. 

Shelby: What was that you said? 

Tara: Oh, um… nothing!
| Sometimes in the forest it's hard to see the path ahead. Then boom: a clearing. |
| Tara: Hey, Shelby. |
Shelby: … Wa… wa… what, what, fire! 

Tara: There's no fire, Shelby! 

Shelby: There's always fire, girl. What matters is who controls it.
| Ya gotta be careful eating mushrooms. They'll take you places. |
Tara: Oh yeah, I've heard of people going on journeys. 

Shelby: Yeh - to the outhouse in my case. I'm mushroom intolerant.
| Be careful where ya put ya feet. |
Tara: Oh no, is there poison ivy here? 

Shelby: Poison? Who's talking about poison? Nah, I mean the forest folk. 

Tara: You mean like bugs? 

Shelby: Don't call them that!
| Ya from the city, eh? Terrible smells, people, noises… |
Tara: Oh, you've been there?  

Shelby: In a past life. I'll never leave the forest again.

| Summer |
| Yer grandma knew ya would come back to take care of her in the end. |
Tara: She did? 

Shelby: Well, yeh, I told her. 

Tara: I don't get it. 

Shelby: Nah, ya sure don't.
| I've lost more loved ones than I care to speak about. I still see them sometimes, though. |
Tara: Oh, you mean like in dreams? 

Shelby: ….. Sure.
| Tara: Looking forward to the Summer Festival, Shelby? |
Shelby: It's a bit tamer than the Summer festivities I remember from my boyhood. Less music and dancin'.  

Tara: Shelby! I didn't know you danced. 

Shelby: Oh, I can dance circles around any of you young folk. Hehe.
| Kids must be bored already with school out. Seen Juliet round here a fair bit. But maybe she just wants a quiet place to practice her music. Still waters run deep with that one. |
| Emmi and Finn remind me so much of my cousins. They always had some scheme going. Running around with them probably saved my life, in the end. |
| Tara: Shelby, you don't mind having that fire going when it's this hot out? |
Shelby: No. I made my peace with fire long ago.
| Heard there's a new rancher in town. Don't think he knows much 'bout ranching, though. |
Tara: How do you know that? 

Shelby: Cuz one of his chicks wandered all the way over here the other day! Lucky it wasn't picked off by a cat!
| Tried giving Angus a recipe from back home to use up leftover bits of bread. No reason to waste what's perfectly useful. |
| Tara: So you were living in the city before you came here… what did you do? |
Shelby: Heh, you mean like a job? I was a stock trader. Did well enough. 

Tara: Wait, really? Why did you quit? 

Shelby: Eh. Just because you're good at somethin' doesn't mean it's the right life for ya. One day it all came crashing down. So I walked away.
| I don't venture into town too often, but days like these? Well, I think it's worth the noise. |
| Tara: Shelby, have you ever seen anything um… strange happen at the Summer Festival? |
Shelby: Otto wore a three piece suit to the beach. That strange enough for ya?
| Tara: What did you think of Otto's speech, Shelby? |
Shelby: I don't like it. I've seen this all before. Even after the war, when there were troubles, whoever was in charge would point to us as the source of their problems. That's what they do, so their people don't notice their failure at managing things is the real reason.
| Tara: Have you met Giva yet, Shelby? She's a meteorologist. |
Shelby: Ah, so she spends time staring at these skies, does she? I wonder what they tell her.
| It will be nice when Fall comes. |
Tara: Oooh, are you a fan of spooky season, Shelby? 

Shelby: Don't go taunting the spirits, girl.
| Tara: How are you doing in this drought, Shelby? |
Shelby: Oh, I've got what I need. You should look after some of the other folks.
| Hope this drought ends soon. Heat makes people ornery. Livestock too. One summer my cousin Kristoff got so bored he tried wrestling a goat. |
Tara: Uh… how did that go? 

Shelby: Bout as well as you'd imagine. And he smelled so bad after his ma refused to let him back in the house. He had to sleep in the barn that night...
| Tara: Do you remember there ever being a drought as bad as this on the island before, Shelby? |
Shelby: I don't. But truth be told I haven't been here all that long. Only about 20 years. 

Tara: Oh. That makes sense. I thought I would have remembered you. 

Shelby: Heh, I guess I'm hard to forget!
| Ya see any stones, ya best stay away. |
Tara: But there are stones everywhere! 

Shelby: Ya mind what I said.
| Death is a part of life. I once thought, if I could make enough money, I could escape it. But it came for me all the same. |
| Tara: Enjoying summer, Shelby? |
Shelby: Night skies are clear. That's useful. 

Tara: Useful for what? 

Shelby: Fer lookin' at the sky, what else?

| Fall |
| Tara: How are you feeling, Shelby? Any more headaches? |
Shelby: Feelin' much better, thanks. But I still feel a chill in the air. 

Tara: Well, it is Fall! 

Shelby: I know that. Young folks think they're so clever these days.
| Tara: Angus and Francis are so cute together, huh? |
Shelby: Yes. Love is in the air for you young folks these days. 

Tara: You sound a bit wistful, Shelby.  

Shelby: Well. It's a ship that sailed for me a long time ago.
| Francis reminds me a bit of an old friend of mine. We met not too long after I first came to this country. |
Tara: Are you still in touch? 

Shelby: Ah, no. Not for years. Some bridges can't be mended, I'm afraid.
| Tara: Violet's really worked up about the election. Do you think Otto will win again? |
Shelby: Otto won't be mayor much longer. 

Tara: Oh, so you think Marty will win. 

Shelby: That's not what I said, is it? 

Tara: Okaaaay.
| That Marty fella came all the way out here tryin' to win my vote. Didn't have the heart to tell him it's a lost cause. |
Tara: Why do you say that? He could still win. 

Shelby: Hmmph. It's not in the stars, I'm tellin' ya.
| Tara: Hey Shelby. What's on your mind? |
Shelby: Fall always makes me think about the folks on the other side. It's when they feel close. 

Tara: Oh, you mean like in Ravenwood Hollow? 

Shelby: No. I mean your gram. Giacomo. My parents. Not Gloria, though. Always found that curious.
| Tara: I'm not sure that I'm ready for my first Winter on the farm. |
Shelby: It's going to be a long one. But you'll do fine. You're ready, Tara. Your gram made sure of it.
| Tara: The woods are so nice this time of year, aren't they Shelby? |
Shelby: Time doesn't mean much. 
Tara: Oh, because you mean you've lived a long life?  

Shelby: I meant what I said.
| I never married. Things were different, in my day. Angus and Francis, their stars aligned at just the right time. I'm glad for them. |
| Now I see what song Juliet was practicin' that whole time. She's really got an ear for music, just like her Pa. |
Tara: Oh, I didn't know her dad was a musician.  

Shelby: He wasn't as such. But he had a sweet singing voice. He used to sing below Sophia's window when they were courtin'. 

Tara: Awww, really? That's adorable! 

Shelby: Yes. It was quite a love story.
| Any ol' fool can see Sophia is no witch. But Otto is a young fool. Young and ambitious. |
| Tara: Well, I guess you were right! Otto's still mayor after all. |
Shelby: For now.  

Tara: Right. Until the next election. 

Shelby: ….. 

Tara: Okay then. I will see you around!
| Didn't expect Otto to escalate things to a neighborhood watch. Time was, this would be my cue to pack up and leave. |
Tara: No, Shelby! Don't move away! 

Shelby: Don't worry, girl. I'm not going anywhere. Not for Otto or anyone else.
| No one woulda called Sophia a witch if Markus were still around. And Giacomo. And Gloria… |
| Tara: I'm so glad Natalia came to her senses and recanted her accusation. |
Shelby: There's no sense in that head of hers at all, nor the others. They're on the hunt, now. I've seen this happen before. Always ends the same way.
| I'm glad your gram wasn't here to see that sham of a trial. She would have given Otto an earful and then some. |
Tara: Oh, I bet you're right about that!  

Shelby: We've lost so many in the past few years. I'm not goin' anywhere, though. Just watch 'em try to take 'ol Shelby.
| Tara: Mmmm, what do I smell? |
Shelby: Makin' some soup for Sophia. Thought it might cheer her up. Was my mam's recipe. Any time we had to pick up and move, she'd dig up whatever was left in the garden to bring with us. Soup could make the vegetables stretch over a few days.

| Winter |
| Tara: Maybe the Winter Feast can help turn things around in town. |
Shelby: Hmmph. Don't know what they're celebratin'. It's not like the old days. 

Tara: Oh, so you're not going to come? 

Shelby: Didn't say that.
| It was Winter when my family moved into new government housing on the outskirts of a village. The buildings had hardly any insulation. The locals claimed… our fire just got out of control. But my pa knew how to build a fire so the sparks wouldn't jump. |
Tara: Oh Shelby. I am so sorry. 

Shelby: It was so long ago. But every Winter I feel like it was just yesterday.
| Brrrr. It's so cold I might have to trek out to the mountain pass and try that onsen Kim's always going on about. |
| Tara: Hey Shelby, I just helped Natalia make a big kettle. Cameron's going to be offering free soup at the Consciousness Center. |
Shelby: Hmmph. I never trusted that Cameron. ….. What kind of soup?
| Be careful lighting fires on these long winter nights. They can keep ya warm, but you gotta keep 'em controlled. |
| Tara: The ferry being out is making people even more anxious. You can feel the tension every time you walk through town. |
Shelby: Just another reason to avoid town, I reckon. Not that I needed one. People become even more dangerous when they feel trapped. Hope Parker can fix it soon.
| Tara: I'm worried about Francis. He took the news a bit hard. |
Shelby: He'll be fine. And maybe he and his brother will reconcile, to boot.
| Gah! There's so much snow comin' down, I - Wait, was it even snowin'? |
| Tara: Did you try Giva's soup at the feast? |
Shellby: Course I did! It was quite nice! 

Tara: And did you know Cameron helped make it? 

Shelby: Huh. Is that so?
| Tara: Now Angus has accused Marty. It feels like things in town are falling apart. |
Shelby: They're turning against outsiders and newcomers now. It's just how it goes. I can't imagine how low their opinion of witches must be if they think Marty is among our number. Ha!
| Winter is a good time for making peace with those who have wronged you. No point carrying that bitterness into the New Year. |
| In my family, Winter was a time of remembrance. Not that I ever forget them, even the rest of the year. |
| Tara: Wow. The woods look so beautiful in the snow. |
Shelby: That they do. Winter brings beauty… and the dark.
| Tara: You wanna know the scariest part? Even after the wraith left Angus, I wasn't sure that he would recant. |
Shelby: He wanted to believe it. That's for certain.
| Tara: So Marty and Otto were plotting to turn this place into a resort this whole time? |
Shelby: Seems that way. Don't suppose I would have been allowed to keep my cabin out here if they'd had their way.
| Tara: So Parker's accused Giva. What now? |
Shelby: I'd say we count our lucky stars that these witch hunters are so bad at huntin' witches. We can set Parker free. And maybe sort out this whole mess, finally.
| I hope Giva doesn't leave Fairhaven once this is all over. Might sound strange, but I relate to that young lady. |
| So Giva's free. All the same, Cameron might be reconsidering his role in all of this. |
Tara: I hope you're right.

| Epilogue |
| Tara: You know, now that I've had a minute to like, sleep, and think things over, you were right about a lot of things. |
Shelby: Don't worry. I won't let it get to my head. My head's big enough, hehe!
| Tara: I… I used your watch. |
Shelby: I'm glad. Not everyone gets the chance to say the things that needed to be said. 

Tara: Thank you, Shelby. Thank you so much. 

Shelby: Awww. Wasn't nothin'.
| Tara: Do ya think you might move closer to town now, Shelby? |
Shelby: Why would I do that? Nothing's changed. 

Tara: Are you serious? Everything's changed! 

Shelby: Don't rest on your laurels. There's always more things that need to be put back in balance.
| Tara: Do you have any Spring traditions, Shelby? |
Shelby: My family always used to do a dance 'round the Maypole. But every year one kid ran to the center and got woven into the pole by the others' ribbons. Then you had to unwind it all and get them free before they'd start wailin'...
| Tara: What a beautiful day! Birds are singing… |
Shelby: Yeh. Wish they wouldn't start so early. Can't sleep through all that racket!
| If ya sweep all ya problems under the rug, ya'll need a really big rug. Think on it. |
