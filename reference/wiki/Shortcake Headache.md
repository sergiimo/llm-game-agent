# Shortcake Headache

Shortcake Headache is a quest given by Angus Seton in the Summer.  It requires you to grow 6x Strawberry and deliver it to him so he can make a strawberry shortcake for the Summer Festival. The reward for completion is 3x Strawberry Shortcake.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Angus: Hey, I’ve heard you’re helping with the Summer Festival!
* Tara: Word gets around quickly in the down, doesn’t it?
* Angus: Every summer I make my giant strawberry shortcake for the whole town! It’s my own personal mission to make it bigger every year! One day I’ll beat the world record! I need strawberries, heaps and heaps of strawberries! It would be so awesome if you could spare some! Gah! I hope my flour delivery comes in time! 

Part 1: Angus needs strawberries for his Summer Festival shortcake recipe.

* Tara: Here are your strawberries, Angus.
* Angus: Aww, aren’t you sweet? Hehe, see what I did there? If you want a piece of shortcake, just come by the town square during the festival! 

Part 2: Angus wants you to come see him during the Summer Festival. Maybe you’ll get to taste that shortcake…?

* Tara: Wow, Angus, that really is a huge strawberry shortcake!
* Angus: Thanks! You get extra since you helped! Although… Cameron has been telling us that we really shouldn't celebrate the Summer Festival, since it's a folk tradition. He says old superstitions block our way to the Summit. Maybe this will be my last year...
* [Reward: 3x Strawberry shortcake]
