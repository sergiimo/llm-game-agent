"We buy and sell fish and tackle"; 
Soft and Son's Fish & Tackle is a small stall run by Bruno Soft, located to the left of Cafe Moretti. This is the primary source of bait in the game (and the only source of chub until the Mountain Overpass is unlocked) as Compost Bins do not supply a significant amount of worms and there is no other way to obtain crickets at this time. 
The name "Soft and Son's Fish & Tackle" refers to Bruno's son, Mayor Otto Soft, who is never seen interacting with the stand -- a source of minor contention between father and son.
