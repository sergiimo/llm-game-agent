  CharacterDescription: Sophia Moretti (36) is the warm, chatty proprietor of the town diner, Cafe Moretti, as well as Juliet's mother. She specializes in Italian Jewish dishes.

Sophia is Jewish and Italian-American, but has never visited Italy - she feels no need to travel. In contrast, her late husband Giacomo, was born in Italy. Ever since his death, she has struggled to adjust to life as a widow, and she sometimes goes to the graveyard to speak to Giacomo's grave. She's trying to keep her tiny family tight-knit, but she feels like Juliet is pushing her away.
