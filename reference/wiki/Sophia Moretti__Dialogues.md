# Sophia Moretti/Dialogues

CharacterNav
This page lists the dialogue lines for Sophia Moretti.

| Spring |
| Hello, I don't think I've seen you before! I run Café Moretti, the diner in town! Stop by anytime! |
Tara: Oh, hi, yeah, I'm new here. 

Sophia: Wait, you're Hazel's granddaughter! Welcome! I hope you come by soon. Moving always works up an appetite! OH! Where are my manners? I'm Sophia! 

Tara: Hey Sophia, I'm Tara. It's nice to meet you. I think I've gotten a bit turned around. Do you know the way to my Gramma Hazel's house? 

Sophia: Of course! It's back the way you came and then behind the statues head up the stairs. At the top of the stairs, turn left and keep walking till you get to your grandma's house.
| Just who I was hoping to see! |
Tara: Who, me? 

Sophia: Here, take this to Hazel.  

Tara: Oh, I couldn't… 

Sophia: Nonsense, take it. Your grandma and I have a friendly pie-rivalry of sorts. This is my answer to her last move: a devastatingly perfect key lime tart. I bake with love, but I also bake to win. 

Tara: Okay... I'll make sure she gets it. 

Sophia: Let me know how she likes it!
| Tara: This is the homiest diner I've ever been in. Do you run it all by yourself? |
Sophia: Oh, I've always been the only owner. Though my husband, Giacomo, used to help me. He passed two years ago. Pancreatic cancer. 

Tara: Oh, Sophia. I didn't know. 

Sophia: Hazel was so sweet to me. She knew what it was like on account of your nonno's passing.
| Juliet told me you said hello to her. Thank you! That girl is so shy. |
Tara: Well, teenagers have a lot going through their heads. It's no wonder some of them deal by just not talking. 

Sophia: Her father passing so unexpectedly hasn't helped…
| Tara: So when did you and Gramma start trying to out-bake each other? |
Sophia: We'd appreciated each other's skills ever since I moved here. That was, what, seventeen years ago? But when Giacomo was diagnosed, Hazel formally challenged me to a bake-off. She said it'd take my mind off things. And you know? She was right.
| So Bruno's given you a fishing rod too, huh? He tried to get Juliet into fishing, but she didn't take the bait. Poor guy. I think he's just lonely. |
| Mannaggia! This pan needs replacing… |
Tara: Sophia, you mentioned moving here - did you move here from Italy? 

Sophia: Oh, no. Giacomo did. So did my nonna, sixty years ago! But my mamma raised me in the suburbs outside the city. I still absorbed plenty of Italian slang. Like, this pan is a scocciamento! A pain!
| Last night, it took me an hour to find Juliet. And I found her just - sitting by the lake! Drawing! She didn't realize it was ten PM already. That girl. She was born with a caul over her face, did you know that? Worrying me from the moment she came into this world. |
| Have you met Violet and Sebastián? They're the sweetest kids. And way too independent, given their age. Since Gloria disappeared, I give them as much free food as they want. But I don't want to mother them too much. |
| This may sound funny, but the barstool on the far left is my favorite. That's where Giacomo sat the first time he walked into my diner fifteen years ago. He and Markus - Lina's father - were supposed to be talking about something serious, but Giacomo only had eyes for me... |
| Tara: If Giacomo is from Italy… what was he doing here fifteen years ago? |
Sophia: Oh, he was a friend of the Dahl's. Markus met him on a trip to Italy. Funny, Giacomo never mentioned why Markus was visiting… but I guess you don't need a reason to visit Italy!
| Juliet is a prodigy on that guitar, I swear! I know what you're thinking. "Sophia, you're her mamma! You have to say that!" But Juliet really does have a gift. Maybe someday, she'll let someone other than me hear her play… |
| I heard Hazel's been feeling worse. Are you doing okay? |
Tara: I - I think so. It's hard, honestly, because I'm the only one that lives with her. Like, what if she falls? Wow, that just popped out of me. I didn't even realize I was afraid of that. 

Sophia: Giacomo used to say I had that effect on people. They tell me all their worries. Well, I don't mind!
| Tara: Your mom still lives outside the city, right? Do you ever go visit her? |
Sophia: Oh, yes. On all the High Holidays, Juliet and I go stay with my mamma and visit the synagogue I went to as a kid. Juliet… does not care for the synagogue. But she doesn't mind the food! Mamma is an even better cook than me.
| Hazel told me that before I moved here, there was nowhere to get a cappuccino on the entire island. Can you imagine that?! |
| Cameron's a looker, isn't he? He hasn't been here much longer than you, but I wouldn't be surprised if he has admirers already. That Consciousness Center of his, though… I don't know about that place. |
| Things seem pretty tough with Hazel's health, sweetheart. You need a little comfort, you come here any time, okay? This diner is always open for you. |
| Oh hey, honey. Have you met my daughter Juliet? |
Tara: Yes! She was very nice and welcoming. 

Sophia: Are you sure that was her, then? That doesn't sound like Juliet.
| If you find your grandma's recipe book, I'd love a quick peek! |
| How are things going on your farm? I know I'm not the only chef excited about fresh local produce from Hazel's again! |
| Ruined again! Ugh! |
Tara: Is everything alright? 

Sophia: I burned the caramel again! Giacomo would always say I should just buy it in the store, but it's not the same! I could never serve a dessert made with caramel from a jar, like at some highway truck stop! I have pride, you know! 

Tara: I agree. Store caramel is basically the devil. 

Sophia: It is. It truly is.

| Summer |
| I'm so sorry about your grandma! She used to joke I would have to pry her secret cookie recipe from her cold dead hands… But I thought: that's just Hazel being Hazel, you know? |
Tara: It's okay. I'm fine, really. 

Sophia: You're being so brave. She would have been very proud. 

Tara: Thanks.
| Juliet says I spend too much time at the cemetary. But I find it comforting. If you ever want company when you go to visit Hazel, let me know. |
Tara: Thanks. I will.
| I know you don't sit shiva, but I wanted to give you this anyway. (Gives 1x Chicken Soup.) |
Tara: Thank you, Sophia. 

Sophia: As a community, it's our job to come lift you up in your time of mourning.
| Every time Juliet complains that she's bored, I just ask her to help me prep something for the Summer Festival! And magically, she's hardly ever bored anymore, ha! |
| Vanessa was in charge of the Summer Festival last year. It was very… classy and understated. |
Tara: That sounds… festive. 

Sophia: Not at all! At least it rained and we didn't have to feel guilty about leaving early.
| It's so hot that everyone is ordering milkshakes. I take pride in making them the old-fashioned way with hand-scooped ice cream. But ow. My right bicep is really sore! Giacomo used to say I could beat him at arm wrestling in the Summer. |
| Juliet can be a real pain in the neck sometimes… but you know what? The other day I turned my ankle and Juliet rubbed it a bit, and it instantly felt better! |
| Every Summer I tell myself to take advantage of Juliet's break to spend more time with her. But I think she's already reached the stage where hanging out with your mom is no longer cool. |
| Do you have a good relationship with your mom, Tara? |
Tara: We do now. But my teenage years were definitely a bit rough. 

Sophia: Well that's good to know! Maybe Juliet and I will get along better someday too.
| I was going to offer to set you up on a date but it doesn't look like you need my help! It's like I tell Natalia when she complains about being single: attitude is everything! |
| You know, when Thomas first started coming to my diner, he always took the seat at that booth that faced Lina's store. But now sometimes he sits at the counter instead. Still doesn't say much, though. |
| I'm ready for it to be Fall. It's peak pie season, after all! |
| Tara: I can't wait to see the bonfire, Sophia! |
Sophia: I know, it's even bigger than the one Vanessa put together last year! Not that it's a competition! But if it was I would win. All thanks to your help!
| You might just call it superstition, but Vanessa got married the year she was the Summertide Star! |
Tara: Oh, Vanessa was the Summertide Star? 

Sophia: Yes, five years ago, right after she moved here from Milkwater to work on Otto's campaign. But I think the Summer Festival is when she really caught his eye! Maybe you'll have similar luck!
| Every year, we spend so much time preparing the Summer Festival, and then it goes by in a blink! |
Tara: Yep, um, sure did! 

Sophia: My nonna used to say the same thing about making the Passover seder. But all the hard work is worth it if it brings the ones you love together.
| Juliet's out of school. I wish there was a half-decent summer camp nearby. It's a good thing there's a Summer festival at least. Young people need more things to do in this town. |
| It's so hot! Everyone is ordering apple pie a la mode without the pie! |
| Tara: Sophia, how are you and Juliet doing with the water shortage? |
Sophia: Oh, we're managing! I hate having to charge customers for water but I must admit we waste less of it.
| I'm not trying to gossip but… do you think all is well in the Dahl-Johnson home? I know Lina is busy, but I feel like Parker and the kids have been coming in without her more often. |
| It's finally hot enough to go to the beach! I love looking for seashells. |
| I'm still not ready to date again. Let me live vicariously through you! Anyone catch your eye yet? |

| Fall |
| Fall makes me nostalgic. It's the start of the busy season for truckers. |
Tara: I didn't figure you as nostalgic for truckers… 

Sophia: Business at the diner where my mamma worked would always pick up when the truckers came through! Imagine the smell of sandwiches grilling and sauce bubbling around the clock. Mmmm.
| I wish Angus and Francis were having one of those fancy ceremonies where everyone dresses to the nines. But not every wedding can be like mine! |
| I hear Angus and Francis aren't the only ones in love. Do you think you'll tie the knot soon, too? Please say yes! |
Tara: Sophia! I only moved here in Spring! 
| Don't tell Juliet, but I'm sad she's gone back to school. This summer, I sometimes persuaded her to come walking on the beach with me. She has an eye for the prettiest seashells! |
| The other day, Otto and Marty came into the diner for coffee at the same time. You should have seen the two of them. Trying to hard to be polite, but staring daggers at each other. You can tell they didn't grow up in Italian neighborhoods! They would've just started yelling and gotten it over with! |
| Tara: Any new Fall recipes at the café, Sophia? |
Sophia: You bet! Kai just brough over a bunch of new spices I can't wait to try!
| Oh, I love weddings! And that cake was divine! |
| I'm usually a big crier at weddings, but I managed to keep it together. Juliet would never have let me hear the end of it if I burst into tears during her song! |
| Tara: Juliet played so well at the wedding? How come I haven't heard more of her sick guitar solos? |
Sophia: It's the awkwardness, I'm telling you! That was the first time she's played in public. I'm just so proud of her!
| Another term with Otto in charge, huh? I don't have anything against Otto, but I would have liked Marty. Maybe he could've brought more young transplants to the island. |
| What have I ever done to Natalia except commiserate over all those worthless exes of hers? I didn't think we were as close as sisters, but I… I thought we were friends. |
| Now that I've had more time to process, you know what? I'm angry. At Natalia, sure, but Otto, too. What a jerk! All his neighborhood watch is doing is turning neighbors against each other! |
| I'm just worried sick. What if they all decide I'm a witch? What will happen to Juliet? |
| Tara: Hey Sophia, what's new? |
Sophia: Nothing! Absolutely nothing! I'm so bored with my menu I could cry. 

Tara: Well, why don't you add some new recipes?  

Sophia: Ooh, do you have any ideas? Did you inherit Hazel's gift in the kitchen? 

Tara: Nope, not me. I didn't inherit anything. 

Sophia: Oh well, that's too bad. Well, if you do have any food items you want to sell, let me know. I'll buy it if I can sell it in the cafe. At least then there'll be some variety.
| I hope Otto remembers the mistakes he made, letting people throw around accusations like they're nothing! But I have this feeling that he won't. |
| All this time, my diner has been the place where other people come for comfort. But where am I supposed to go if I need comfort? |
Tara: Oh, Sophia… 

Sophia: It's all right. I'll make it through this. For Juliet.
| I wish Hazel were still here. If she'd been in Town Hall when Natalia said what she did… |
Tara: Yeah. I literally shudder to think about what Gramma would have done.
| Tara: What are you making? There are so many smells! |
Sophia: Oh, some eggplant parmesan, hash browns, and a few beef turnovers from my mamma's recipe book… When I'm upset, I cook through it.

| Winter |
| Tara: Kim came by and asked me to help out with the Winter Feast. I don't blame you for not feeling up to organizing it this year, but do you think you might come? |
Sophia: I'm just not feeling up to it. Even if everyone in town didn't agree with Natalia's accusation, no one defended me. Not one person. 

Tara: I'm sorry, I... 

Sophia: It's fine. I don't blame you for not wanting to stick your neck out. I have a feeling this isn't over yet.
| You know what I will miss? I'll miss making a chocolate pie for the Winter Feast. |
Tara: Can't you make one anyway? 

Sophia: I could, but that would be so much pie just for Juliet and me! Now that I'm saying it out loud, it isn't the worst idea…
| I just can't get Juliet excited about Hanukkah anymore, at least not the first few nights. But I still have some surprises in store! |
| I can't help but wonder if Natalia would have accused me if Giacomo were still alive. Maybe a woman on her own is an easier target. But then, the fact that it was Natalia feels like even more of a betrayal. |
| Giacomo used to insist on doing Chismakkah and he would always get Parker to help him cut down the best pine tree and drag it home. It would fill our whole living room. I never thought I would miss it and the sap that got everywhere… but I do. |
| Tara, are you eating enough? Come by whenever you need a hot meal! |
| Juliet's been freaking ever since the ferry broke down. She keeps saying we should have just moved to Italy after my trial. As if you can "just" move to Italy! I've never even been there! And she didn't even seem to enjoy the one trip she took with her dad. |
| There isn't a decent deli 'round here for miles, but at least I can get Bruno to smoke some lox for me. |
| Giacomo had this way of making me laugh no matter how upset I was about something. Even when he was sick, he would make a face or crack some joke… He could have helped bring our community through this moment of crisis. I know it. |
| I heard Kim did a nice job with the Winter Feast. I'm glad. It took them a while to feel at home here, I think. I hope this helps. |
| Tara: Sophia, are you okay? |
Sophia: No, I'm definitely not okay. Now Angus has accused Marty? What is happening to this town? I always thought of Angus as this sweet, flour-covered teddy bear. I guess I don't know anyone here as well as I thought I did.
| Thomas has been grumbling that Marty probably is guilty. But he's obviously biased against him. I'm worried. Marty has even few friends here than I did at my trial. |
| Juliet's been saying she doesn't want to go to school anymore. But you can't just drop out at twelve! When I threatened to drop out of high school, I at least had the decency to wait until I was sixteen! Come to think of it, I guess I should just be grateful that she isn't attempting an ill-advised piercing or tattoo. |
Tara: You have a tattoo? Where? 

Sophia: I'm not telling!
| Juliet keeps arguing with me about her curfew. I can't even imagine where she wants to go. It's freezing out! |
| Staying inside every Winter makes me want to start a scrapbook. I just never have time to finish them. And sometimes they make me too sad. |
| Witch or not, I can't believe Marty came here hoping to turn our island into some fancy resort. I bet he was hoping to replace my diner with some trendy place that only serves avocado toast and matcha lattes. |
Tara: We would never let that happen, Sophia. 

Sophia: Damn right we won't.
| And Otto was in on Marty's scheme too. Technically their plan wasn't illegal, but what chutzpah! I'm starting to wonder if these witch trials were part of their plan. I've certainly considered leaving, ever since I was accused. |
| Cooking for everyone in Fairhaven has always made me happy. But now, when someone comes through the diner door, I feel this little sting of suspicion. Agh. What's happening to me? |
| I can't imagine how Lina must be feeling right now. She and I have never been friends, exactly. But Parker and Giacomo worked together every day. And poor Giva. Some welcome to our island! |
| How's your farm? It must be hard to work outdoors in this weather! Come see me if you ever need a cozy break. |
| Giacomo and I used to take Juliet over to Milkewater on the ferry for ice-skating lessons. |
| Lina does a wonderful job of keeping this store stocked. I only have to order a few diner staples from Milkwater. When your family's been running a store for generations, I guess you get pretty good at inventory. |
| I'm happiest around people! But I also love the quiet Winter mornings when the sun isn't up yet. It's just me, the kitchen, and the smell of challah French toast. |

| Epilogue |
| Hmmm, yeah… I've had an awful lot to think about lately. One thought that bubbled to the top was: of course Hazel was a witch. That's how she knew to bring you here. And you're the best thing that happened to this island in a long time. |
Tara: That's sweet, but- 

Sophia: I mean it. You helped save Juliet. You helped rescue Gloria. You helped drag Vanessa's evil, ungrateful tush back to Milkwater. You didn't do any of it alone. But without you, I'm not so sure any of it would've happened.
| Sometimes I look at Juliet and think, "My daughter's a witch!" I've been trying not to stare. I don't want to give her a complex. |
| I'm so angry at Giacomo for never telling me he was a witch! I wish he were here so I could give him a piece of my mind. And then give him a kiss. |
| Natalia did the sweetest thing this morning. She brought me a set of cast iron pans she'd made! And she apologized for what she said last Fall. |
Tara: How shocking! I had no idea that would happen! 

Sophia: She hadn't come into the diner for months. And to be honest, I don't know if I'd have let her. 

Tara: Natalia doesn't always know what to say. 

Sophia: This time, she said the exact right words. Gosh, I feel so much better!
| Juliet's more excited about school this Spring than she's ever been. She said Giva taught them how sound waves move through the air. If I'd known that all it took was to get her interested in STEM subjects-! |
| Now that the ferry's repaired, I can finally refill my condiment stocks. I had to ration the mustard! |
| Hey, Tara. I heard you grew the flowers Thomas gave me. Thanks, they're beautiful! |
Tara: You're very welcome! So, did you guys have dinner? 

Sophia: Yes, it was very nice! I never really thought I might be ready to move on, but he doesn't seem to mind if we take things slowly.
| Penne, fettuccine, linguini… but no bucatini. There's never any bucatini in stock! |
Tara: Did you just make up a new pasta shape? 

Sophia: No! Bucatini is one of the best pasta shapes because it's hollow. Double the sauce! Double the cheese!
