# Spring Ritual Incantation

[Potions Incantations]
  itemName: Spring Ritual Incantation
  description: Turns the season from Winter to Spring. It must be cast at the coven circle.
  recipe: 1x Parchment; 1x Black Ink; 5x Dragon's Blood Vial; 1x Primrose; 1x Adder Stone
  use: Change the season from Winter to Spring
  other: ==Other information==
The first time this incantation is delivered to the High Priestess, it will not be cast the same night. Winter crops can be left in the garden for one more day.
