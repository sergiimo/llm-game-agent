# Spring

Spring is the first season in the game and features Tara's first introduction to Fairhaven and its residents.

## Questlines
The following main quests occur during the Spring season:
* From Bud to Spud
* The Fungus Among Us
* The Most Elaborate Icebreaker
* Wood-n't You Know It
* Rice and Shine
* Steeped in Mystery
* You Must Be Plotting Something
* Where Have You Bean?
* Incantation Vexation
* What is the Wellspring?
* Something's Brewing
* Med Over Heels
* To Summer it Up For You
Spring Specific Quests:

* Not Today, Seitan
* Thistle Be The Best Bouquet Ever
* Paper Jam
* When Tulips Kiss
* We Make a Great Pair-ogi
