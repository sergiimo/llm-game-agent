## Summary
The following is a chronological overview of the events through the season. Major spoilers ahead -- continue at your own risk!&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;
Click to reveal
The season begins on a somber note, with the entire village gathered in the cemetery around the now-deceased Hazel's gravesite. After a cutscene in which Tara shares a few tearful words about her grandmother, the villagers remain standing around in the lower Town Square area, giving Tara the opportunity to go around and speak to everyone. Several characters give her gifts along with their condolence (for example, Chicken Soup from Sophia). All stores remain closed for the rest of the day.
