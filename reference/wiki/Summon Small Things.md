# Summon Small Things

[Potions Incantations]
  itemName: Summon Small Things
  description: An incantation that causes nearby wood, stones, shells and other small objects to jump into your bag! Lasts for a few days.
  recipe: 1x Paper; 1x Black Ink; 1x Weed; 1x Moon Flower
  use: Magnetic effect on forage and mined items on the ground
  duration: 4 days
  sellTo: Arcane Texts
  sellPrice: 15
  other: ==Other information==
The effect timer counts down each morning, with the first morning after casting displaying that three days remain. It is most effective to cast the spell in the morning, since the first day counts as a full day regardless of the time!
