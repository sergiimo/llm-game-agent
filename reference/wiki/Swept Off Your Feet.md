# Swept Off Your Feet

Swept Off Your Feet is a quest given by Zephyr Grimspark to unlock fast travel via magic broom. It requires you to deliver 1x Essence of Silver, 1x Levitation Potion, and the Broom from Gramma's room to her, then wait a day for her to enchant and balance the broomstick. 

Once you have the magic broomstick, you can fast travel to any point in Fairhaven at the cost of 1x Levitation Potion.
