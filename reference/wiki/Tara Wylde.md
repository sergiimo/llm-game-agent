Tara Wylde is the playable character and protagonist of Wylde Flowers. 

A young woman from the city who's just moved to the island of Fairhaven to help out on her grandmother's farm.

Shortly after Tara arrives in Fairhaven, she discovers that she's a witch. Her grandmother is part of a secret coven that meets in the woods. So, in addition to her farming duties and social life, Tara must also hone her magical abilities, brew potions and master flying on her broomstick.

### Life Before Fairhaven
Before arriving to Fairhaven, Tara was engaged and had a job. After losing her fiancé and job in the same week, she moves to Fairhaven to care for her grandmother.
