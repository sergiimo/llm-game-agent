# The Biggest (Only) Election in Fairhaven

The Biggest (Only) Election in Fairhaven is a quest given by Violet Miranda at the beginning of Fall. It acts as a 15-day countdown timer to the election between Otto Soft and Marty Emerson. 

Most characters will have an extra speech bubble with the election symbol in it that you can select to hear what they have to say about the election before and after the mayoral debate, which takes place 5 days before the election.

The candidate you choose to vote for does not affect the outcome of the election. The final vote count will remain the same regardless of whether you vote for Otto or Marty.
