# The Fairhaven Coven/Dialogues

| Overview | Dialogues |
The following is a list of quotes and conversations the player can have with other coven members throughout the game outside of quests, organized by character and season when the conversation takes place.

| Hey! You can call me the Acolyte. It's nice to meet you, but I'm not supposed to speak to you much yet! |
| It took me ages to get this far in the coven, but don't give up. You'll get the hang of it. |
| Don't go near the standing stones. Our coven has kind of a bad history with them. |
| I'm not the newest witch in the coven anymore! Now you have to run errands for everyone! |
Tara: Oh… that doesn't stop? 

Acolyte: Nope. It's in the fine print. Sorry. 
| Cast any good incantation spells lately? |
Tara: Er… no. 

Acolyte: Ugh, me either. I'm not very good at them. Potions, though? I'm a killer. 
| One time I messed up my proportions when I was making a big vat of Miracle Grow. I tested it on a mushroom, and it exploded to like… ten times its original size. |
Tara: What did you do? 

Acolyte: I stuffed the mushroom in my bag and took it home, duh. Fresh produce is expensive! 

| Summer |
| My familiar's started clawing at our back door and then prancing over to the couch like it's his. Sebastián's like, "Why is this cat so chill with you?" And I'm like… "Uh, who knows? Cats, dude." |

| Fall |
| Damon acts like he doesn't take coven stuff seriously. But he was the youngest witch initiated in a century. He's super talented. I don't know if we'd make it this year without him. |
| When I was younger, my mom used to take Sebastián and me out to Shelby's campfire. He'd tell us stories and he'd give us the best stew. When I was initiated, I realized what Mom had been doing. She was showing us that we didn't have to be afraid of Shelby. And now? I trust him more than, like... anyone. |
| I can't get my mom's face ouy of my mind. She seemed scared. Worried. But about what? Ugh! It's keeping me up at night! I need my beauty sleep! |
| Betcha never thought you'd be planting crops in the Gloaming! |
Tara: I'm still not sure it's safe. 

Herbalist Violet: Honestly? It probably isn't!
| Mom was - is - really good at acting calm, even if she doesn't know what to do. We'd probably all be less stressed if she was here. |

| Winter |

| You know, we should put up a sign that says "NO WRAITHS ALLOWED ON THIS PLANE OF EXISTENCE." |

## Adept
| Spring |
| I welcome you to this Gathering. |
| Watch out for the Journeyman. You're new and don't know better than to stay out of his schemes. |
| We cannot let the townspeople know our true identities. Witches have died this way. |
| Joining a new coven is thrilling. I felt that way myself when I was first initiated. Just make sure you're not running yourself ragged. There's more to life than magic. |
| The High Priestess may seem overly cautious to you. But we've lost too many members in recent years. We need every witch we have to keep the Wellspring balanced. |
| As with all common knowledge, witchcraft mythology is sprinkled with half-truths and outright fabrications. |
| Your grandmother never approved of the masks outside of rituals. She wasn't really the shrinking violet type. And I suspect you're not either, are you? |
| What kind of witch will you be? |
Tara: A good witch! 

Adept: There are no good witches or bad witches. Only good or bad actions. 

| Summer |
| Tara: Does every witch here just accept that seasons have to get turned? Like mattresses? |
Adept: Hah! No, not at all. It was hard for me to accept, too. What I learned is that places close to Wellsprings are easily untethered from the realities of our world. For example? Weather. 
| Tara: So if I use my boat to get to Ravenwood Hollow… how does everyone else get there? |
Adept Amira: A different form of transportation reveals itself to each of us over time. Mine, for example, is a funicular. And no one can see it but me! 

Tara: Wow. I thought my boat was cool. Now I find out it could have been a bullet train or a penny farthing bike... 
| Tara: What about you, Adept? Is your familiar around here somewhere? |
Adept Amira: Of course! She's a red fox that Aryel rescued from a trap a few years ago. She's a little skittish, but I adore her. 

| Fall |
| The Farseer's advice may seem unclear… But if you really listen, everything he says makes sense in the end. |
| I've seen many people possessed by jinn. But a disembodied wraith whooshing right by my face on its way to find a victim? That's a new one for me. |

| Winter |
| Did you know that women healers were burned for being witches for centuries, even when many of them weren't witches? Strange. A woman who can heal terrifies people. I try not to think too much about what that means. |
| There's an Iranian festival, a week before the New Year, where we burn rue and frankincense. I wonder if it started as a way to appease local Wellsprings? Just as we're doing with the pine cones. |

Farseer: Name's Farseer. I see. Far. 

Tara: Like, you've got great vision? 

Farseer: Visions. 

Tara: Right… okay, nice to meet you, Farseer. 
| Light follows the darkness. Remember that. |

## High Priestess
| Spring |
| Tara: Um, excuse me, Your Magicalness? |
High Priestess: High Priestess works fine, you know. 

Tara: Okay, well… High Priestess, how long has this coven been in Fairhaven? 

High Priestess: Witches have always been drawn here. And because of that, the coven has been here for many, many generations. 
| As you may have sensed, your grandmother doesn't believe in keeping our identities secret. It's a noble idea, but I've concluded that it isn't practical. |
| You are forbidden to approach the standing stones. |
Tara: Why's that? 

High Priestess: It is not for you to know. 

Tara: Who is it for… to… know? 

High Priestess: That isn't your concern. 
| Tara: Who are you really? |
High Priestess: Persist in your studies with the coven and you will know one day. 
| Witchcraft can make any task easier. It can make the lives of those around you easier. You must keep working at it. But don't ignore the rest of your life. It is a balance. |
| Tara: Are you sure I can handle finding the things you need for something as important as changing the seasons? |
High Priestess: Why do you doubt yourself? 

Tara: I guess my life was kind of falling apart when I came to Fairhaven, so… 

High Priestess: But look at everything you've done since you got here. 
| The forest is full of useful ingredients for potions. Do not neglect its bounty. |
| Our ways must seems foolish and secretive to one who has not grown up in the coven. |
Tara: A little. 

High Priestess: We must wear disguises, we must meet in secret and we must be careful of who we let into our order. To fail in these duties is to invite scrutiny from others. Then there is fear. Fear is dangerous and in the wrong hands, a weapon. It has always been this way. 

| Summer |
| We've lost many witches over the last few years. I thought that someday, I'd get used to the ache those absences leave behind. But I never do. |
| Tara: Would I still need to figure out every witch's identity if I had grown up in Fairhaven? |
High Priestess: No. We'd have a better sense of your trustworthiness. You've been a welcome addition to the coven thus far, but it hasn't been a fill season since you began coming here! 
| So… I hear you were the one who welcomed the meteorologist to town, Tara. |
Tara: Yeah, Otto volun-forced me for the job. But Giva seems nice, so I didn't mind. 

High Priestess: She may be nice, but her presence here could be dangerous. She came here to look into Fairhaven's weather patterns. We cause those weather patterns. 

Tara: (Isn't it harmless?) She's just researching! It's harmless, right? 

High Priestess: Maybe. And maybe not. Maybe it's a front. If she's a competent meteorologist, she'll dig far enough into the island's weather that she just might find us at the center. We should all keep an eye on her discoveries. 

Tara: Wait. No. No. I don't think that's what Gramma would have wanted. Didn't she warn us against this exact attitude? Even if Giva does uncover something that points to us, that isn't necessarily bad. Maybe she's trustworthy. 

High Priestess: I wish I agreed with you. But as witches, we can never be too careful. We're not only responsible for our own safety, but the safety of the entire island. Even the parts beyond the veil. 
| Tara: So the head of the Fairhaven coven is my former babysitter, Lina- |
High Priestess Lina: Don't say it! 

Tara: But no one can hear us out here. And you're not wearing your mask! 

High Priestess Lina: It's the principle of the thing. 
| Tara: Do you have a familiar, High Priestess? |
High Priestess Lina: I do indeed. But she's a cormorant, and she spends most of her time over the water. You're not likely to see her. 
| Be certain that no one sees you coming or going from this new place. With Otto's new Neighborhood Watch, we can't be too careful. |

| Fall |
| I wish I had been with you and the others when you found that Echo Blossom. Gloria was a respected and powerful member of the coven, but she was also my friend. |
| What would my dad say if he knew the coven had let a wraith slip through under my leadership? |
Tara: He'd probably say, "This could have happened to anyone." 

High Priestess Lina: You're probably right. But… still.
| Don't delay in gathering the supplies we'll need to banish the wraith from Natalia. I want to believe that she and Otto will stop before they go too far. But we can't take risks. |
| Thank you for helping us save Sophia. And Natalia. They may not know what you did, but I won't forget. |
| The Wellspring has challenged us more this Fall than any season I can remember. And yet… Winter is often the most difficult season of the year… |

| Winter |
| Please be responsible when calling storms, Novice. Hazel used to call them whenever she didn't feel like watering her crops. It was a bit of a problem. |
| Tara: Zephyr finished the improvements on my wand, High Priestess. |
High Priestess Lina: I am glad to hear it. We will need you to be at your full power to face what is to come.
| Tara: How do you know that another witch is going to just - join us? |
High Priestess Lina: The Wellspring has a way of calling witches from all over the world. Sometimes, people who move here don't even realize that they're witches. Like you.
| Tara: I hope we can convince Francis to come back. That was a pretty shocking way of learning he's a witch. |
High Priestess Lina: Ideally, every witch's discovery of their powers would be more like yours. Someone you love and trust helps you realize what you're capable of. But right now, we don't have time for that kind of gentleness.
| Angus tries not to show it, but he carries a powerful fear within him. He's pushed it away to try to keep himself safe, I'm sure. Unfortunately, that's had the opposite effect. |
| The nights get longer, and the Wellspring gets harder and harder to balance… |
| Tara: High Priestess… what happens if we can't turn the seasons? |
High Priestess Lina: I've asked Westley to research that for us. But he hasn't turned up anything helpful yet.
| I've tried my best to keep my role in the coven separate from my family life. Maybe that was a mistake. But it's a mistake that's too late to undo. |
