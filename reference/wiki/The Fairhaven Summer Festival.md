# The Fairhaven Summer Festival

The Fairhaven Summer Festival is a quest delivered by letter from Otto Soft at the beginning of Summer. It begins a 15-day countdown to the Summer Festival. The quest has no objectives, but can be checked at any time so see the number of days left until the festival.

## Letter Contents
* [constituent’s name here]
* You are cordially invited to attend the annual Fairhaven Summer festival.
* It’s in just a few weeks!
* Hope to see you there!
* Sincerely,
* Mayor Otto Soft
