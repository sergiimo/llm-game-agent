# The Fennel Word on the Matter

Can’t Hold a Candle is a quest given by Peri in the Winter. It requires the player to deliver 3x Fennel Powder to her so she can cast a protective charm on Ravenwood Hollow. The reward for completion is 3x Restore Magic Potion.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Peri: I hope you’re here because you’re preparing an offering for the Wellspring. The coven cannot allow a wraith to escape from The Gloaming again.
* Tara: We didn’t… I mean maybe we did… we still don’t know why the Wellspring isn’t balanced.
* Peri: Ravenwood Hollow cannot be at the mercy of your incompetence. Once your coven had a member who gave me fennel so that I might cast a charm to block wraiths from entering our village… But now all I have left is a small packet of fennel seeds. It won’t make enough fennel seed powder for my charm. Perhaps you could grow some fennel in your garden, and grind me some more fennel seed powder. [Gives 3x Fennel seeds.] It certainly is the least you could do.
* Tara: Um… yes, ma’am. 

Quest completion:

* Peri: Have you the fennel, witchling?
* Tara: Yes, here it is. And Peri? Could you teach me the charm? Maybe I could place the same one around the coven circle.
* Peri: And why would you do that?
* Tara: Um… to block out the wraiths?
* Peri: Tell me, when the wraith escaped through the stones, did it try to possess a member of the coven?
* Tara: No, it went after Natalia.
* Peri: Correct.
* Tara: But I can’t put fennel seed powder around the entire island!
* Peri: I don’t pretend to be an expert at protecting mortals. Perhaps you should speak with your High Priestess.
* [Reward: 3x Restore Magic Potion]
