# The Fungus Among Us

The Fungus Among Us is a quest given by Gramma. It requires you to find 2x Mushroom and deliver them to her. The day after completing the quest, you will receive 2x Cream of Mushroom Soup.; 

Quest: Gramma has asked for mushrooms in order to make something tasty.; 
Hint: There should be mushrooms not too far off the path North of the farm.

Quest intro:

* Gramma: Oh, and I was thinking about cooking up some mushrooms. How would you like mushroom soup for dinner?
* Tara: Mmm. Is it the same one you used to make when I was little? Because if it is, yes please!
* Gramma: Lovely. You’ll find plenty of mushrooms good for eating if you head up that mountain path a little.
* Tara: Wait, we have to pick the mushrooms ourselves?
* Gramma: This isn’t the city, honeybee! Besides, freshly-picked mushrooms taste better.
* Tara: Yeah, if they’re not the kind that poison you.
* Gramma: You’ll do just fine!
* Tara: I’ll do my best! Is there anything else I can do for you?
* Gramma: Well, if you wanted to get some new seeds in town, it might be a good time to stop by and see the Mayor, introduce yourself. He’s at Town Hall. A big building in the center of town with a flag above the door. You can’t miss it!
* Tara: I’ll take good care of you and this farm, Gramma.
* Gramma: I’m just so glad you’re here my sweet little honey bee!
* [Gramma leaves]
* Tara: Alright, here I am… farming… This place has changed so much… it’ll take a lot of work to get it back to how I remember. I don’t know if city life has prepared me for this. But I’m ready to give it my best!

Quest completion:

* Gramma: Spring is the time when the earth reawakens. You can forage for the gifts the land offers up, if you know where to look. Have you found any mushrooms?
** [Choice: No, nothing yet]
* Tara: Sorry, Gramma, I haven’t found any yet, but I’ll keep an eye out for them.
* Gramma: Oh. Well, that’s ok, I don’t have much of an appetite these days anyway. You should take better care of yourself, though. I hate to see you go hungry.
** [Choice: I found some mushrooms]
* Tara: Will these work?
* Gramma: Those are nice plump ones! You’re a natural forager.
* Tara: When I was out searching, I came across that old mine. You used to tell me that there were goblins inside, remember?
* Gramma: I told you that, did I?
* Tara: Haha! It was a smart way of keeping a curious little girl out of danger!
* Gramma: The wood in the supports rotted clean through. A shame – I’m sure there’s still perfectly good iron and copper down there. Come back tomorrow and I’ll have the mushroom stew prepared. I’m a bit tired just now.
