# The Reluctant Lycanthrope

The Reluctant Lycanthrope is a quest given by Westley Vuk after completing the quest Oh, Chute. It requires you to deliver 3x Mulberry<nowiki/>to him. The reward for completion is 200 coins.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Westley: Welcome to my humble little shop. See? I really am a rare bookseller! Just – very rare books.
* Tara: I used to have fantasies about browsing books in stores like this! What kind of books are these?
* Westley: Many of them are incantation collections. There’s some history of magic and magical theory mixed in, too. Ooh, and detective stories. I love a good detective story! By the way, the last time I was in town, Kai mentioned he’d sold you a mulberry seedling. Do you have any extra mulberries?
* Tara: I’ll check. What are you making?
* Westley: Oh – just an incantation.
* Tara: That’s not vague at all.
* Westley: It’s for – well, I – you see…
* Tara: It’s okay. You don’t have to tell me! As long as you’re not making, I don’t know, some magical bomb.
* Westley: I would never!
* Tara: I was joking! Mostly. Let me see about those mulberries. 

Quest completion:

* Tara: Here you are, Westley. Enough mulberries for an entire pie.
* Westley: Blech. What I’m making is much more interesting.
* Tara: And what you’re making is…
* Westley: …
* Tara: … oh, fine.
* Westley: Okay, okay! I’ve been researching ways to lessen the effects of lycanthropy. That is, being a werewolf. I would prefer to be a little less, ahem, werewolfish.
* Tara: But you have such lovely fur!
* Westley: That’s kind of you. However, being a were-creature comes with plenty of downsides. One of the texts I read suggested that an incantation made with ink of the mulberry might help.
* Tara: I hope you get what you want.
* Westley: Me, too. Here, take this. Fair is fair.
* [Reward: 200 coins]
