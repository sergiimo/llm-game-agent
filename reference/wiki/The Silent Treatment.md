# The Silent Treatment

The Silent Treatment is a quest given by Thomas and Marty in the Winter. It requires the player to brew 1x Silencing Potion for Westley so he can stop howling at night. The reward for completion is 215 coins.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: Hi there Marty… and Thomas?
* Marty: I bet you never thought you’d see the two of us together!
* Thomas: Hmmph.
* Marty: But we share a common problem. We’ve heard howling coming from this area every night lately! If you don’t mind, can we cut through your property to see if we can find any wolf tracks? Wolves are a threat to all the livestock on the island.
* Thomas: And I want to make sure they’re captured humanely and relocated to a proper habitat.
* --[Choice: Huh?]
** Tara: Howling? What howling?
** Marty: You haven’t heard it? It sounds like it’s coming from… well you lake, which doesn’t make any sense! Maybe there’s a hidden cave somewhere?
* --[Choice: Sure]
** Tara: Go ahead, but I don't think you'll find anything. 
**Marty: Well, that howling has to be coming from somewhere.
* Thomas: We’re going to check the mountain pass area first. We’ll let you know if we find anything.
* Tara: Uh oh. If they start looking around the lake they might accidentally wind up in Ravenwood Hollow! I better check up on Westley to see what’s going on.

Ask Westley about the mysterious howling.

* Tara: Westley, some of my neighbors have been complaining of howling lately. Everything okay?
* Westley: Oh, I’m so sorry, Tara! Now that it’s Winter, nights have gotten longer, and I’m running out of my stock of potion!
* Tara: What’s in it? Maybe I could brew you up a batch!
* Westley: I don’t know. I got it from a witch I met before I moved to Ravenwood Hollow.
* Peri: I think I might know the recipe. But it will requires wolfsbane from The Gloaming.
* Tara: One No-Howling potion coming right up! So do you think this will really work?
* Peri: Look, even in magic some thing have, what do you humans call it, a “placebo” effect? But if it works, it works. And it would be nice to have more peace and quiet around here.

Make a silencing potion to help out Westley.

Give the silencing potion to Westley.

* Tara: Got somethin’ for ya, Westley.
* Westley: Oh Tara, thank you. It was really kind of you to help me like this.
* Tara: Hey, it helps us too. If the Fairhaven folks are this upset about witches… I can’t imagine what they’ll do if they find out there is a werewolf close to their town!
* Westley: I’m sorry if I caused you any trouble with your neighbors.
* [Reward: 215 coins]
