# The Unjolly Rancher

The Unjolly Rancher is a quest that that acts as a 7-day countdown timer, starting once the questline of Witch You Were Here is completed and another wraith has escaped. Mayor Otto calls an emergency town hall meeting about the broken ferry, and Marty is accused of witchcraft by a wraith-possessed Angus. 

The player must complete the questline Seton's Secrets before time is up to banish the wraith from Angus.
