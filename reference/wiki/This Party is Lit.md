# This Party is Lit

[Quest]
  title: This Party is Lit
  type: To-Do Quests
  start: Sophia Moretti
  end: Thomas Lightfoot
  level: Summer
  rewards: 1x Apple Pie
  previous: ?
  next: A Corny Request

This Party is Lit is a quest given by Sophia Moretti in early Summer. It requires you to deliver 25x Wood to Thomas so he can build the bonfire for the Summer Festival. The reward for completion is 1x Apple Pie.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Sophia: Morning! I’ve got exciting news! I’ve been named this year’s Captain of the Summer Festival!
* Tara: Wow. So, does being captain mean you get to ride on a parade float?
* Sophia: No, but I DO get to organize everything and make sure the festival is perfect!
* Tara: Sounds… like a lot of work.
* Sophia: Oh, it is! Francis did it one year and almost had a nervous breakdown! Hey hon, I need your help! The festival is a daylong celebration, culminating in a big bonfire down by the beach! I need wood – lots and lots of wood! Please bring any you can spare to Thomas, he’s volunteered to help assemble the bonfire. Thanks so much! You’re the best! 

Part 1: Gather wood for the Summer Festival bonfire and deliver it to Thomas.

* Tara: Here’s the wood for the bonfire, Thomas.
* Thomas: Thanks.
* Tara: It’s really nice of you to help Sophia out.
* Thomas: If that’s bait, washashore, I’m not bitin’.
* Tara: Well, nice talking to ya, Thomas! 

Part 2: Let Sophia know you’ve dropped off the wood and she’ll give you something sweet.

* Tara: I gave Thomas the wood you needed.
* Sophia: Lovely! Here, this is fresh out of the oven. You deserve it! 
* [Reward: 1x Apple Pie]
