# Thistle Be The Best Bouquet Ever

[Quest]
  title: Thistle Be The Best Bouquet Ever
  type: To-Do Quest
  start: Violet Miranda
  end: Violet Miranda
  location: Violet's Violets
  item_needs: 3x Thistle
  rewards: 5x Tulip Bulb

Thistle Be The Best Bouquet Ever is a quest given by Violet Miranda. It requires you to deliver 3x Thistles to her. The reward for completion is 5x Tulip Bulbs.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Vanessa: I place a monthly order for fresh flowers, Violet. It’s not like I asked for a rush job.
* Violet: I’m sorry. It’s just that the ferry is running behind schedule… again. Maybe if the ferry wasn’t underfunded, this wouldn’t happen.
* Vanessa: Make excuses all you like. Late is late, as I tell my students.
* [Vanessa leaves]
* Tara: Bad time…?
* Violet: Ugh. No. It’s fine. I just – when my dad was mayor, the ferry always ran on time. Hey, do you have any wildflowers growing on your farm? Maybe I could make some substitutions.
* Tara: Sort of. How do you feel about thistles?
* Violet: You’re suggesting I make a bouquet out of thistles? Thistles?!
* Tara: Look, if you don’t want them-
* Violet: Did you hear me say ‘no’? The sooner I can get Vanessa out of my hair, the better. 

Quest completion:

* Violet: So? Anything I can put in my bouquets?
** [Choice: I’m still looking]
* Violet: Please tell me you have the thistles I asked for.
* Tara: They’re harder to find than I thought.
* Violet: Well, hurry up! The hothouse blooms still haven’t come. And I think Vanessa’s planning some fancy dinner. So she’s not gonna let this one go.
** [Choice: Yes]
* Tara: Stress no more, I found just the thing. See? They’re pretty!
* Violet: Yes, those will do nicely! It’s hard sometimes, filling all these orders to decorate the house I grew up in.
* Tara: Does the mansion go with the job?
* Violet: You got it. Anyway, thanks. You saved my butt.
* [Reward: 5 Tulip Bulbs]
