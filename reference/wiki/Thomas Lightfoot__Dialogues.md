# Thomas Lightfoot/Dialogues

CharacterNav
This page lists the dialogue lines for Thomas Lightfoot.

| Spring |
| Tara: Hi! I'm Tara, Hazel Wylde's granddaughter. Nice to meet you. |
Thomas: I remember ya. Used to run past my farm on the way to the beach when you were little. 

Tara: Yes! And Gramma always told me to mind that I didn't trample your crops. You're Thomas, right? 

Thomas: Yep. 

Tara: Your farm has the most stunning view on the whole island! 

Thomas: I'm not selling. 

Tara: I didn't mean... 

Thomas: Your gramdma needs help on her farm. But it's not as easy as just moving here and picking up a pitchfork. 

Tara: I know. I'm here to learn.  

Thomas: For Hazel's sake, I hope you learn quick.
| Tara: Hi! It's me again. I've been trying to help out my Gramma… But I don't know much about farming at all. |
Thomas: Yep. Like I said. Not so easy. Hazel's got some of the best soil on the island. Shame for it to go to waste. Look city girl, just water your plants, and you'll do fine.
| Tara: Maybe I just don't have a good memory. But when I was little, didn't your farm look different? |
Thomas: Probably. I was growin' more kinds of crops back then. That was right after my Mom left the farm to me. I was ambitious in those days! Tried growing a little bit of everything. Now I grow what actually suits the soil.
| Tara: Is it just you on your farm, Thomas? |
Thomas: Sure is. My dad passed around the same time as your grandpa, and my mom moved in with my sister near the rez in Milkwater. Stayin' here was too painful for her.
| I was s'posed to visit my sister in Milkwater today, but she said she's too busy. Figures. |
Tara: She does that a lot? 

Thomas: At least once a month. But Crystal really is a busy lady. She teaches music at the university. Wish she'd come out here more often. But she says the ferry makes her seasick.
| Tara: So how long's the farm been in your family, Thomas? |
Thomas: My dad used to come over on the ferry to work in the mine after the harvest was done for the year. One day Hazel mentioned the land was for sale. Mom wasn't too sure about leavin' our family and community back in Milkwater... But once she saw the view she knew Dad had found somethin' special.
| Well, now. Your first potatoes didn't look all that bad. |
Tara: Thanks, I think. And how'd you know? 

Thomas: I stopped by Lina's not long after you. We were admiriin' your work. No rot. Not too many corky spots on the tubers. 

Tara: Oh. I was definitely checking them over for rot and, um, corky spots.
| Good thing you learned how to fish from Bruno. When I was a kid, the fisheries were focused on getting' bigger and bigger. And when they got too big, they went outta business. Bruno never did that. He fished as much as he needed. No more. |
| Hazel's a good cook, but if you want a meal so big you'll hardly be able to walk afterwards, go to Sophia's. And at the end of a day plantin' seeds, you'll want that kind of meal, trust me. |
| Tara: In a good mood today, Thomas? |
Thomas: I am, I am. Just got back from Milkwater. We were all celebratin' the New Year. 

Tara: Oh! Happy New Year!  

Thomas: I mighta overdone it, though. Ate so much herring, you could probably roll me back to the farm.
| Can't believe it. Kim's been sayin' I don't like them! |
Tara: I mean, you don't. Do you? 

Thomas: I just told them what I thought! But now I hear they're spreadin' rumors about me… no. I don't like them.
| Sebastián saw I was strugglin' with my wheelbarrow the other day. He hopped the fence just to give me a hand. Good to see that Violet kept up her parents' work. Sebastián's turnin' out just fine. |
| Tara: How's it going, Thomas? |
Thomas: Just finishin<nowiki></nowiki> up the potato harvest, since it's nearly Summer. The leaves on the plants turned out so pale. Means they're nitrogen-deficient. After all the fertilizer I added, too…
| Have ya met Parker yet? |
Tara: You mean Lina's husband? 

Thomas: … Yep. He'll help you build a tool shed when you're ready. 

Tara: Oh, I already have a shed.  

Thomas: … If ya wanna call that a shed, I won't argue with ya. He can build you other things ya might need too.
| Not everyone is as easygoing about folks tresspassing onto their land as I am. Mind whose property you wander onto. |
| That butcher Kim thinks I have it out for them, but I don't I said they should hunt what they butcher, and they made some joke about how impractical that is… And somehow I'm the one bein' disrespectful of others? |
| Tara: Hey… |
Thomas: Still here, washashore? 

Tara: I sure am! 

Thomas: Better mind the ebb tide.
| Tara: Hi Thomas! I'm settling in to life on the farm. Lina's been very encouraging. |

| Summer |
| Tara: Excited for Summer, Thomas? |
Thomas: Well, it brings its own challenges. My mom taught me to use trees to shade your crops, especially in Summer. So I've been checkin' in with every tree. Havin' a little chat. Makin' sure they're healthy.
| Tara: So were you and Line ever…? |
Thomas: Ugh. Hazel told you about my high school dating life? That was a long time ago. And she moved on. Parker, he's a stand-up guy. 

Tara: But did you move on? 

Thomas: Sure.
| My dad helped out with the Summer Festival. Once. He never did it again. Said it was too much work for a middlin' party. |
| I left my best spade outside in the rain last week. Rookie mistake. Leads to rust. But when I went to get it, the thing was propper up outside my door. Dry as a bone. Oddest thing… |
| Out of all the ranchers Otto coulda hand-picked to move into the ranch… he chose Marty? I asked if his animal husbandry program had any classes on sustainable ranchin.' And he said, "I'll make things sustainable one day. Once I get this place off the ground." We all know what 'one day' means, Marty Emerson. |
| So. I hear you're raisin' livestock, too. |
Tara: Are you upset? 

Thomas: Not as long as you treat your animals right. And keep 'em away from my corn plants.
| There hasn't been anyone up in that weather station for goin' on three decades. I hope Giva doesn't mind dust bunnies for company. |
| I asked Marty how many animals he planned to have. He said, as many as possible. Does he know what all that run-off will do to my soil? Eugh… |
| Tara: Thomas, I hope this isn't too intrusive, but your land - Gramma mentioned it used to be, well, different. |
Thomas: Back when my dad moved us here, a ridge protected it from the sea. But that ridge crumbled, and all that seawater… it isn't good for the crops. So I do what I can. I'll make it work.
| Kai gave me some seaweed in exchange for cutting across my farm every mornin'. Seaweed's a potent fertilizer. I appreciate him lookin' out for me. |
| Tara: It's strange living alone again. I suppose I'll get used to it in time. |
Thomas: I'm too set in my ways to live with someone. And I've grown accustomed to the quiet.
| Were you the one who helped restore the ranch so that pretender in designer muck boots could move in? |
Tara: Um…. 

Thomas: Great. Just great.
| Thomas: You know what's worse than getting your socks soaked out in the fields? |
Tara: What? 

Thomas: It was an honest question. I can't think of anything worse.
| Here's some popcorn. No butter, and we ran out of salt… |
Tara: It's… great. Thanks, Thomas.
| You know, I was the Summertide Star once. |
Tara: Are you joking? Honestly, I can't tell if you're joking. 

Thomas: Scuse you, Tara. Anybody can be the Summertide Star. It's all about the attitude.
| Sophia's an inspiration. She spent so much time captainin' the Summer Festival this year. |
Tara: That's all you're inspired by? Her work ethic? 

Thomas: I see what you're tryin' to do. Well, I'm not answerin'.
| Careful with your crops now. Sun's hot and they won't water themselves. I used to swear your grandma's did though. |
| Summer's almost over, so don't go leavin' your crops in the ground. The Fall air will shrivel them right up. |

| Fall |
| Temperatures 'r droppin', but we still got Fall crops to get in the ground. After all… planting season's not over lentil it's over. |
Tara: Thomas… did you just make a pun? 

Thomas: Argh. I knew that baker was gonna rub off on me if I kept stoppin' by for croissants!

Tara: You thought they'd break up? 

Thomas: No, no. I thought they'd move away. Establishin' two businesses at once - well, they're beatin' the odds financially and romantically. So... good for them.
| Tara: So, Thomas. Did you ever consider moving anyone into your farmhouse? |
Thomas: What, like my sister? I asked Crystal if she'd move back, but- 

Tara: No, no. I mean, like… marriage. 

Thomas: Oh. Well. Not yet, but I go on plenty of dates. I do! Don't look at me like that!

| I caught Marty's cows eatin' my soybean seedlings. Again. He says he's runnin' a grassroots campaign. Well, can't his cattle just leave my roots alone? |
| We're real lucky Sophia landed on Fairhaven all those years ago. Who would I get my pumpkin pie from, otherwise? |

| I couldn't make it to Ancestors' Day this year. Had to repair my rototiller. So Crystal made her girls call me. There's no guilt trip stronger than your little nieces beggin' you to come visit soon… |
| Tara: Relieved that Fall is here, Thomas? |
Thomas: Hmmph. Droughts have been going on longer every year. People say the weather is the same every year but you can't fool a farmer.
| I don't know what to make of the debate. On the one hand, I don't want folks runnin' around the woods at night, messin' them up. On the other… I'm no fan of curfews. |
| My sister got married young and divorced soon after. It just wasn't the right match from the start. Sad to see somethin' start out with hope and end in sorrow. Francis and Angus though… I think they'll make it. |
| All those heavy metals have gone to Natalia's brain. She needs a vacation. Or at least a swift dunk in the lake. That'll bring her to her senses. |

Tara: I would never, Thomas. Farmer's honor.

| I'm gonna find the folks who are really out there in the woods, whether I'm a part of the Neighborhood Watch or not. I'm not lettin' Sophia, or anyone else, shoulder the blame. |
| You think Kim could be the one out in the woods at night? Maybe they're huntin' without a permit- |
