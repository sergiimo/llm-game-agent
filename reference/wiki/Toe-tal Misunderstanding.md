# Toe-tal Misunderstanding

Toe-tal Misunderstanding is a quest given by Kim and Marty in the epilogue. It requires the player to brew truth serum-laced Malasadas to give to Aryel to get him to admit to being the one that harassed Kim and Marty after they moved to Fairhaven. The reward for completion is 210 coins. 

The recipe for Malasadas can only be found through fishing up bottles, and which recipe a bottle contains is a matter of luck. 

The recipe for Truth Serum Malasadas requires 1x Malasada, 1x Wool Weed, 1x Wolfsbane, and 1x Dragon's Blood Vial. Wool Weed and Wolfsbane can be found past the second pillar in the Gloaming, and Dragon's Blood Vials can be obtained by tapping dragon's blood trees in the third section of the Gloaming.
