# Tool Shed

## Tool Shed
The tool shed is a space next to Tara's farmhouse where she can build a variety of crafting stations to refine resources. Originally 5x6, it can be upgraded by Parker Johnson to have a useable layout of 7x12 (total size 9x12). The upgrade costs 50x wood and 200 coins and requires Tara to clear the surrounding space, which may call for a wood axe and/or pickaxe. The upgrade will be complete on the second morning after purchasing.
