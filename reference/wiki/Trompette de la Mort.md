# Trompette de la Mort

[Forage]
  itemName: Trompette de la Mort
  description: This magic mushroom is only found in the Gloaming, and can be used in powerful spells.
  location: Gloaming
  other: ==Information==
The Trompette de la Mort can be found to the West on the first level of the Gloaming, inside a Fey Circle. Use a Shrinking Potion to shrink down and enter the Fey Circle, collect the mushrooms there, and use a Stretching Potion to return to normal size.
