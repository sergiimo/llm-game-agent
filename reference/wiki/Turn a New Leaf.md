# Turn a New Leaf

Turn a New Leaf is a quest given by the High Priestess during the Summer after progressing the quest For Crying Drought Loud. It requires you to craft 1x Fall Ritual Incantation and 1x Spirit Flame. The reward for completion is an Increase Magic spell, which permanently increases your maximum magic. 

To craft the Fall Ritual Incantation, you will need the Deep Mine Key the High Priestess gives you to travel deeper in the Mine in order to get a Ruby (available on floors 15-19).
