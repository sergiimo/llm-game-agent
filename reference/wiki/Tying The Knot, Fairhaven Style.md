# Tying The Knot, Fairhaven Style

Tying The Knot, Fairhaven Style is a romance quest given by Lina Dahl-Johnson after you gain seven full hearts with any romanceable character. It requires you to craft visit Lina at her store, where she will explain the traditional Fairhaven proposal customs. You can then buy a wedding ring mold from Francis and take it to Natalia, who will forge you a ring if you provide her with the raw materials of 3x Iron Ingot and 2x Gold Ingot.  

The wedding will take place 7 days after the proposal.
