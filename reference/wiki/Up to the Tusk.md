# Up to the Tusk

[Quest]
  title: Up to the Tusk
  start: Kim Izumi
  end: Aryel
  location: Farm
  item_needs: Baby Baku Formula
  rewards: 200 Gold; 1x Aryel's Hair
  imagecaption: Tara feeding formula to the baby Baku
  next: Fairy Poor Sleep Habits

## Description
The baku needs some baby formula in order to put her to sleep.

## Dialog
### Quest Intro
Tara: Uh oh. You've got a look... I'm guessing this isn't just a friendly visit?

Kim: It's a look of incredulity.

Kim: I was at the onsen and saw something I can't believe is real. Could you be another pair of eyes for me?

Tara: Oh boy. Nothing's on fire, right?

Kim: Nope. But I do think Aryel's involved with this.

---

Kim: So you can see that too, right?

Tara: I sure can.

Aryel: Ah, Tara. Mortal. I see you've found my new friend.

Tara: We have, but what are we looking at?

Kim: It's a baku.

Aryel: You're right. This calf's mother was taken by poachers. How do you know if the Baku?

Kim: I used to have a figurine of one as a kid. My mom always said it'd protect me from nightmares. Was that true?

Aryel: Unfortunately so. This poor creature traveled a long way to find safe haven.

Tara: That's horrible!

Kim: Awww. Hey there. What's your name?

Aryel: You seem to have an affinity for the creature. Why don't you name her?

Kim: Really? Hmmm...

Kim: What about 'Zoey'?

Zoey: (trumpets)

Tara: It's perfect!

Aryel: I am no mother baku, but we must make her strong before she can feed off the nightmares of those around her.

Tara: Is there anything I can do?

Aryel: I was going to ask. Here is a recipe for some baby baku formula.

Tara: Baby baku food, coming right up!

Zoey: (trumpets)

### Mid-Quest
Kim: How's everything going with Zoey?I've been thinking about her... she must be really hungry.

Tara: She's in good hands with Aryel, and I'm working on whipping that formula up right now.

### Quest Completion
Tara: I've got the formula, Aryel.

Aryel: Wonderful. I wish I could feed her my nightmares but alas, I have always slept like a baby.

Aryel: Plaese, come.

Aryel: Well, go on, give it to her!

Tara: Wow! you were really hungry!

Aryel: Soon she will be strong enough to feed off nightmares alone. She is truely a special being.

Aryel: I never imagined trusting a human with things so precious.

Tara: That means a lot. Thanks you for trusting me, Aryel.

Aryel: As the baku grows, she'll begin to shed her juvenile teeth.

Aryel: They make powerful additions to spells and potions. I'll sell them to you when they come in.
