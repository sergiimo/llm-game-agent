# Violet's Violets

| <b>Violet's Violets</b> |
| <b>Mon | Closed |
| <b>Tue-Sat | 9am-5pm |
| <b>Sun | Closed |
"Bouquets and seeds for your every need."; 
Violet's Violets is a flower shop run by Violet Miranda, where she sells flower seeds. Violet also buys honey harvested from bee hives, flower crops grown in garden beds, and bouquets crafted at the Bouquet Table.

The shop is renamed "Violet's Boutique" when Violet begins selling designs for outfits that can be crafted with the Sewing Machine.
