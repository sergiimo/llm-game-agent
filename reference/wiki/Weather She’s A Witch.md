# Weather She’s A Witch

Weather She’s A Witch is a quest that begins the morning after starting the quest Not To Spring One On You. It requires the player to accompany Giva to a mandatory Town Hall meeting where Parker accuses Giva of witchcraft. The quest acts as a 7-day countdown timer to Giva's trial. 

Lina confronts Parker outside Tara's farmhouse the morning of the trial and banishes the wraith, and Parker withdraws his accusation before the trial begins. When Mayor Otto asks for comments, Cameron says he's spotted Otto going into the woods at night, and Bruno agrees. Otto ultimately admits to meeting a masked woman in the woods for the past ten years.
