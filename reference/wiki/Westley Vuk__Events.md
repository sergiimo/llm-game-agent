## Partner
Trigger: Visit Westley in front of his shop and he will ask you to catch a salmon. When you have it, he will invite you to meet him at the beach the next morning.

| Dialogue |
| npc Westley Vuk Sweetheart, if you have a moment, could you catch me some salmon? I want to make something special. |
Player Well, you never know what’s going to bite, but I’ll try!
-; 
npc Westley Vuk Is that fresh salmon? Perfect! Tomorrow morning, would you take a walk with me down by the beach? I’ll have your surprise ready then.
-; 
Player All right, what’s your scheme, honey?
npc Westley Vuk I made you roast salmon the way my nana taught me! She used to tell me stories while she cooked. Do you want to hear one?
Player Of course I do!
npc Westley Vuk Once upon a time, a man found a falcon’s feather and gave it to his youngest daughter, Alenushka. That night, an enormous falcon landed on Alenushka’s window. But when it saw her, the falcon turned into a man – Finist. Finist and Alenushka fell in love, but Alenushka’s jealous sisters put knives on her window. When Finist landed, he cried out and flew all the way back to the magical land where he lived. Alenushka went on a long journey to Finist’s land, where she finally found him and told him she hadn’t been the one to hurt him.
Player And then what? Did they live in Alenushka’s country or Finist’s?
npc Westley Vuk None of the stories say. But I still like it. Nobody judges the fair maiden for loving someone who turns into an animal. And I want us to be able to make a life together, even if it’s hard, because I love you. This is that collection of Russian fairy tales I told you about. I want you to have it. [Gives Book of Russian Fairy Tales]
Player Oh, Westley, thank you. I love you, too. And for the record, I prefer werewolves to werefalcons.
