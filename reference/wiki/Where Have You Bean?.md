# Where Have You Bean?

[Quest]
  end: Thomas Lightfoot
  item_needs: 8x Green Beans
  rewards: 150 Coin
  title: Where Have You Bean?
  type: Town Quest
  start: Gramma Hazel
  location: Farm
  previous: Rice and Shine

Where Have You Bean? is a quest given by Gramma. It requires you to first follow her to Town Hall for a meeting, then to grow 8x Green Bean for Thomas. The reward for completion is 150 coins, received from Thomas.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Gramma: Oh good, you’re up! The Mayor’s called a town meeting this morning. We should get down there and see what he’s making a fuss about this time.
* Tara: Do you think it’s some kind of trouble?
* Gramma: Oh, I really hope not. But I guess we’ll see.
* Tara: Lead the way, Gramma!
* Gramma: Follow me!

Part 1: Follow Gramma to Town Hall for a meeting.

* Otto: Settle down, everyone, settle down. I’ve brought you together to discuss a very important matter. There have been very strange goings-on in our beloved town of Fairhaven. There is a force at work in our community and I don’t know who is responsible. But whoever they are, they don’t have Fairhaven’s best interests at heart.
* Gramma: Oh, I don’t like where this is going.
* Tara: Me neither…
* Parker: I’ve seen strange lights in the forest at night.
* Thomas: Yeah! And folks in robes and masks wandering around after sunset.
* Otto: Yes! Yes, good. You know exactly what I’m talking about.
* Amira: Wait, I’m sure there’s a rational explanation for all this!
* Otto: No matter the cause, we must ensure our town doesn’t become a home for roustabouts and unsavory types. I ask all of you to keep a sharp eye out for signs of… mischief. And if you see anything, report it to me.
* Vanessa: Or me, if you’d prefer. I’d hate for anyone in Fairhaven to feel fearful.
* Gramma: Well! I never!
* [Outside town hall, after the meeting.]
* Gramma: Mayor Otto: Soft of business? Soft in the head is more like it. Him and his fancy-britches wife, standing up there and casting judgment on others!
* Tara: Uh oh, you’re even madder than I thought…
* Gramma: He can’t just tell people to spy on their neighbors – To be so afraid that they distrust everyone around them!
* Tara: Maybe we could cast a goodwill spell to make everyone be nice to each other?
* Gramma: I wish it were that simple. But just because you can solve a problem with magic doesn’t mean you should. Thomas could use some help on his farm. I know he’s got an order of beans due and he doesn’t have enough plots goin’ to make it in time. He’s too proud to ask for help, though. Here, take these seeds. [Gives 2 Green Bean Seeds.] They should be enough to make the order.
* Tara: Are these… magic beans?
* Gramma: Haha heavens, no! But if you’ve got the means to give ‘em a boost, it certainly wouldn’t hurt. 

Part 2: Gramma wants you to grow Green Beans to help Thomas with an order.

* Tara: Hi Thomas! I’ve got some beans for you. Gramma said you needed them to make an order.
* Thomas: Oh, she did? Hmmph. Well, thank you. That will be a help. Stay there a moment. I’ve got somethin’ for you.
* [Thomas goes inside.]
* Tara: Um, hello? Who are you?
* Zephyr: Whatzit? You can see me?
* Tara: Well yeah, you’re standing right there.
* Zephyr: Oh, it’s just – most humans don’t take much notice of me. My family and Thomas’s family hot a long history. I like to help out where I can.
* Tara: I do too, I guess!
* Zephyr: Well, you must be a good witch, then.
* Tara: Wait, how did you know I was…
* [Zephyr teleports away, Thomas comes outside.]
* Tara: Where did she go?
* Thomas: Where did who go?
* Tara: Um. The cat. You know, the stray?
* Thomas: Right. Well, here’s something for helping me out. It’s not much, but it’s what I have.
* Tara: Thanks, Thomas.
* [Reward: 150 coins]
