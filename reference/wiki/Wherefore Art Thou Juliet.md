# Wherefore Art Thou Juliet

Wherefore Art Thou Juliet is a quest given by Sophia and Kim the day after completing the quest Not To Spring One On You. It requires the player to use 1x Scrying Potion with their Scrying Bowl to locate Juliet, who was discovered missing earlier that morning. The scrying spell leads the player to the bramble gate, where Lina, Amira and Damon are discussing an unknown enchantment that was cast at the Circle. Upon investigating, they discover that the enchantment has Juliet spellbound in a floating bubble.

To break the enchantment, Lina asks the player to gather 1x Pinecone, 1x Toadstool, and 1x Mandrake. After the items are delivered, she safely breaks the enchantment and Juliet is released from the bubble. Juliet explains that Vanessa blackmailed her into meeting her in the woods at night and used her to break the glamour over the Circle, which was possible because her father was the one who wrote the spell. 

After Sophia arrives and takes Juliet home, Zephyr, Aryel, Peri, and Westley teleport in. Westley admits that he had been giving Vanessa information about the coven in exchange for a werewolf suppression potion, then uses his nose to track where Vanessa had gone after breaking the glamour - the third pillar of the standing stones, which are covered in ice. 

To begin to make up for his betrayal, Westley provides the coven with the recipe for an incantation that can thaw the ice as well as some of the ingredients (1x Parchment and 1x Chicken Feather). After crafting 1x Thaw Incantation, the player unseals the standing stones and the coven prepares to pursue Vanessa into the Gloaming.

The reward for completion is the Apple Pie recipe.
