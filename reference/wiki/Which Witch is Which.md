# Which Witch is Which

Which Witch is Which is a quest given by the Adept to unlock her identity. It requires you to brew 1x Honey Mint Cooler in your distillery and deliver it to her during the day. 

It is recommended to brew two Honey Mint Coolers by using a Boost Harvest Potion, as it is also one of her favorite foods and can be given to her immediately before or after finishing the quest. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Adept: The Farseer tells me that you’ve done well. Let’s see if your observation and intuition is as strong with me.
* Tara: What will it be then? Do you want a potion that will turn the mayor into a frog?
* Adept: Haha, nothing that advanced yet.
* Tara: See, it’s the “yet” part that concerns me.
* Adept: I want a bottle of my favorite Summer drink: Honey Mint Cooler! It’s so refreshing on a hot day! You have a beehive, yes? Back home, we make a simple syrup with honey and mint, but brewing it in your distillery gives it an extra kick! If you are successful in completing my task, perhaps more witches will be willing to reveal themselves.
* (Receive Honey mint cooler recipe)

Quest completion:

* Tara: Um… here you go… Dr. Syed.
* Amira: What is this?
* Tara: Oh! Er…
* Amira: Haha, I’m just fooling with you, Tara-joon. Well done! I suppose the honey and mint might have been a bit of a giveaway. The others might present a bigger challenge! Talk to the coven members and see if any are willing to take this leap of faith.
