# While the Cat's Away

While the Cat's Away is a series of quests given by the High Priestess and the Acolyte to gather information on Cameron's goals. It requires you to learn the Animal Shapeshifting Spell from Aryel and travel to the Consciousness Center as a cat. 

During this quest, time will not pass while you are in cat form. 

The quest objective in Part 3 says to follow the Cat-colyte to the Consciousness Center, but the next cutscene will trigger as normal if you have a speed boost active and run ahead of her.
