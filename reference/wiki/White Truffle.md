# White Truffle

[Crafting Ingredient]
  itemName: White Truffle
  source: Magical Pigs
  sellTo: Kai's Treasures
  sellPrice: 70 coins
  description: An extremely valuable and rare fungus only found by magical pigs. Used in recipes or sell to Kai.

White truffles are found by adult magical pigs (non-magical pigs only find regular truffles). White truffles can be picked up by petting the pigs.
