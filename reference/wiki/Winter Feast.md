# Winter Feast

Winter Feast is a quest given by Kim Izumi at the beginning of Winter that serves as a 10-day countdown timer to the Winter Feast. 

You can receive four free food items during the feast: 1x Cabbage Soup from Amira, 1x Beef & Shiitake from Kim, 1x Sweet Potato Casserole from Angus, and 1x Hot Chocolate from Damon.

Some of the dialogue during the feast changes depending on if the quests A Fab-Yule-Us Feast and Salmon Has to Do It were completed in time.
