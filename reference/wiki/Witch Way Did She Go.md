# Witch Way Did She Go

Witch Way Did She Go is a quest that begins immediately after completing the quest Wherefore Art Thou Juliet. It requires the player to go through the third pillar to the Gloaming to pursue Vanessa. 

Tara, Violet, and Lina work together to break the previously impassable barrier in the third area of the Gloaming. Past it, they find Vanessa (dressed as Raven) using magic on a large tree, believing it to be the heart of the Wellspring. Lina warns her to stop, but Vanessa accuses her of lying and continues until the tree unravels to reveal an unconscious woman - Gloria. Moments later, a wraith escapes the hole in the tree and possesses Vanessa, who taunts them and leaves.

Lina instructs Tara to give Gloria a Reviving Potion, then leaves to gather the coven to follow Vanessa. Once Tara returns with the potion, Violet gives it to her mother and despairs when it seemingly has no effect, but Gloria wakes up moments later and reunites with her daughter. Violet stays behind to help her mother while Tara goes on ahead to help the others in town. 

The reward for completion is 3x Max Restore Magic Potion.
