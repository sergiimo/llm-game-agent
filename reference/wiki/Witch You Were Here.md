# Witch You Were Here

Witch You Were Here is a series of quests given by the High Priestess in the Winter. It requires the player to visit Westley's store in Ravenwood Hollow to find a Summon Witch incantation, travel past the second pillar in the Gloaming to find a giant lotus, and craft the incantation. 

The Fairhaven Ferry will be broken down the morning after starting this quest.

* Farseer Shelby: There’s help out there if we’re ready to seek it.
