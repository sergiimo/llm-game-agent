# Wood-n't You Know It?

[Quest]
  title: Wood-n't You Know It?
  type: Foraging Quest
  start: Hazel Wylde
  end: Hazel Wylde
  item_needs: 6x Wood
  location: Wylde Farm
  previous: The Fungus Among Us
  rewards: 1x Cream of Mushroom Soup
  next: Rice and Shine

Wood-n't You Know It? is a quest given by Gramma. It requires you chop 2x logs and gather 6x wood. As a reward, she gives you 1x Cream of Mushroom Soup.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Gramma: Lina brought us some extra milk while you were out, so I made us a delicious mushroom soup sure to warm our bellies. 
* Tara: And you're sure it had the right number of… gills… or whatever? We're not going to start seeing things or frothing at the mouth? 
* Gramma: Oh no, if I gave you something that made you see things, it'd be on purpose. 
* Tara: Gramma! 
* Gramma: Just kidding, honeybee! Now, I have another chore for you that would be a great help, if you don’t mind?
* Tara: Of course, Gramma, what is it?
* Gramma: Some wood for the fire would be nice. Nights are still cold in the Spring around here. Could you go and find a couple of the big old logs lying around the farm, chop them up for me and bring me the wood?
* Tara: Sure thing, Gramma! 
* Gramma: You’ll need this to get started! [Gives Rusty Wood Axe]

Quest completion:

* Gramma: Oh, that wood looks nice and dry and ready to burn! Great! Thank you for doing that for me! 
* Tara: It was no trouble! Phew, it’s hard work, though!
* Gramma: Look at you, you’re plum tuckered out! What do they teach young girls in the city these days anyway? If you work too hard and don’t keep an eye on your energy, you’re likely to pass out! But there’s an easy solution! Good, old-fashioned, country cooking! Here’s another serving of the soup I made earlier. It’ll fix you right up!
