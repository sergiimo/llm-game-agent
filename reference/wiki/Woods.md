# Woods

The Woods is a wooded area to the far east of Fairhaven where many resources can be foraged, including respawning trees for collecting wood. There are opportunities for fishing, and its upper left area serves as the meeting place for the Fairhaven Coven!

First time interacting with the Silver Willow Tree; 
Tara: Hmmm, there's a plaque that reads: This beloved tree be olde as thee towne itselfe

First time interacting with the Bramble Gate; 
Tara: There's whispering...what is that? Something spooky and weird is going on here.

## Connections
* Town Center (lower left; exits next to Nailed It!)
* The Gloaming (upper left, once unlocked)

## Residents
