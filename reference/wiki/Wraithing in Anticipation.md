# Wraithing in Anticipation

Wraithing in Anticipation is a series of quests that begins during the questline of Glam-more, as you exit the Gloaming after planting Anemone Seeds.  

The wraith will only reveal itself after the Fall election is over. You must complete this quest before the Sophia's trial to banish the wraith from Natalia. 

You need the materials for Natalia's Wraith Banishment.

* Tara: I have the incantation ready. Can we rid Natalia of the wraith now?

Now that the incantation is prepared, there’s nothing to do but wait until Sophia’s witch trial to use it.
