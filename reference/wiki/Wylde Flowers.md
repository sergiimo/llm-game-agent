Wylde Flowers is a cozy farm sim developed and published by Studio Drydock, a game studio based in Australia.
Players take on the role of Tara Wylde, who travels to Fairhaven to help her grandmother. Very soon Tara discovers she has a gift and that she is an actual witch. During her adventure, she will learn new skills and make many new friends.
