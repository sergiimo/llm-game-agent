# You’re a Catch

You’re a Catch is a romance quest given by Kim Izumi during the epilogue if they are dating Tara. They invite the player to meet them by the lake in the woods to talk. 

They can be found at the northernmost area of the lake, near Shelby's cabin. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Kim: I had tonight penciled in as solo fishing time, but why don’t we hang out instead? Just the two of us?
* Tara: Oh, no. Is this a “we should talk” moment?
* Kim: Yeah, but not in a bad way. We should talk because I love talking to you. And we’ve barely gotten time to decompress. See you at the lake after seven?
* Tara: It’s a date!

Kim would like to meet you at the lake in the woods. Head there after 7pm.
