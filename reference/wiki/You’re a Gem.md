# You’re a Gem

You’re a Gem is a quest given by the High Priestess in the Winter. It requires the player to go through the second pillar to the Gloaming to collect an Adder Stone so the coven can turn the season. The reward for completion is 10x Levitation Potion.

If you get stuck trying to upgrade your wand: there will not be a todo list item or an exclamation point conversation with Zephyr, go to the coin conversation with Zephyr to buy the new wand.
