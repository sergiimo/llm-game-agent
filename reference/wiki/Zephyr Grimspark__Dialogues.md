# Zephyr Grimspark/Dialogues

CharacterNav
This page lists the dialogue lines for Zephyr Grimspark.

| Summer |
| Gosh, I just love meeting new witches who grew up outside Fairhaven! You must have so many stories about the city! |
Tara: People keep thinking that. But my life has been a thousand times more exciting since moving here!
| Tara: So is there a whole colony of goblins living in the mines? |
Zephyr: Nah, we never lived in the mines. We need fresh air just like you folks! By my ancestors did work the deep caves for generations 'n generations.
| Tara: Which of the fae was the first to live in Ravenwood Hollow? |
Zephyr: My great-great-great-grandparents were fiddlin' with whirligig bits when Peri and Aryel came along. I hope my nephews'll move back to continue the Grimspark tradition!
| Tara: You mentioned you have nephews? |
Zephyr: Sure do! My sister and her family live in the city. Moved there years ago. 

Tara: I didn't know goblins lived in the city! 

Zephyr: Oh my, yes. Goblins love a good abandoned subway station. Nice 'n dank.
| Tara: I keep expecting more goblins to teleport into the mines while I'm there. But I've only seen you do it. |
Zephyr: Well… that's because I'm the only goblin left in Ravenwood Hollow. 

Tara: Oh…
| Tara: Sorry if I made you uncomfotable before. If I'd known you were the last goblin… |
Zephyr: Ah, no need to get your gears out of joint. You didn't know. Ravenwood Hollow's nice 'n all, but it's more isolated than goblins like to be. We're a social people. Over time, plenty of folks left for goblin outposts elsewhere. Not me, though!
| Tara: You look tired today. |
Zephyr: I am! I stayed up late because I thought I had a real breakthrough on my nimbus cloud condenser. But I ended up with a mechanical breakdown, not a breakthrough.
| Sometimes, I zip over to the ferry and do an invisible ride-along. |
Tara: Wait, really? Why? 

Zephyr: I get all excited seeing that engine at work!
| Adamantite, orichalcum, bolonium, alicorn… |
Tara: Excuse me? 

Zephyr: Just mutterin' under my breath! Those are some of the best conductors for currents of magic.
| Tara: This workshop feels really, well, worked-in. |
Zephyr: That's because it is! My ma, Anbaria, ran the place for years before she retired 'n moved to Atlantis.  

Tara: She retired to a famously magical island?! 

Zephyr: Heh. Don't you live on a magical island? 

Tara: Touche.

| Fall |
| I heard that during Fall, humans like to jump into piles of leaves. Why would ya do that? Leaves are covered with bugs! Jump in a pile of lithium metal instead! It's real soft! |
| Fall is harvest time, right? I wonder how Thomas is getting' on. It takes a lot of machinery in good condition to pull off a harvest! I better have a look-see in his shed while he's sleeping… |
| So that baker fella's getting' married, is he? Good for him! He better not go on any post-wedding trips. Then I won't be able to pop in behind his bakery and smell the cakes in the oven! |
Tara: Zephyr… that's creepy. 

Zephyr: Ah, I know! But I can't help it!
| How's the mine treatin' ya? Found any goblin machinery yet? |
Tara: I wasn't sure where it came from. But it looks like it came out of this very workshop! 

Zephyr: Aww, that's sweet. Maybe one day I'll be as talented as the ones who made the mining machines!
| You know goblin cuisine doesn't use sugar? Ma said it was so human to make food sweeter. And my sister'd make fun of my sugar cravings. But I still have a sweet tooth! If I lived in Fairhaven, I'd go to that precious blonde fella's bakery every day! |
| I don't understand why humans have to have elections. |
Tara: How do goblins pick a leader? 

Zephyr: We all take turns serving on the goblin council. Ya gotta do your part! But now that I'm the only goblin in Ravenwood Hollow, I'm happy to let Peri keep that gear turning. 
| So ya visited the Gloaming, huh? Wish I coulda gone with you! Unfortunately, the deeper I go into the Gloaming, the more I break out in hives. The magic reacts with the electricity in goblin skin! It's neat… in theory, not in practice. |
| Westley asked me to see if I could measure the light from the moon and make him clothes that blocked only those light frequencies. He thought maybe that would keep him from turning at night. But it didn't work. At least, not yet. I gotta keep trying! |
| Aryel acts like he always prefers creatures over humans, or even fae. But sometimes, when he's not doing anything, he comes into my workshop and tells me stories about the river where he grew up. He's real sweet, honest! You just gotta get past… everything else. |
| Isn't that whirligig a real time-saver? And you won't get a better design than the Grimspark one! We've perfected it over generations! Not that you got other goblins around to buy from, hehe. |
| I wish my ancestors woulda built Ravenwood Hollow close enough to Fairhaven to share its weather. Then it would be easier for me to test out my weather systems! |
| Hazel used to bring me apple cider every Fall. I tried using it as fuel once, just to see what would happen. That's how I ended up cleanin' cider off my ceiling! |
| I've seen that human scientist - Giva, right? - diggin' around in the mines. She's got grit, that one! She'd fit right into goblin society. |
| Aryel has the nicest hair! I wish he'd sit still 'n let me brush 'n braid it for him. But 'Aryel' and 'sittin' still'… well, think of the two like poles on a magnet. |
Tara: Huh? 

Zephyr: What I mean is, they just don't go together!
| I just feel so bad for Sophia. Her daughter, too. To feed your neighbors for years and years, and then have them turn on you… I wish Giacomo were still here so he could protect his girls. |
| Thank goodness Sophia had you and the rest of the coven to help her out. I don't want to think about what might have happened if… you know. Tara: I have to ask: why do you care so much about someone you've never even talked to? Zephyr: Because she's innocent! She didn't do a thing. I can't bear that. |
| I've never seen wraiths myself, but Peri says they're real nasty business. As a girl, I heard plenty of stories, too. Wraiths used to be more common around here. What's making them come back? |
| Shelby and Lina seem real worried about turning the seasons. But I believe in you! You're spunky enough to turn the seasons all by yourself. Tara: Let's hope it never comes to that. |
| Westley gave me some chocolate he got from that merchant in Fairhaven! Since he's half-human 'n all, I reckon he sympathizes with my human taste for sweets. |

| Winter |
| So is your town startin' preparations for the Winter Feast? It's my favorite! |
Tara: But, Zephyr, you're not allowed to go to the Winter Feast, are you? 

Zephyr: Oh, no one's ever seen my pop by! I just can't resist festive baked treats!
| It's even more important to feed your livestock every day when it's cold out, help 'em keep their body heat up. Let me know if ya need to charge your whirligig for 'em! |
| Why do humans "deck the halls" with holly in the wintertime? Don't they know that's to keep goblins away? Hurts my feelings, to tell ya the truth. |
| I really miss my family this time of year. |
Tara: Do you ever visit them?  

Zephyr: Sometimes. I like seeing new places. But home is home.
| I wonder who would want to break into your coven circle like that? I tell ya, if someone trespassed here, Peri would be real sore about it. That's why she got her feathers ruffled when you just showed up last Summer. |
| What ya workin on, Zephyr? Zephyr: I'm tryin to make a whirligig that will shovel snow! But, it… kinda explodes the snow instead. |
| I wish I could figure out what happened to the ferry. But it only feels safe to look around there when the humans are asleep. |
| I like Winter usually. My workshop keeps me plenty warm 'n cozy. But I think this year, Spring can't come soon enough. |
| Someday I'll get my weather machine working, then the coven won't be all on your own for sortin' out the seasons! |
| I always thought Peri was being paranoid about what might happen if the Fairhaven folks found out about us. But if this is the way your people treat human outsiders, I don't want to think about how they would treat fae. |
| Ya can just feel somethin' is off with the Wellspring. I've never seen it like this in all my life. |
| Peri decided she didn't want some of her old clothes anymore, so she gave 'em to me. Just try imaginin' me in Peri's pants! I was swimmin' in 'em! Like a kid plain' dress-up in her ma's jumpsuits. |
| Seems like a good day for hot cocoa, hmm? |
Tara: But it doesn't even get cold here!  

Zephyr: Who said anything about the cold? Every day's a good day for hot cocoa!
| When the days get short, that's when humans should be real grateful that goblins figured out electrical lightin' all those years ago. But never a word of thanks! |
Tara: Well, I'm thankful. 

Zephyr: I appreciate it.
| So the rancher was the one who wanted to buy out Thomas's land all along? I don't side with Aryel much in life, but I might this time! |
| Heard you've got a new witch in the coven! Must be such a strange thing to just find out you're magical one day. We goblins have plenty of time to get used to that idea. |
| Balancin' broomsticks, building whirligigs, these things I can do. But capturing wraiths I must leave to you. |
| Sometimes I can't believe goblins used to live out near Fairhaven. All that snow in the Winter-! Seems like a real pain in the keister. |
| I hear that in Winter, humans sometimes go whizzin' around on skates they attach to their feet. I'd love to try it myself! But I'd probably fall on my nose, and then what would I do? I got a perfect nose! Ma always said so. |

| Epilogue |
| It makes me so happy to see the Miranda family back together. Makes me a little wistful, I'll admit. |
| Can't believe Hazel's been gone a year now. She'd be real proud of ya, Tara. |
| Oh, I can't wait to balance brooms for Sebastián and Juliet! They'll be so cute whizzing around! |
| I know it wasn't easy for Westley to dump all that potion into the Wellspring. But it was right for him to make amends. |
| Tara: So have you been by Thomas's place lately, Zephyr? |
Zephyr: Yeah. He gets startled when I just pop in, so I'm workin on poppin' in a little further away and then walking up so he can see me comin'. But then sometimes I just startle Marty instead. Can't really win!
