# Zephyr Grimspark/Events

# The first time you meet Zephyr will be year one when you make a delivery to Thomas' farm. She will be working on the generator next to the front door.
# The Second time you will meet Zephyr is in the the mines. Tara opens the door of the mine and spot Zephyr working. She says hello, apologizes and teleports away!
# Zephyr's workshop is located in Raven's Wood. After a sufficient amount of time/friendship she will ask you to find her Versorium  from the Mine.  I found it on Level 16 of the Mine
# Once you've returned Zephyr's Vesorium, Zephyr will come find you on your farm, and tell you about whirlygigs. You will need 15 Iron Bars, 15 Copper Bars, and 10 Silver Bars. Meet her at her workshop in Ravenswood!
