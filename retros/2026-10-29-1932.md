## Suggestions (payoff / effort)
1. **Nav events always logged** to `tools/nav/events.jsonl` (not only during a run), every skip with a reason.
   Debugging a silent skip: ~6 calls → 1. *Low effort.*
2. **Graph first, map last; no retry of a failed map.** In `goto`, "map did not open" → take a reading and try the
   graph; in the skill, one map failure = stop using the map for this trip. ~4 wasted calls → 0. *Low effort.*
3. **Offline before live:** `grep -n "^NAME ="` before adding a constant; every log-parsing change gets a unit test
   with a real log line from `./wf log`. Then 2 of the 7 live cycles would have been 0. *Low effort.*
4. **Quality on recorded points:** a scene/exit fact recorded without calibration is marked `rough`; a later precise
   one replaces it, and the graph never pushes through a rough exit blindly. *Medium.*
5. **Scope rule:** do exactly the ask first (go home), report, then offer the extras. Max one live debug cycle per
   bug, then pause and report. *Behavioural.*
6. **Clock guard:** after 23:00 game time, stop optional work and ask about bed. *Low.*
