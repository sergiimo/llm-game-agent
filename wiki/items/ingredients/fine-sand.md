# Fine Sand

[Crafting Ingredient]
  itemName: Fine Sand
  source: Mine
  description: Fine, pure sand can be used as a crafting ingredient to make glass. Beach sand won't do, so you must seek out sandstone in the mine. Sandstone is an orange stone that will appear starting from Level  1 of the mine.
