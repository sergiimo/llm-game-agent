# Spile

[Crafting Ingredient]
  itemName: Spile
  description: You can craft a spile at your metalworking table, and then use it to tap a tree for sap. Can be used on maple trees to produce maple syrup.
  source: Metalworking Table
  recipe: 1x Gold Ingot; 1x Copper Ingot
