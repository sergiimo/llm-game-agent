# Animals

> **Summary:** Pet cat Cleocatra; coop (chickens/ducks) and barn (cows, sheep, alpacas, pigs) unlocked by building quests.

## Rules (wiki)
- Pet the cat daily for free chub/crickets. Later: Animal Shapeshifting → fish as the cat for free.
- Coop: *All Cooped Up*; buy chicks from Marty (Hayseed Homestead; closed Thu–Sat). Pet daily; **feathers** needed for later spells. Keep 7+ days of feed.
- Barn: *A Barn in Stable Condition* (120 Wood + 60 Iron Ingot). Kim buys adult animals; lard only from cows.
