# Money And Shops

> **Summary:** Coins from selling; shops level up when you sell to them; fixed hours and closed days.

## Rules (wiki)
- Start with 50 coins (verified). Buy/sell ×1, ×10 or all.
- **Shop levels:** selling fills a progress bar; stars top-right of the shop window = remaining levels → more stock. Open Lina's store early (spring seeds); sell paper to Kai.
- Hours: [../world/businesses.md](../world/businesses.md). Inventory is unlimited, so hoard respawning forage.
- Money ideas: town bulletin board quests; crops; fish; early "fish fingers" trick (Bruno tuna + Lina flour → cook → sell to Sophia).

## How to see
Coin badge under the inventory button, top-right ([../ui/hud.md](../ui/hud.md)).
