# Seasons And Weather

> **Summary:** Seasons change only via coven rituals; out-of-season crops vanish at the change.

## Rules (wiki)
- Spring → Summer: Spirit Flame potion. Summer → Fall: Fall Ritual incantation + Spirit Flame. Fall → Winter: Winter Ritual. You choose when.
- **Harvest before changing season**: out-of-season crops disappear; trees stay.
- Rain waters crops. Weather spells exist (Summon Rain is part of the summer drought quest).
- Per-season crops/forage: `reference/wiki/Spring.md` … `Winter.md`.
