# Pining Away

Pining Away is a quest given by the High Priestess in the Winter after completing the quest The Unjolly Rancher. It requires the player to deliver 10x Pinecone to her so the coven can make an offering to the Wellspring. The reward for completion is 2x Moly Seeds.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: Peri mentioned that there might be some way to protect the islanders from the wraiths?
* Adept Amira: We will need more of the fennel seed powder to capture the wraiths when they escape from The Gloaming. You can help us maintain a steady supply.
* High Priestess Lina: And the best way to protect the islanders is to keep the Wellspring in balance. Winter does make it difficult to grow and gather offerings. But there is still abundance, if you know where to look. Go and gather some pine cones. We will make an offering for our observance of the shortest day of Winter. 

Quest completion:

* Tara: Here are the pine cones, High Priestess.
* High Priestess Lina: Well done. We will fashion them into a wreath.

* High Priestess Lina: With this offering, we welcome the rebirth of the sun.
* Novice Tara: Whoa. Cool! How long will the offering boost the Wellspring?
* High Priestess Lina: If we are prudent with our use of magic, it may last us some time. But eventually, we must turn the season so that we can harvest Spring crops once more.
* [Reward: 2x Moly seeds]
