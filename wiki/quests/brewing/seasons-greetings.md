# Season's Greetings

Season's Greetings is a quest given by the High Priestess during the Fall after finishing the quest Know Witch Way the Wind Blows. It requires you to craft 1x Winter Ritual Incantation and 1x Spirit Flame. The reward for completion is 5x Revitalizing Potion.
