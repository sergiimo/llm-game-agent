# All Cooped Up

[Quest]
  title: All Cooped Up
  type: To-Do Quests
  previous: Give a Ranch Hand

All Cooped Up is a quest received after completing the Give a Ranch Hand questline. It requires you to deliver 1000 coins and 100x Wood to Parker Johnson so he can build you a coop. You then buy a chick from Marty Emerson for 250 coins. The reward for completion is the Barn blueprint, the Butter Churn blueprint, and 10x Chicken Feed.

* Tara: Are you trying to get me to change my mind?
* Marty: No, sorry, just running my mouth!
