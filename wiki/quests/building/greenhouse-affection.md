# Greenhouse Affection

Greenhouse Affection is a quest given by Thomas Lightfoot in the Winter. It requires the player to deliver 50x Wood, 20x Iron Ingot, 18x Glass, and 5000 coins to Parker Johnson so he can build you a greenhouse. After building the greenhouse, Parker will request that you grow 4x Marigold for him. The reward for completion is 100 coins.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: Mornin’, Thomas.
* Thomas: Mornin. Got somethin’ for ya. [Gives 5x Sunflowers.] Happy Holidays. Hmmph.
* Tara: Aww, Thomas, this is so nice of you! Wait, Sunflowers? Where did you get these this time of year?
* Thomas: A friend built a greenhouse and sent me this. Parker could build you one too. You just need a glassworks in your tool shed first. Lina can sell ya any off-season seeds ya need. She gets some from time to time. Off-season crops fetch a higher price, too.
* Tara: That’s a great idea! I’ll get started on it!

Gather wood, iron ingots, and glass so Parker can build a greenhouse.

* Parker: I’ll get this greenhouse up in no time. Should only take a day or so!

Wait for Parker to finish construction on the greenhouse.

* Tara: Parker, it looks amazing! Thank you so much!
* Parker: My pleasure! Hey, think you might plant some marigolds? We sure could use a burst of sunshine around here! Here’s some seeds I found leftover from last Summer!

Parker has asked you to grow marigolds in your new greenhouse. 

* Tara: Got somethin’ for ya, Parker.
* Parker: Oh wow, thanks! These will really brighten up my living room. Maybe brighten up the mood in the house, too.
* Tara: Oh, is something wrong?
* Parker: Oh, ya know. Tis the season for winter doldrums! It will pass.
* [Reward: 100 coins]
