# Water Under the Bridge

Water Under the Bridge is a quest received by Parker Johnson one morning in the Summer. It requires you to deliver 200 coins, 50x Wood, and 15x Iron Ingot to him so he can repair the bridge to the mountain pass.

* Danger!
* The bridge has been declared unfit for use. The path to the mountain is off-limits.

* Parker: Well, yes, actually! Natalia told me all about the great job you did on the mine… and you know that broken bridge over yonder? 
* Tara: Uh-huh. What’ll it be? 
* Parker: Well, some more wood, and some iron from your mine would shore up the supports! The Mayor’s excited because a scientist with a big research grant wants to move into the old lookout post. But I just want to take walks up the mountain pass again and visit my favorite flowering dogwood trees! 
* Tara: Okay, I’ll help! 
* Parker: Thanks! You can bring the supplies by my shop when you’re ready.
