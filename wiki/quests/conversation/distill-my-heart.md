# Distill My Heart

Distill My Heart is a quest given by Damon Mthembu-Haas in the Winter. It requires the player to brew 3x Sweet Potato Vodka so he can make his signature Bloody Marys. The reward for completion is the Sweet Potato Vodka recipe.

The dialogue differs slightly depending on whether the player chooses to use Instant Brew Powder or not. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Damon: Hey, Tara! I had an idea. It’s been so long since I made my signature Bloody Marys. But what if we got sweet potato vodka and started serving them up at my bar? The fancy brunch places in Milkwater keep buying all the sweet potato, apparently… Now my luck’s turned, because Lina has sweet potato seeds for sale. If you could grow some and distill it into vodka, we’d have Bloody Marys in a few days.
* Tara: Distilling takes time, Damon!
* Damon: Weeelllll… not always. Try this. [Gives 1x Instant Brew Powder]
* Tara: Sure thing! Uh, what is it, exactly?
* Damon: This is Instant-Brew powder. Put it in your distillery and your ingredients will ferment in no time. It works in your cauldron, too! Here’s how to grind it up if you want to make more batches. You’ll need some of the glowing fish from the pond through the second Gloaming pillar in order to make the Essence of Joy. 

Quest completion:

* Damon: How’s that Sweet Potato Vodka coming, Tara?
* Tara: Here it is. Up to your standards?
* Damon: It sure is. You’re a master distiller! Did you use some of the Instant Brew powder?
* (If the powder was used) Tara: Yes. I didn't have to wait on the still at all!
* Damon: You know what they say: a watched still never ferments.
* (If the powder wasn't used) Tara: I didn’t need it, actually.
* Damon: Ahh. Very old-fashioned. Instant Brew is always handy to have around. Give it a try sometime!
* [Reward: Sweet potato vodka recipe]
