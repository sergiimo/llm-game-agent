# Lady Who Lunches

Lady Who Lunches is a quest given by Vanessa Soft in the Winter. It requires the player to deliver 4x Quiche to her so she can provide hot lunches for her students while the ferry is broken down. The reward for completion is 200 coins.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Vanessa: Drat. That was the last of it.
* Tara: Last of what? Hope?
* Vanessa: Hah. No, my hot lunch supply. Because we’re such a small school, the Milkwater school district usually sends over hot lunches on the ferry for the children. But no ferry means no hot lunches. I stockpiled some extras for winter storms. I just didn’t plan for this.
* Tara: Lina’s still got plenty of supplies at her store. All the kids should be getting enough to eat.
* Vanessa: Yes, theoretically. But – and I don’t mean to insult any of the adults preparing the children’s lunches – I’m not sure the meals they’ve been bringing are, er, filling enough. Which results in sleepiness and irritability. My students keep getting - what is the word? - hangry.
* Tara: The worst feeling! I might be able to grow some extra vegetables in my garden.
* Vanessa: If you could make them into lunches for the children, that would be magnificent! Here’s a recipe that will keep them filled up. 
* [Receive Quiche recipe]
* Tara: I’m no master chef, but let me see what I can do. 

Quest completion:

* Tara: Here, I just finished a batch of lunches for the kids.
* Vanessa: This is just what we needed. Thank you!
* Tara: Maybe my true calling was to be a lunch lady.
* Vanessa: If you want to keep bringing me hot lunches, I won’t say no. I can get the school board to reimburse me.
* Tara: The ferry may be out of commission, but my garden’s not!
* Vanessa: Wonderful. Life in Fairhaven is so haywire right now. I want the children to have small comforts to look forward to.
* [Reward: 200 coins]
