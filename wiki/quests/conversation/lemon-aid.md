# Lemon-Aid

Lemon-Aid is a quest given by Emmi and Finn Johnson in the Summer. It requires you to deliver 20x Wood and 15x Sugar to them so they can make a lemonade stand. After giving them the items their lemonade stand will be open in the Town Square on weekends, and the reward for completion is 5x lemonade which can be picked up when the stand is open.
