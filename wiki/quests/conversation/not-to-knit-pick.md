# Not to Knit Pick

Not to Knit Pick is a quest given by Shelby Demeter in the Winter. It requires the player to deliver 2x Wool to him so he can knit himself a warm blanket. The reward for completion is 3x Parchment.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Shelby: Was hopin’ I might see ya today. Are ya tending any sheep on that farm? I could use some wool if you have some to spare.
* Tara: Oh! … an offering for the coven cauldron?
* Shelby: No, this winter won’t be letting up any time soon, and I live in a cabin in the woods. I’ve got enough wood to keep my fire goin’, but I thought I might knit myself a warm blanket.
* Tara: Of course. I’ll see what I can do.
* Shelby: Appreciated. 

Quest completion:

* Shelby: Ya got any wool yet?
* Tara: Yes, here it is!
* Shelby: Kind of ya. Here. Westley gave me some new parchment, but my eyes aren’t what they once were. It’s a chore to sit and scribe incantations by lamplight at my age. You’ll have more use of it than I will.
* [Reward: 3 parchment]
