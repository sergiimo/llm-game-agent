# Oh Chai There

Oh Chai There is a quest given by Giva Joshi in the Winter after the ferry has broken down. It requires the player to deliver 3x Black Tea and 1x Cinnamon to her so she can brew Chai tea. The reward for completion is 315 coins, 1x Chai, and the Chai recipe.

Black Tea Seeds can be purchased from Kai's Treasures, when available.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Giva: Tara, you wouldn’t happen to be growing any tea on your farm, would you? Kai’s all out of loose leaf tea and I haven’t had a good chai in ages!
* Tara: I’m not, but I could!
* Giva: That would be amazing! Kai said he still has some tea seeds. Oh, but for a proper chai, you’ll need a few other things too. Here’s my favorite way to make it. 

Quest completion:

* Giva: Have any luck growing the tea?
* Tara: Yes, it’s ready!
* Giva: Fantastic! Would you mind coming with me, then? I promised Cameron I would bring a pot of tea for our meeting.
* Tara: Meeting? I didn’t know you joined the League.
* Giva: Oh, I didn’t! It’s, well, just come. You’ll see.

* Cameron: Tara, how nice that you’re joining us today.
* Giva: She brewed a pot of chai for our meeting.
* Tara: What type of meeting is this?
* Cameron: It’s a weekly sobriety meeting we hold here at the Consciousness Center. It’s open to the community, but so far it’s just been Giva and me.
* Giva: Come on, Tara, sit down, we won’t bite.
* Cameron: Giva, last week we were discussing how Winter can make it more difficult to stay in recovery. All those long hours indoors.
* Giva: Yes. Being outdoors working during the day helps, but nights are hard. I’ve wanted to go into Damon’s bar more times than I can count, just for some extra company. But I’ve held fast. I’m proud of myself.
* Cameron: You should be. Every day without a drink is a victory. We’re not open for regular service just now, Bruno.
* Bruno: Oh, ‘scuse me. Thought I read on the noticeboard there was a meeting today?
* Cameron: Yes. Absolutely. Welcome!
* [Reward: 315 coins, Chai, Chai recipe]
