# When Tulips Kiss

When Tulips Kiss is a quest given by Parker Johnson in the epilogue. It requires the player to deliver 4x Tulip to him so he can give them to Lina for their anniversary. The reward for completion is 80 coins.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Parker: I was hoping to give Lina some tulips for our anniversary, but Violet’s all sold out… I’ve been checking every day to see if she gets some in stock but now I think Violet’s avoiding me! Are you growing any flowers on your farm?
* --[Choice: No, I grow crops] (rejects quest)
** Tara: No, I’ve got to save space for food crops.
** Parker: Oh… right. Sure, I get it.
* --[Choice: Yes, I’m growing some.]
** Tara: Yes, I’ve planted a few flowers.
* Parker: That’s great! Lina’s never really understood why I love flowers so much, but she does like tulips. I think it’s because they’re Dutch.
* Tara: Oh, is Lina Dutch?
* Parker: Um, no, Norwegian. But that’s kinda close, right?
* Tara: Not even remotely.
* Parker: Haha! Geography was never really my strongest subject. Anyway, if you have any tulips to spare, you would really save the day!
* Tara: Okay, I’ll let you know if I’ve got some.
* Parker: Awesome, thanks!

Bring Parker some tulips from your garden. 

* Parker: Any luck with the tulips?
* Tara: Yes! Here they are!
* Parker: Thanks so much! I know it might seem old-fashioned, but I’m a fan of the surprise romantic gesture.
* Tara: I’m sure she’ll love them, Parker.
* Parker: You think so? She’s a hard nut to crack sometimes. But that’s part of why I love her.
* [Reward: 80 coins]
