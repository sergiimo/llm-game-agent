A Quick Spell Check is a quest given by the High Priestess to unlock her identity. It requires you to create 1x Speed Time Spell and deliver it to her during the day. You get to keep the incantation.

* Tara: Why? The coven doesn’t hurt anyone.
* High Priestess: The word “witch” conjures up old stereotypes and superstitions in people’s minds. They might panic when faced with something they don’t understand. That’s why we keep our identities a secret. Our friends and families might not accept us if they knew the truth.
* --[Choice: Give them a chance?]
** -
* --[Choice: I had no idea!]
** Tara: I didn’t know my gramma was a witch until a few months ago!
** High Priestess: Really? You never even suspected that Hazel was a bit… special?
* --[Choice: Makes sense.]
** -
* High Priestess: So. I think you suspect who I am. Write this Speed Up incantation. When you cast it, any crops or cauldrons nearby will grow or brew faster. [Gives Speed Time Spell recipe.] Bring it to me in town when it’s ready.
* Tara: Okay. I’ll make it speedy! Get it?
* High Priestess: Run along, Novice.
