# Something's Brewing

Something's Brewing is a quest given by the High Priestess and the Acolyte. It requires you to brew 3x Miracle Grow Potion. The reward for completion is the Revitalizing Potion recipe.

## Dialogue
Quest intro: 

* High Priestess: Ah, Initiate.
* Tara: I’m Tara, remember?
* High Priestess: Not here and not now. You are a fledgling witch who has yet to master her powers. For that reason, we’ll refer to you by the designation that indicates your skill level. Initiates are the first rank, but I expect anyone from Hazel’s bloodline will quickly progress. To begin, as all witches before you, you must master the art of potion brewing. Speak to the Acolyte witch. She will be able to guide you in your task. 

Part 1: Speak with the Acolyte to begin your magic lessons.

* Tara: The head witch lady said I should come and talk to you about potions…
* Acolyte: Right! We’ll start you with an easy one. That way you can’t accidentally magic yourself into a pile of goo.
* Tara: What?! Is that a thing that could actually happen?
* Acolyte: Theoretically? Yes. Practically? I’ve never seen it. So… relax! Anyway, if you’re like Elder Hazel, you’ll have a natural talent for herblore. Go home and make some Miracle Grow potions. It will make your flowers grow faster! And other crops, too! Here’s the recipe! Bring them back to me when you’re done! 

Part 2: Brew miracle grow potions for the Acolyte witch.

* Acolyte: Oh, great! Let me have a whiff… ahhh, yes they all smell just right! Here, you keep them. They’re super useful for someone running a farm. Brew more any time you have the ingredients handy! Now go ahead, let the High Priestess know that you’ve finished your potion training. 

Part 3: Tell the High Priestess you learned the basics of potion making.

* High Priestess: Well done, Initiate! You have mastered the art of herbology very quickly. The Acolyte tells me she couldn’t have done better herself. To recognize your skills and achievement, you may cast away your Initiate designation.
* Tara: Yay! I really prefer to be called by my-
* High Priestess: You will be known as Novice from this point on.
* Tara: Right, I see. Right.
* High Priestess: Rest now, and find ways to use your new craft. There will be more work to do before long.
