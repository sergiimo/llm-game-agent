# A Banner Day for Otto

[Quest]
  title: A Banner Day for Otto
  type: To-Do Quests
  start: Otto Soft
  rewards: 200 coins

A Banner Day for Otto is a quest given by Otto Soft during the Fall before the election. It requires you to deliver 1x Silk to him so he can make a preemptive victory banner. The reward for completion is 200 coins.

This quest is optional and must be completed before the election to get the reward.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Mayor Otto: Ah, there’s one of my favorite citizens! I want to make a banner to hang up at our victory party in the mayor’s mansion: Otto Soft: Fairhaven Strong!
* Tara: Victory party? Feeling confident, I see!
* Mayor Otto: Why wouldn’t I be? Has anyone said they actually plan to vote for the new guy? They hardly know him! Haha, of course they don’t! Anyway, can you supply me with some fabric for the banner?
* Tara: Sure, no problem.
* Mayor Otto: Excellent! It’s going to be the best banner any mayor ever had! Huge! 

Quest completion:

* Mayor Otto: Do you have my fabric yet? Vanessa wants to get them painted so they have time to dry before election day!
* Tara: Yes, here you go.
* Mayor Otto: Fantastic! Thanks for doing your party to make my celebration a success! I wish I could extend an invitation, but it’s really just a few dozen of my closest donors, I mean friends!
* [Reward: 200 coins]
