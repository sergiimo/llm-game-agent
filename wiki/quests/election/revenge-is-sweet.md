# Revenge is Sweet

[Quest]
  title: Revenge is Sweet
  type: Story Quest
  item_needs: 1x Pumpkin Pie, 1x Pranking Pumpkin Pie
  location: Ravenwood Hollow
  start: Journeyman Damon
  end: Journeyman Damon
  rewards: Miracle Grow Potion Miracle Grow Potion 3x Miracle Grow

Revenge is Sweet is a quest given by the Journeyman during the Fall. It requires you to cook 1x Pumpkin Pie and then brew 1x Pranking Pumpkin Pie in order to try to make Otto Soft cluck like a chicken during the mayoral debate. The reward for completion is 3x Miracle Grow Potion.

This quest is optional. Completing it before the mayoral debate that takes place [x] days before the election will alter some of the dialogue during the debate and many of the villagers will comment about it afterwards, but it will not affect the outcome of the election.
