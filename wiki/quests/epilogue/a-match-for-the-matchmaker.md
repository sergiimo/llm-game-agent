A Match for the Matchmaker is a quest given by Thomas Lightfoot in the epilogue. It requires the player to deliver 8x Snowdrop to him so he can give them to Sophia when he asks her out on a date. The reward for completion is 300 coins.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: Hi Thomas! Get your Spring crops in yet?
* Thomas: Well, yes, but… you see, I filled up all my plots before I realized I um… wanted to plant some flowers.
* Tara: I didn’t know you liked flowers! Thought that was more Parker’s thing. Unless… they’re for someone else?
* Thomas: You’re really going to make me come out and say it, aren’t you?
* Tara: Yep. Sure am.
* Thomas: Fine. I want to give them to Sophia. Happy?
* Tara: Very.
* Thomas: I was going to invite her over to dinner, but thought I oughta give her some flowers first. Folks still do that, don’t they? Well here. I got some seeds from Violet. If you’ve got room in your garden or your greenhouse, I sure would appreciate it. [Gives 2x Snowdrop Seeds]

Thomas has asked you to grow snowdrops.

* Tara: Here you go, Thomas! One bunch of flowers for a gorgeous bouquet.
* Thomas: Thanks, Tara. Um… can I ask you something?
* Tara: Sure, what?
* Thomas: I, uh… don’t know what I should say. When I ask out Sophia. You’re friends with her. What do you think?
* Tara: Just tell her how you feel about her. Sophia’s kind of a no-nonsense person.
* Thomas: Yeah, that’s one of the things I like most about her. Huh. Okay. Well, I better get these in some water if they’re going to last long enough for me to give them to her.
* Tara: Good luck!
* [Reward: 300 coins]
