# You Must Be Plotting Something

[Quest]
  title: You Must Be Plotting Something
  type: Foraging Quest
  previous: Where Have You Bean?
  start: Gramma Hazel
  rewards: 2x Carrot Seeds; 3x Cauliflower Seeds

You Must Be Plotting Something is a quest given by Gramma. It requires you to build an additional 3x Garden Bed in the Garden, which requires a total of 30x Wood from chopping logs and trees and 3x Soil from the Compost Bin. The reward for completion is 3x Cauliflower Seeds.

Even though you don't get the quest until after the first town meeting, the dialog with Tomas (including his gift of Carrot Seeds), will trigger as soon as you build your third plot, regardless of other quest completions.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: Gramma, I’ve been thinking about my childhood memories of your garden. Just bursting with veggies in every color! I think we could bring it back the way it was.
* Gramma: Are you sure? That’s quite a bit of work.
* Tara: What would we need?
* Gramma: Well, for it to be enough to feed us and turn a profit, you’d need to build a bunch more garden beds. That means keeping a compost bin so you can get a whole lot of good soil to fill them. And you’ll need to chop some wood to make the beds.
* Tara: Okay, I’m on it!
After making the first plot:

* Thomas: Don’t pack the soil too tight, now. You’ll choke the seedlings.
* Tara: Oh! Hello, Thomas. I didn’t hear you.
* Thomas: That’s because I don’t wear big, clompy boots like you city folk. Anyway… not a bad effort for your first plot.
* Tara: Wow, you give compliments?
* Thomas: Don’t make me regret bringing these for you. [Gives 2x Carrot Seeds.]
* Tara: Thank you! But what’s the occasion?
* Thomas: Hazel’s a good woman. Strong. But you don’t need me to tell you she’s struggling. So when I saw you out helping with the garden beds… well, she’s not the only one who appreciates it.
* Tara: I’ll do my best with these seeds. 

Quest completion:

* Tara: Gramma, the garden beds are ready.
* Gramma: Oh, I can’t thank you enough for helping me like this. I found some seeds in the bottom of a drawer you might like to plant. Otherwise, there’s more variety down at Lina’s store. [Gives 3x Cauliflower Seeds]
