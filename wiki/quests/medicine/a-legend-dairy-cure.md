A Legend-dairy Cure is a quest given by Marty Emerson. It requires you to speak to Aryel to learn the recipe for the Cure Animal Sickness Potion, then to brew a dose of the potion for Marty's cow. The reward for completion is 2x Milk and 250 coins.

## Dialogue
* Marty: Mornin’, Tara. Any chance your grandma may have passed down any folk remedies for ailing cattle?
* Tara: No, why? Is one of your cows sick?
* Marty: Well, that’s the thing, I just don’t know! She’s not producing milk, but Dr. Syed checked and said she doesn’t seem to have an infection. I may have forgotten to feed her once… or twice.
* Tara: The animals get sick if you forget to feed them?
* Marty: Apparently! And I can’t get a vet to come in from off-island for at least another week or so. I just wish someone in Fairhaven really knew animals. Let me know if you think of anything.
* [Marty leaves]
* Tara: I may know someone…

Speak to Aryel to see if he can help Marty’s sick cow.

* Tara: Aryel, do you know of any remedies to cure sick animals?
* Aryel: I might. Who’s it for, a pequeno woodland creature?
* Tara: No, a farm animal.
* Aryel: Ah. Perhaps freedom will cure what ails her. But the magnificent bovine does not deserve to suffer due to her owner’s neglect. Gather some coconuts from the beach. Make this potion. That should set her right. [Gives Cure Animal Sickness Potion recipe.] Oh, and you’ll need this. I’ll give it to you this one time, but in the future you can buy more from me. [Gives 1x Aryel’s Hair.] Although, I do hope you tend your animals well enough that you won’t need it.

Make the cure animal sickness potion.

* Tara: Try giving this to your cow, Marty.
* Marty: Wow! What’s in it?
* Tara: Um… secret Wylde family recipe.
* Marty: Well thanks! I’m going to try this right now!
* [Wait and see if the potion helped Marty’s cow.]
* Marty: That medicine you gave me worked a miracle! Moona Lisa is back in action! Just look at this fresh milk! Oh, and here, you should have this for your trouble, too!
* Tara: Happy to help!
* [Reward: 250 coins, 2x milk]
