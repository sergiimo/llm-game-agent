# Light My Candle

Light My Candle is a romance quest given by Damon Mthembu-Haas after you gain six full hearts with him. It requires you to craft 2x Beeswax Candle for him. After delivering the candles, he invites you to meet him at the bar the next morning, where he professes his love and gives you his mother's necklace. 

This quest cannot be triggered until the quest An Uplifting Brew has been completed. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Damon: Kitten, do you happen to have any spare candles? I’m working on something.
* Tara: That’s not vague at all. But okay, let me see what I can do.

Damon has asked you to bring him some candles for a surprise he’s working on.

* Tara: Here are the candles you wanted!
* Damon: Thanks! Come by the bar in the morning, okay?

Wait until tomorrow morning, then meet Damon at the bar.

* Damon: So…. What do you think?
* Tara: Damon, this is so pretty! And also a bit of a fire hazard.
* Damon: Well, I’m not going to leave the bar like this all the time. This is just for you.
* Tara: For me? Why?
* Damon: ‘Cause – how do I say this without sounding corny? – you make me feel like I have all these candles in my chest. When my parents moved home, I thought about going, too. But… I didn’t. I wasn’t sure why. Now you’re here. And I think I made the right call, to stay. There’s this Zulu idea called “ubuntu.” My mom talks about it a lot. It means “I am because we are.” Ever since she and my dad left, I’ve been thinking about the ways… y’know, ways that we’re all connected.
* Tara: I didn’t realize you thought about this kind of stuff…
* Damon: Bartenders-slash-witches-slash-class-clowns have deep thoughts all the time, kitten! And here’s another one: I think you and I make each other into complete people. My mom told me to give this necklace to someone special. And that’s you. I… okay, here it comes… I love you. [Gives Kaya’s Necklace.]
* Tara: I love you, too. You’re my favorite bartender-slash-witch-slash-class-clown.
* Damon: That’s a relief. There are so many in Fairhaven.
* Tara: But you’re the cutest!
