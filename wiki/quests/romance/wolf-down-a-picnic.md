# Wolf Down a Picnic

Wolf Down a Picnic is a romance quest given by Westley Vuk after you gain five full hearts with him. You receive a letter from him inviting you to a picnic, and he asks for 1x Bread. Bringing the bread to Westley will cause the time to advance to 7pm for the picnic. Westley confesses that he cares for you, and you have the option to return his feelings or ask to just be friends. 

## Dialogue
Letter from Westley:

* Hey, Beautiful!
* I’ve been thinking about it and I would like to take you up on your dinner offer. Shall we have an evening picnic in the hollow?
* I’ll make an amazing charcuterie platter, perhaps you could bring some bread? Just come by my shop when you’re ready for our picnic!
* -- Westley

Buy or make some bread for Westley’s picnic.
