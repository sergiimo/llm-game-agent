# Some Fairy Good Gifts

Some Fairy Good Gifts is a quest given by the Farseer at the beginning of Summer. It requires you to deliver 1x Milk and 1x Honey to him to leave as an offering for the fae folk. The reward for completion is 240 coins.

The Farseer advises you to buy a cow from Marty to get milk, but you can also buy it from the General Store for 100 coins. 

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Farseer: Heard you’ve been asked to help with the Summer Festival.
* Tara: Yes, Sophia asked me to fetch wood for the bonfire.
* Farseer: Balefire, you mean. Witches have been celebrating the solstice with balefires for millennia. Non-witch folk came along, kept the custom, changed the name. So it goes. They only remember part of the legend.
* Tara: How does the coven celebrate the festival?
* Farseer: Oh we join in with the rest of the town at the balefire. Our own traditions we keep to ourselves. Say, have you got any milk and honey?
* Tara: What for? Oooh, is it another potion?
* Farseer: Ya gotta learn your summer rites, Novice. We leave offerings for the fae folk every summer. It’s tradition.
* Tara: Okay well, honey I can handle. But milk… I don’t have a cow.
* Farseer: The island will provide.
* Tara: Are you telling me to just… search for a cow?
* Farseer: Course not. Ya might be able to strike a deal with the rancher who just moved in. 

Part 1: The Farseer needs milk and honey to give to the fae folk as part of the Summer Festival.

* Tara: Here’s your milk and honey, Farseer.
* Farseer: That’ll do. I’ll leave some out for the fae folk.
* Tara: What will you do with the rest?
* Farseer: Put it in my tea, of course. Fool question. 

Part 2: Speak to the Farseer after the Summer Festival to see if the fae folk liked your offering.

* Tara: Did your um… friends like the milk and honey?
* Farseer: Didn’t ask. Can’t be bothering them for compliments. Oh, here’s something for yer troubles.
* [Reward: 240 coins]
