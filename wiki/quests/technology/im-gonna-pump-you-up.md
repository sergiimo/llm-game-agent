# I’m Gonna Pump You Up

I’m Gonna Pump You Up is a quest given by Zephyr Grimspark in the Winter after the ferry has broken down. It requires the player to deliver 20x Copper Ingot to her so she can fix the pump on the ferry. The reward for completion is 500 coins.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Zephyr: Hiya, Tara! Wanna see these build plans?
* Tara: Always. What’s your latest invention?
* Zephyr: Oh, I didn’t invent this! It’s what humans would call and Archimedes’ Screw. Very old.
* Tara: Okay, regardless of who invented it… what does it, um, do?
* Zephyr: It pumps water from one place to another. So I figure I can replace the ferry’s bilge pump with one o’ these! That way, the ferry won’t take on more water while I’m repairing it. I mean… while Thomas, Parker, ‘n me are repairing it. All together.
* Tara: That’s genius! What are you going to make the Archimedes’ Screw out of?
* Zephyr: Bronze is best. It holds up real well in salt water. I’ve got loads of tin lying around to make a bronze alloy, but no copper. Could ya fetch me some?
* Tara: Sure, no problem.
* Zephyr: Woohoo! Thanks. I can’t wait to get started! 

Quest completion:

* Zephyr: Didja get your hands on any copper?
* Tara: I sure did. Have a look!
* Zephyr: This is just what I need. I’ll melt the copper down and combine it with the tin straightaway! I use a Grimspark family incantation to make the alloy extra-strong. It’s the niftiest thing! I’d show it to ya, but you’re not a Grimspark, so… take this instead.
* Tara: Thanks, Zephyr.
* Zephyr: Don’t mention it, my friend.
* [Reward: 500 coins]
