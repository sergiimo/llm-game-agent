# Try Giving this a Whirl

Try Giving this a Whirl is a quest given by Zephyr Grimspark the first time you water your crops in the Fall. It requires you to deliver 15x Iron Ingot, 15x Copper Ingot, 10x Silver Ingot to her. The reward for completion is a farming whirligig that, when powered by a gem, will automatically water your crops for you. It will also water crops in the greenhouse, once you unlock that.
