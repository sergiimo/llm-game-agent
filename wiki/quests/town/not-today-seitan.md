Not Today, Seitan is a quest given by Kim Izumi. It requires you to deliver 8x Wheat to them so they can make a meat substitute. The reward for completion is 600 coins. It is given after you unlock wheat seeds in Dahl General Store.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Kim: Hey. Do you like seitan?
* Tara: Um, excuse me?
* Kim: Seitan. Like, the meat substitute?
* Tara: Oh, that stuff. It does taste pretty good, now that you mention it. Why? Have you suddenly gone vegan?
* Kim: Hah. No. But I was thinking… maybe Thomas will stop giving me the stink-eye if I sell some chickenless nuggets, too.
* Tara: Do you care what he thinks?
* Kim: Psh. No way! I just don’t want to lose sleep over petty drama. The real problem here is that I don’t have any wheat gluten. If you and Hazel are growing wheat this Spring, could I have some?
* Tara: For… money?
* Kim: Haven’t you heard of paying in exposure? Joking, joking. I’ll pay you! Course. If you need wheat seeds, I think Lina sells them. 

Quest completion:

* Tara: I have the wheat you wanted! Better get that meatless grinder going. Or… whatever you use to make seitan.
* Kim: Aww yes. Give me a day to make it, then drop by my store for some fresh seitan. And in the meantime – fair wages for fair work.
* [Reward: 600 coins]
