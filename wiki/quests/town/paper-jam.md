# Paper Jam

[Quest]
  type: Town Quest
  start: Cameron
  end: Cameron or Francis
  item_needs: 5x Paper
  location: In front of The Jewelry Box

  title: Paper Jam

Paper Jam is a quest given by Cameron Conner. It requires you to give 5x Paper to either him or Francis Calderón, who overheard the request. The reward for completion is 50 coins and a relationship boost with whichever person you deliver the paper to.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Cameron: Hey there! Are you ready to help your neighbors reach The Pinnacle?
* Tara: Um… the Pinnacle of what?
* Cameron: Haha, you’re funny! Everyone knows about the Pinnacle! The Climb to the Pinnacle is the very foundation of the League of the Conscious Mind! This community is ready for some new ideas! But I’m running out of pamphlets. If you could find me some paper, I’ll make sure you are rewarded!
* Tara: Monetarily or spiritually?
* Cameron: Both!
* [Cameron leaves, Francis approaches.]
* Francis: So you’ve decided to climb the Pinnacle too…?
* Tara: What? No. Cameron just asked for some paper.
* Francis: I heard. For printing pamphlets. Which will help spread his ideas. Don’t get me wrong, Cameron’s a nice guy. But I don’t trust the League. And it’s strange to see Angus get caught up in this…
* Tara: I’m sorry. I wasn’t thinking about it like that.
* Francis: I figured. It’s easier to act like everyone on the island has good intentions, right? Hey – I’ll pay you for that paper, too. I could always use more wrapping paper for the store.
* Tara: I did promise Cameron…
* Francis: I know, I know. Just think about it. 

If the paper is given to Francis:

* Francis: Tell me this is about the paper. Are you giving it to me instead of Cameron?
* [Choice: Yes]
* Tara: Guess who’s the lucky winner of the passive-aggressive paper wars?
* Francis: Thank you, Tara. I know this whole thing might seem petty.
* Tara: I mean… a little.
* Francis: I just don’t want people to get in over their heads, you know? Here’s something for your trouble.
* [Reward: 50 coins]

If the paper is given to Cameron:

* Cameron: Do you have the paper yet?
* [Choice: yes]
* Tara: Here’s your paper.
* Cameron: Excellent! We’ll have so many more pamphlets printed just in time for our new seminar offerings! I know you had to cut down quite a few trees to make this for me.
* Tara: You know what, I would really rather not think about that.
* Cameron: Those trees gave their lives to help free this town from the Valley of Disillusionment! You’ll see.
* [Reward: 50 coins]
