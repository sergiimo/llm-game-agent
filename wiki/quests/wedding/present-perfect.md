# Present Perfect

Present Perfect is a quest given byParker Johnson during the Fall before Francis and Angus' wedding. It requires you to deliver 1x Silk and 3x Turtlehead Flower so he can make them a wedding present. The reward for completion is 250 coins.

Although Parker says Lina sells Turtlehead Flower Seeds in her store, they can only be bought from Violet's Violets.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: You look like you’re in a good mood.
* Parker: I am! Angus is getting married soon, and he’s one of my best buddies. I’m so happy for him and Francis!
* Tara: Aww, that’s sweet! I love a wholesome bromance.
* Parker: I want to get Angus the best wedding present. Not the most expensive, but the most meaningful, you know? Here’s my plan: take a photo of them, blow it up, and print it on silk. Then I’ll frame it with dried turtlehead flowers. Francis likes to give Angus big bouquets of turtleheads! Hey, what if I pay you for some turtleheads and silk? Lina sells turtlehead seeds in the store.
* Tara: How could I turn down such a cute request?
* Parker: I knew you’d understand. Thanks, Tara. 

Quest completion:

* Parker: The wedding’s not far off. Think you’ll be able to get me some silk and turtleheads?
* Tara: Way ahead of you.
* Parker: This is exactly what I needed. Thanks so much! Lina says I like craft projects more than a sorority girl, but what can I say? I enjoy making things for people I care about.
* Tara: Oh, Parker. Never change.
* Parker: Thanks again for helping me out, I couldn’t do it without you. Here.
* [Reward: 250 coins]
