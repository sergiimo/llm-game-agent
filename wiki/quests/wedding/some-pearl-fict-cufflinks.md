# Some Pearl-fict Cufflinks

Some Pearl-fict Cufflinks is a quest given by Francis Calderón during the Fall before his wedding. It requires you to deliver 1x Pearl to him. The reward for completion is 115 coins.

## Dialogue
Quest intro:

* Tara: Hiya Francis. Whatcha working on?
* Francis: Can you keep a secret? I’m making Angus a pair of pearl cufflinks for our wedding! Only problem is my pearl source is on vacation and I’ve only found one on the beach so far! If you could help me find another one, I would be so grateful!
* Tara: Of course I’ll help! I’ll keep my eye out!
* Francis: Thanks! I really want to make him something special. 

Quest completion:

* Francis: Any luck finding a pearl yet?
* Tara: Here you go!
* Francis: Wow! What a beauty! Thanks so much, this is a perfect match for my other one! Oh, I hope he likes them!
* [Reward: 115 coins]
