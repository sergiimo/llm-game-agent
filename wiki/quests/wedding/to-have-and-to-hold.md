# To Have And To Hold

To Have And To Hold is a quest given by Francis Calderón and Angus Seton by letter during the Fall announcing their wedding. 

The wedding does not take place until after the quests Musician in Treble and Cake It Or Break It are complete. The earliest it can take place is four days before the election, the day after the mayoral debate. 

Your partner will accompany you to the wedding. If you are not dating anyone, then Violet Miranda will be your friend date. 

## Letter contents
Dear Tara Wylde,

Francis Calderon and Angus Seton invite you to celebrate the joy of their union.

Ceremony in the Fairhaven woods.

Cake reception to follow.

Date and Time to be announced (there’s so much to do!)

Angus and Francis

## Dialogue
If you are single:

* Violet: Thanks for being my friend date to Angus and Francis's wedding! Now Sebastian won't rag on me for being single. Or maybe that's just wishful thinking. He is my little sib. 
* Tara: Oh no, I didn't think about what to wear! Should I change? 
* Violet: The ceremony is in the woods, so I think they're keeping it pretty casual. Besides, you could wear a potato sack and still look good. 
* Tara: Hey, you too! Let's go. 

If you are married to Damon:

* Damon: I rolled out of bed waaaay earlier than usual so we could make it to Francis and Angus’s wedding. You ready?
* Tara: Yes! I can’t wait! But what should I wear?
* Damon: The ceremony is in the woods, so I think they’re keeping it pretty casual. Works for me! Hey, come on, kitten, you gotta know you look gorgeous no matter what you wear.
* Tara: So do you. Come on, let’s go! 

At the wedding:

* Tara: Hi Cameron! It’s such a lovely day for a wedding, no?
* Cameron: Well, Angus thought it would be lovelier in our Consciousness Center, but alas. Francis was quite set on a civil ceremony, it seems.
* Mayor Otto: I am honored that Angus and Francis have chosen me, their mayor, to preside over their special day. Angus do you take Francis to be your lawfully married, by me, husband?
* Angus: I do!
* Mayor Otto: And Francis, do you take Angus to be your husband?
* Francis: I do!
* Mayor Otto: I now pronounce you husband and husband!
* Mayor Otto: And I would like to take this opportunity to remind everyone to vote! (If before election)
