# Salmon Has to Do It

Salmon Has to Do It is a quest given by Kim Izumi at the beginning of Winter. It requires the player to cook 1x Roast Salmon as your household's contribution to the Winter Feast. The meal must be in your inventory the morning of the Feast to complete the quest.
